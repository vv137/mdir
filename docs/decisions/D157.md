# D157

Recorded 2026-10-03.

**Two destinations of one loop over pairs that a loop over particles adds merge into one when the pair gives them by the same contract of exchange, as `md-exec-accumulate-destinations` merged those of a loop over tuples (D150).** A term over the pairs of a topology (D137) fuses into the loop of the nonbonded pairs, but its forces stayed a field of their own: the loop over groups added two contributions to each particle by atomics, and the integration read one field more. The merged loop yields the sum of the two contributions of a pair and adds it once. On JAC (RTX 3090, mixed precision) with a tenth of the Lennard-Jones again as a term over all pairs, the step falls from 0.248 to 0.211 ms, against 0.210 without the term and OpenMM 8.6.1's 0.234 with the same term as a `CustomNonbondedForce` (~/notes/mdir/openmm-speed); the energy of the term is a tenth of the Lennard-Jones, 912.352508 against 9123.525006 kcal/mol.
