# D61

Recorded 2026-09-29.

**The members of a relation of a topology are the places of the particles in their fields**, and are renumbered where the particles are put in a new order, so a loop needs no table from numbers to places. The incidence structure has rows as wide as the particle with the most tuples needs, found when it is built. A sum over tuples takes the contribution of a tuple from its member at place 0, with no weight.
