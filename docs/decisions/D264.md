# D264

Recorded 2026-10-16.

**The barostat and the log take the pressure of the groups that the barostat scales, with the kinetic energy of their centers at the half steps [[Jung2020]](../references.md#jung2020): on axis $a$, $P_a = (2K_{\text{half},g,a} + W_{g,aa} + C/3V)/V$, with $W_g = \sum_g\mathbf R_g\otimes\mathbf F_g$ the virial of the groups and $K_{\text{half},g,a} = \sum_g\tfrac{1}{2M_g}P_{g,a}^2 + \tfrac{\Delta t^2}{8}\sum_gF_{g,a}^2/M_g$.** Issue #268, amends D45, D72, and D203. The groups are those that the constraints keep rigid, the waters of SETTLE and the clusters of SHAKE (a heavy atom with its hydrogens), and every other particle is a group of one: the groups that D72 scales by their centers and whose virial D92 and D116 count. The forces of the constraints sum to zero within a group, so neither they nor a measured half step enter: the relation between the momenta of a center at the step and at the half steps is that of the forces alone, under every integrator, Langevin dynamics included. Without constraints this is the pressure of D203, unchanged. Isotropic, semi-isotropic, and anisotropic coupling take it alike, and so do the first-order, exact, and Trotter counts of the work, which keep their meaning. The column `virial` of the log is the trace of $W_g$, the virial of its pressure.
