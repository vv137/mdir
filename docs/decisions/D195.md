# D195

Recorded 2026-10-05.

**M2 is split into M2a, the Python API of D187, and M2b, differentiable simulation; M2b adapts PyTorch first and JAX second.** Amends D169, set by the maintainer on 2026-10-05. M2b fits parameters of a potential to ensemble averages by reweighting stored frames [[ThalerZavadlav2021]](../references.md#thalerzavadlav2021): the framework computes the weights and the loss, and its automatic differentiation of the softmax gives the covariance form of the gradient, so MDIR supplies a sampler (not differentiated) and an evaluator of the energy, the virial, and observables at stored frames with a vector-Jacobian product in the parameters (D161), over parameters held in tunable buffers. One framework-neutral contract over DLPack buffers; the PyTorch adapter comes first, aligned with the metatomic models of M3, then JAX. Free energies, the pressure, and relative entropy are later targets of the same evaluator. M2b starts after M2a and does not change M2a's scope or gates; "M2" in [python.md](../python.md) and D187–D193 means M2a. Roadmap Section 6.1.
