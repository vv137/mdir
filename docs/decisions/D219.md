# D219

Recorded 2026-10-07.

**A minimization stops at the first row of the energies whose largest force, without its parts along the constraints, is below a tolerance: `[minimize] force_tolerance` (kcal/mol/Å) and `Simulation.minimize(steps=None, tolerance=None)` (kJ/mol/nm).** Issue #106. Without it a minimization takes its steps as before, from a program whose text is unchanged. The measure is $\max_i\lVert m_i\mathbf g_i\rVert$, the `MAX_FORCE` of the log, with a strict test; it bounds the root mean square and does not shrink with the size of the system, as a test on the root mean square (OpenMM 8.6's `tolerance`) or the norm does; GROMACS's `emtol` is on the largest force too. `mdir run` checks each row of its log, step 0 included, on the host, where it reads the forces for the row anyway, so the check takes no synchronization of its own: `mdrtCheckMinimization` keeps in a buffer of the host whether a row was below the tolerance and its step, and every loop of steps, rows, and frames after that row has a trip count of zero; the checkpoint is written at that step, a frame at it if it is a frame step, and the log ends with `converged at step S` or `not converged in N steps`. A Python simulation checks where the call begins (a part of no step writes the row of a new activation), every `Schedule.energy_period` steps, by ending its parts there, and where the call ends; its program is that without a tolerance, and since parts continue each other to the bit (D215) it stops where `mdir run` stops. `minimize()` still returns the steps taken; `state().minimization["converged"]` is `True`, `False`, or `None` without a tolerance. *Evidence.* On the dipeptide in water with PME, SHAKE, SETTLE, and a restraint, rows every 10 steps, a tolerance of 15 kcal/mol/Å: the criterion applied in NumPy to the rows of a run without the key gives step 100 in double and mixed precision on the CPU and a GPU; `mdir run` with the key stops there with the same rows, and writes the energy file, trajectory, and checkpoint of a 100-step run without the key byte for byte; the simulation stops at step 100 with the row of `mdir run` to every printed digit. Without the key the module of `mdir run` is textually that of main, and its outputs on the four targets and precisions are byte for byte those of main. See [python-minimize.md](../python-minimize.md#convergence) and Section 6.7 of the paper.
