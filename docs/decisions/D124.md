# D124

Recorded 2026-10-02.

**Under a barostat the reference of a restraint is a center that follows the cell plus an offset that stays: $\mathbf x^\text{ref} = \mathbf s \odot \mathbf c + \mathbf o$, $\mathbf s$ the edges of the cell over those of the file.** With `reference_scaling = "CENTER"`, the default, $\mathbf c$ is the mean of the references of every particle so restrained and $\mathbf o = \mathbf r - \mathbf c$, so that a restrained solute keeps its shape; with `"ALL"`, $\mathbf c = \mathbf r$ and $\mathbf o = 0$, as D74 had it for every reference, which restraints spread through the cell need (the options `com` and `all` of `refcoord-scaling` in the GROMACS manual [[GromacsManual2025]](../references.md#gromacsmanual2025)). The virial is the derivative of the energy when the positions and the centers scale and the offsets do not, $\mathrm{diag}\,\sum -2k\,\mathbf d \odot (\mathbf d + \mathbf o)$, a second sum over the particles; the fields hold $\mathbf o$ and $\mathbf c$. Scaled about the origin, the references of ubiquitin shrank with a cell that had become 0.926 of the file's, the restraints pushed the protein outward, and the barostat held the cell 2.3% larger than without them (200,900 against 196,300 Å³); with the center, 196,262 ± 580 against 196,293 ± 658 Å³. The virial was checked by the conserved energy of the Trotter count on the peptide of D65 compressed by 16%: −10.4 kcal/mol with the restraints, −8.8 without, −20.2 without $\mathbf o$. On the POPC bilayer, whose phosphorus atoms are restrained, `"CENTER"` holds the area of the file, and its tutorial takes `"ALL"` ([dynamics.md](../dynamics.md), Section 5).
