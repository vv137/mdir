# D210

Recorded 2026-10-06.

**Under a plain cutoff the free-energy file and the observables file differentiate the potential that the forces sample, shifted to 0 at the cutoff; with a topology the correction for the dispersion is allowed under `POTENTIAL_SHIFT` and adds the estimate of what the shift takes within the cutoff.** A pair term cut at $r_c$ without a shift has forces that are $-\nabla U_\text{shift}$, $U_\text{shift} = \sum_{r<r_c}(u(r) - u(r_c))$, and the impulse of the step at $r_c$ is never applied, so a thermostatted run samples $e^{-U_\text{shift}/k_BT}$ and $\partial F/\partial\theta = \langle\partial U_\text{shift}/\partial\theta\rangle$; the cut $U$ differs by $N_\text{in}u(r_c;\theta)$, which fluctuates. `@alchemical` (the λ-state energies and `dH/dλ`) and `@observe<k>` (the columns of `observe`) are therefore emitted with the topology's Lennard-Jones (each pair less $u_\text{LJ}(r_c)$ of its type pair), `[[energy.pair]]` terms (`truncation(shift)`), a Coulomb cutoff ($fq_iq_j(1/r - 1/r_c)$), and the direct sum of PME ($fq_iq_j[\mathrm{erfc}(\beta r)/r - \mathrm{erfc}(\beta r_c)/r_c]$) shifted, by the code of `POTENTIAL_SHIFT` and `coulomb_modifier = "POTENTIAL_SHIFT"`; the forces, trajectories, log, energy file, virial, and pressure are unchanged, and `NONE` with `[free_energy]` or `observe` is not refused. A decoupled soft-core pair is shifted by $(1-\lambda)u_\text{LJ}(r_c)$, as under `POTENTIAL_SHIFT` and in GROMACS's free-energy kernels, which leaves a step of about $\alpha_\text{sc}\lambda(\sigma/r_c)^6$ of $u(r_c)$ (maintainer decision). With a topology, `dispersion_correction = "ENERGY_PRESSURE"` now takes `POTENTIAL_SHIFT` (switches stay refused) and adds $E_\text{sh} = \nu(\tfrac{4\pi r_c^3}{3V} - \tfrac1N)\sum_{\{i,j\}}u_{ij}(r_c)$, with no virial: $\rho\tfrac{4\pi}{3}r_c^3 - 1$ neighbors a particle, the particle itself out by the compressibility sum rule [[HansenMcDonald2013]](../references.md#hansenmcdonald2013), as GROMACS counts them (maintainer decision); the topology takes the $r^{-6}$ part, $E_\text{disp}(1 - V/(N\tfrac{4\pi}{3}r_c^3))$, a pair term its whole $u(r_c)$. It enters the log's energy under the shift, and the free-energy and observables files under either modifier, per λ state and differentiated. Validation: on the same trajectory the files under `NONE` and `POTENTIAL_SHIFT` are byte-equal, with a Coulomb cutoff and with PME, on the CPU and on a GPU in double and mixed precision (`shifted-derivatives.test`, `-gpu`); the Coulomb cutoff's $\partial U/\partial\lambda_\text{C}$ equals a Python sum of $-fq_iq_j(1/r - 1/r_c)$ to $1.5\times10^{-7}$ kcal/mol (the cut sum is 9.536 against 0.681); `check_free_energy.py` takes the shifted OpenMM references (RF_SHIFT for the Lennard-Jones, and $S = \sum fq_iq_j\mathrm{erfc}(\beta r_c)/r_c = 6.0\times10^{-5}$ kcal/mol for PME).
