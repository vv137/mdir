# D285

Recorded 2026-10-18.

**The tiles of PME (D282) and the tables of the tuple kernel (D283) are two strategies of one privatized scatter-accumulate: the attribute `#md_exec.scatter` states the strategy of an op, the pass `md-exec-choose-scatter` chooses it, and the pass and the lowerings check each precondition with the same functions (`Scatter.h`).** Step 1 of issue #416 (#415; [device-primitives.md](../device-primitives.md), Section 2; white paper, Section 8.5). *The attribute*: `atomic`; `tile(E)` on `md_exec.reciprocal`, replacing `tiles(E)`; `hash(S, P, L)` on `md_exec.tuple_for`, its slots, the slots a search tries before a direct atomic, and the fewest tuples of a launch that take the table, which the lowering derived before and the IR now states; `sorted` reserved for #417 and refused. The verifiers refuse `tile` on a loop over tuples (no bounded footprint) and `hash` on a reciprocal sum (the template has no table). *Not one op*: the tile is a kernel of the runtime template `PMEGPU.mlir`, the table is generated by `MDExecToGPU.cpp`, so one op that both lower through would have meant generating the spreading; the attribute, the pass, and the preconditions are shared, not code, and the two sites keep their own emitters of the addition in block memory (`atomicrmw fadd` at the scope of the block, `red.shared.add`). A scatter op is the form for the sites of step 3. *The preconditions*: the reordering (floating-point sums outside the deterministic mode only; fixed point is step 2); for `tile`, an edge of the template (4, 8, 16) and every edge of the grid at least $E + P - 1$ points, so that a tile wraps once at most; the 48 KiB of block memory of a tile with the particles of a round, or of a table; for `hash`, destinations of f32 or f64 that have a device atomic to fall back on. The pass refuses where one fails, and with `explain` states the reason in a remark; the lowering fails with the reason, except for the joint table of a run of loops in one kernel, formed after the pass, which keeps the atomics where it does not fit, as before. *The choice* is that of D282 and D283: tiles for a grid in f32 of at least 500,000 points, the table from 100,000 tuples on the host. *Switches*: `tile=auto\|on\|off`, `tile-edge`, `tile-min-points`, `hash=auto\|on\|off`, `hash-least`, `hash-probes`, `block-size`, `deterministic`, `explain`; `MDIR_PME_SPREAD` and `MDIR_TUPLE_SUMS` keep their values and set them. `md-exec-choose-spreading` and the options `tuple-block-sums` and `tuple-block-least` of `convert-md-exec-to-gpu` are removed. *No change of behavior*: the module after `convert-md-exec-to-gpu` is that of `main` on JAC NVE and NPT, Factor IX NVE, Cellulose NVE, and STMV NPT 4 fs but for the line of the pass's options, and the PTX of JAC NVE and NPT (245 and 326 kernels, from the compile cache on an RTX 4090) is identical. *Measured* (the Amber suite with a dual list of groups, skins 3 and 0.6 Å, `main` and this decision alternated system by system in one job on ada-001, RTX 4090, three rounds, the median ns/day ratio): JAC NVE 0.997, NVE 4 fs 1.000, NPT 0.997, NPT 4 fs 1.002, Factor IX NVE 1.001, NPT 1.001, Cellulose NVE 0.999, NPT 1.000, STMV NPT 4 fs 1.001; JAC NVE and NPT again in five rounds on ada-003, 1.001 and 0.999, as the identical kernels imply.
