# D203

Recorded 2026-10-06.

**The log reports the optimal temperature of Jung, Kobayashi, and Sugita (2019) [[Jung2019]](../references.md#jung2019), $N_fk_BT = \tfrac43K_\text{half} + \tfrac23K$, and its pressure takes $K_\text{half}$, with and without constraints; with constraints the steps of the rows of the log measure $K_\text{half}$ from the velocities of the half steps after the constraints of the positions. The thermostats and the barostats keep the kinetic energy $K$ of the velocities they scale.** $K_\text{half}$ is the mean of the kinetic energies half a step before and after the step: without constraints $K + \tfrac{\Delta t^2}{8}\sum_i\lVert\mathbf F_i\rVert^2/m_i$ exactly; with them the steps of energy take, after their second half kick, the first half of the next step as that step takes it (a kick, a drift, and the constraints of the positions) and return $K_\text{half}$ from the chord velocities $(\mathbf x_{n+1} - \mathbf x_n)/\Delta t$ of the two half steps; nothing of it enters the state, and leapfrog and velocity Verlet give the same values (`half-step-chords.test` checks it against the chords of the trajectory). The row of step 0 with constraints, and Langevin dynamics with constraints, take $K$. $K_\text{opt}$ is accurate as an equilibrium mean, but it is not a homogeneous quadratic function of the momenta, so velocity rescaling, the Nosé–Hoover chain, and the barostats do not take it: they would not then sample the intended ensemble. At a finite step they sample the physical distribution up to the integrator's error; a thermostat consistent with the integrator's shadow Hamiltonian is #118. With rigid waters the run ends with the optimal temperatures of the solvent (the waters of SETTLE, 6 degrees of freedom each) and of the solute (the rest), with those of the velocities of the steps, as means over the rows with errors from ten blocks: the check of the hot-solvent/cold-solute problem [[Lingenheil2008]](../references.md#lingenheil2008). Control files and checkpoints are unchanged. *Amended 2026-10-16 by D263, D264, and D265:* the thermostats control $K_\text{opt}$, which the steps that close a period measure as the rows do; the pressure is that of the groups, from the forces, at step 0 as well; and Langevin dynamics with constraints measures its half steps.
