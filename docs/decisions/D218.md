# D218

Recorded 2026-10-07.

**The evaluation at the start of a run is a segment of its own in every program: the steps begin with neighbor structures of their own, built at step 1, in `mdir run` with and without checkpoints and in a Python simulation.** Issue #121; the maintainer's decision on PR #173 (rule E). Before, `md-exec-reuse-neighbors` carried the structure of the evaluation at the start into the steps of a Python simulation and of `mdir run` without checkpoints, while `mdir run` with checkpoints started it empty in its first segment (R1, `mdrt.segment`) and built again at step 1. The test of displacement compares with the positions of the last build, so the programs rebuilt at other steps and summed the forces of one configuration in another order: on the dipeptide in water, deterministic, CPU, with positions equal at step 20, forces differed by 1.5e-3 kJ/mol/nm in mixed precision with cutoff electrostatics and 2.2e-10 in double, and by 1.2e-9 at step 40 with PME; `mdir run` itself built 3 times in 50 steps without a checkpoint and 4 times with one. The start branch of the program of segments (D211) is now a `mdrt.segment` loop, and the outermost loop of the steps of `mdir run` without a loop over checkpoint intervals carries `mdrt.fresh`, whose structures the pass does not chain to one built before it in the block. Rejected: continuing the structure of the start into the first segment of checkpoints (rule A), which saves the build but needs a first iteration that neither sorts the particles nor starts empty. Cost: one more build per run, and a storage for the structure of the start besides that of the steps, as every run with checkpoints already had. The trajectory of `mdir run` without checkpoints changes at the rounding of its sums, and `rebuild_interval` (D88) counts from step 1. Minimization is unchanged. *Evidence.* Deterministic mode, dipeptide with SHAKE and SETTLE, NVE: a simulation of N = 1, 19, 20, 21, 50 steps in one part, and 3 parts of 20 and 5 of 7 steps, equal `mdir run` with a checkpoint at the end bit for bit in positions, velocities, and forces, with cutoff electrostatics and PME, in mixed and double precision, on the CPU (`python-deterministic-run-*.test`) and on a GPU; `mdir run` gives the same rows and builds with and without the checkpoint; `run(0, energy=True)` followed by steps equals the steps at once. `mdir run` with checkpoints before the end still differs at the rounding, since each later segment sorts and builds anew (#125).
