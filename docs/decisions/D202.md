# D202

Recorded 2026-10-06.

**A Python simulation of a program whose integrator minimizes runs the minimizer of `mdir run`, `Simulation.minimize(steps=None)`, in parts as `run(n)` runs steps.** Issue #91. `Simulation(program)` accepts a minimization (it raised `UnsupportedError`); `minimize(steps)` takes that many more steps, the program's `Schedule.steps` by default (`[minimize] steps`), any nonnegative count, and returns the steps taken; `run(n)` on a minimization and `minimize()` on dynamics raise `InputError`. The program is that of `mdir run` (`emitMinimization`, D202 adds its form of segments): steepest descent whose first step moves no particle farther than `minimize_step`, accepted steps 1.2 times longer (at most 0.1 nm) and rejected ones 0.2 times as long, constraints and virtual sites, restraints; like `[minimize]`, a fixed number of steps and no convergence criterion (a criterion is a question to the maintainer on PR #104). A segment of a minimization takes its steps and the length of its first step as arguments of the entry, returns the forces at its end, and, when it continues the last, neither projects onto the constraints again nor logs the terms; parts, `request_stop()`, Ctrl-C, and a failed part keeping the state before it are those of D196. `state()` holds the positions in input order, the velocities as given, the forces at the final positions, `step` the steps taken, `time` 0, `energies` `None`, and `minimization`, the row of the log at the last step in kJ/mol and nm (energy with its constant parts, RMS and largest force without their parts along the constraints, the particle of the largest, zero-based, and the next step length). A next stage starts from a new `InitialState` with those positions and cell, as `mdir run` begins anew at the checkpoint of a minimization. *Evidence.* On the dipeptide in water with PME, SHAKE, SETTLE, and a restraint, deterministic: in double on the CPU and a GPU, the row at step 100 (one part), at step 110 after ten parts of one step, and at step 30 after thirty such parts equal those of `mdir run` to every printed digit, positions within 3.2e-12 nm, and an NVT stage from the minimized state equals `mdir run` from its checkpoint to every printed digit at step 10; in mixed precision within three times the difference of `mdir run` in mixed and in double, since with PME the model and `mdir run` differ in mixed precision from the first evaluation (#105); with cutoff electrostatics in mixed precision, one part of 20 steps gives every printed digit and twenty parts agree within 4.6e-8.
