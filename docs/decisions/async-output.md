# D[async-output]

Recorded at its merge.

**The frames of a trajectory of a run of dynamics are copied from the device and written while the steps go on: the call of a frame takes the positions and the numbers of the particles in place and stages copies of them in a slot of a ring of three, on a GPU by a copy within the device on the stream of the kernels and a copy from there to pinned memory of the host on a stream of copies of its own, and one thread writes the frames from the slots in the order of the steps. Every frame queued is written before a checkpoint stages its state, before the trajectory closes, at a stop on a signal or the wall time, before a failure ends the run, at the end of each part of a Python simulation, and at the exit of the process; the files are those of a run that writes each frame in its call, byte for byte.** #113. The dynamics entries (`SimpleStepEntryIR.cpp`) call `mdrtStageFrame` with the fields `in_place` (D215) where they called `mdrtWriteFrame`, which a minimization keeps. On a GPU the call copies each buffer into a buffer of the device that the slot owns, ordered after the step that produced it and before any later kernel overwrites it, records an event, and the stream of copies waits for it and copies to pinned memory (`mdrtStaging*` in `runtime/mdrt_cuda.c`, [runtime.md](../runtime.md), Section 5.3); on the CPU the call copies the buffers of the host. The thread waits for the slot's second event, orders, converts, and writes the frame as `mdrtWriteFrame` does (the order of the input; Å in f32 for DCD and XTC, with XTC's compression; the state in nm for H5MD, under the mutex of #262), and gives the slot back. A call that finds the three slots taken waits for the thread, so the memory stays bounded: three copies of the positions and the numbers of the particles on the device and in pinned memory, 0.66 MB each on JAC and 30 MB on STMV. The changes of the cell of the trajectory that a barostat makes (`mdrtSetBox`, `mdrtSetTilt`) go through the same queue, so each frame has the cell it had. Without a periodic cell the call waits for its own copies and checks the spread of the particles (D142) before it queues the write, so the run stops at the same step with the same error. A write that fails on the thread (H5MD) drops the works queued after it, and the next frame, checkpoint, stop, failure, or end reports its error with the status 1, as the call did; rows of the log of the steps in between may come before the stop. `stopOnFailure` writes the frames queued before a failure and reports the earlier error of a frame first. A Python simulation waits for its frames at the end of each part (`mdrtPartBoundary`), before its reporters change, before a cell set from Python, a frame of its state, or a checkpoint, and when it ends. `MDIR_FRAME_WRITE_DELAY` holds each write back for a test, and `MDIR_CHECKPOINT_TIMES` times the call (the wait for a slot and the staging) and the write ([environment.md](../environment.md)). No control-file key, file format, default, or overwrite behavior changes; the ring of three is not a key. *Measured* (JAC, 23,558 atoms, and a box of TIP3P water of 12,600 atoms, on one RTX 4090, mixed precision, NVE velocity Verlet of 2 fs, groups, 10,000 steps, the mean of windows of 1000 steps after the first quarter, two jobs of two runs of `main` 69d7cafd and of the change, alternated): with energies and frames every 10 steps, JAC with DCD ran at 0.1953 against 0.1591 ms/step and with XTC at 0.2427 against 0.1594, against 0.1580 and 0.1579 with the energies alone (the frames' share from +23.6% and +53.6% to +0.8% and +0.9%); the water box at 0.1371 against 0.1168 and 0.1605 against 0.1164, against 0.1151 (+19.1% and +39.4% to +1.5% and +1.1%). Every 100 steps, JAC: 0.1409 against 0.1369 (DCD) and 0.1452 against 0.1366 (XTC), against 0.1368 and 0.1371; the energies alone every 1000, 100, and 10 steps are unchanged within the spread (0.1346, 0.1368, 0.1580 against 0.1350, 0.1371, 0.1579). The four runs of a program and setting lie within 0.002 ms/step. Under nsys (3000 steps, energies every 100), a step of energy with a frame of JAC on `main` spanned 973 µs, with 641 µs of device idle against 183 µs for a step of energy alone: the copies to pageable memory and their waits, and about 390 µs in which the host wrote the frame. *Evidence.* In the deterministic mode on the GPU (JAC NVE and NPT) and on the CPU (the water box), in mixed and double precision, with DCD, XTC, and H5MD, energies and frames every 10 steps (25 under NPT) and a checkpoint every 200 of 600 steps (400 on the CPU): the trajectories, the energy file, the rows of the log, and the checkpoint and `.prev` of `main` and of the change are identical, byte for byte for DCD, XTC, and the files of columns, in contents for the HDF5 files, whose library writes other bytes from run to run (`main` against itself too), with each frame held back by 5 ms as without; a run stopped at each checkpoint on the wall time and continued gives the files of `main`'s run continued the same way. `frame-writer.test` and `frame-writer-gpu.test` hold each write back by 20 ms, longer than the steps between two frames, so the ring fills and later frames wait for a slot; the files of the run, and of one stopped at its first checkpoint and continued, are those of a run not held back.
