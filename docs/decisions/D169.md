# D169

Recorded 2026-10-03.

**After the first milestone, the milestones are M2, the Python API; M3, learned potentials (MLIP); and M4, distributed execution.** Set by the maintainer on 2026-10-03. M1 (an all-atom protein in water with an Amber force field, at least the rate of pmemd.cuda on every system of the Amber suite, D114) is done. M2 is Section 6 of the roadmap: loading, a `System` apart from its execution, an explicit compile, runs and reporters, state as host arrays and DLPack views, typed errors, one checkpoint format; both front ends produce the same IR. M3 is the single-GPU part of D166 and D167: ML1 (one Allegro artifact through the metatomic adapter, energy, forces, and virial on one GPU) and the single-GPU stage of ML4 (PaiNN, then MACE). M4 is distributed execution: ML2 (ownership, halos, and transfers, with LJ and EAM), ML3 (the ML1 model on two GPUs), ML5 (plan selection and scaling), and distributed particle mesh Ewald (formerly M2c). Features of classical MD between milestones (free energy, collective variables, coarse-grained models) continue without a milestone of their own. Amends P3 (as amended by A10 and A14) and the order of D166, whose first deliverable ended on two GPUs: the two-GPU validation now closes M4, not M3.
