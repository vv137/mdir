# D142

Recorded 2026-10-03.

**`type = "NONE"` in [boundary] runs without a periodic cell: the run places the particles in a cell that keeps every image beyond the reach of the neighbor structures, and stops if they spread so far that one could come within it.** Along each axis the cell is the extent of the particles at the start and three times `pairlist_distance`, so that an image is farther than that reach while the particles spread less than the cell less it, twice the reach more than at the start; the IR and its kernels are those of a periodic run, whose minimum image is then the identity for every pair within reach. Positions are stored as they are, never wrapped, so a molecule that drifts out of the cell placed around it runs on. Wherever positions reach the host, at frames, checkpoints, and the end, the run measures the spread and stops with an error that names the axis if it passes the bound; a larger `pairlist_distance` places a larger cell. The cell of the file of coordinates is ignored, so that a solvated topology runs as in vacuum; the readers of Amber (IFBOX = 0, a file of coordinates without a box) and GROMACS (a box of zeros) take files without one, and a periodic run refuses such a file with a pointer to `NONE`. Refused without a cell: particle mesh Ewald, a barostat, the correction for the dispersion, which takes a density (it is off by default), and `box`. The reaction field is allowed; its continuum beyond the cutoff is the user's model of the surroundings. The log has no column of the pressure; a DCD says that its frames hold no cell and has none, an XTC has a cell of zeros, as GROMACS writes for no periodic boundary; a checkpoint records the boundary as `none`, with the cell placed, which a continued run keeps. This is a periodic cell that no image reaches, not an open boundary: the neighbor structures bin the cell, $O(\prod_k L_k/w)$ bins, cheap for the small systems that run without a cell; an open cell, with the minimum image dropped from the kernels and the templates of the neighbor structures binning the bounding box of the particles at each build, is the next step if a system needs one. On the peptide of `cmap.test` (`test/Driver/no-cell.test`, and on a GPU with both neighbor structures), which in its periodic cell of 60 Å with a cutoff of 28 Å has no pair beyond the cutoff: every term to the printed digit and the forces within $10^{-9}$ of the largest without a cell; given a drift of 600 Å/ps it leaves the cell of 97 Å placed around it within 0.2 ps, and the energies and positions are those of the periodic run, the total energy changing by $2.0\times10^{-9}$ of its value in both; two atoms flying apart stop the run at the first checkpoint after they spread past the bound; a run continued twice is the one that was not interrupted, its checkpoint and its trajectory byte for byte. *Amended by D153: the spread is checked at every row of the log as well.*
