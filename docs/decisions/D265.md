# D265

Recorded 2026-10-16.

**Langevin dynamics with constraints takes the g-BAOAB step of Leimkuhler and Matthews (2016) [[Leimkuhler2016]](../references.md#leimkuhler2016), with one drift in each A ($K_r = 1$), and under Langevin dynamics the log's temperature is that of $K_\text{half}$, the kinetic energy of the half steps around each step.** Issue #268, amends D135. B: a half kick and the projection of the velocities on the constraints (RATTLE, SETTLE); A: a drift of $\Delta t/2$, the constraints of the positions with the positions before that drift as the reference, the velocities of the displacement, and their projection at the new positions; O: friction and noise, and the projection; A; the forces; B. Under the barostat of Trotter type the scaling follows the projection after O. The friction and the noise act on the velocities of the middle of the step, between the drifts, which BAOAB samples at the temperature of the bath where it samples the configurations [[Leimkuhler2013]](../references.md#leimkuhler2013), so the temperature takes $K_\text{half}$, not the optimal estimate; with constraints it is measured from the chord velocities of the second A of the step and of the first A of the next without its noise, without them it is exact from the forces. On the bilayer of `examples/popc-bilayer` in NVT at 303 K, $T_\text{half}$ of the lipids and of the water was 303.1 and 302.9 K at 2 fs and 303.7 and 302.9 K at 4 fs with repartitioned hydrogen masses, where the optimal estimate read 299.2 and 302.3 K, and 293.4 and 300.5 K. *Evidence (#268):* on rigid water the old step left the state +1.4 K above the target at 2 fs and +4.8 K at 4 fs; g-BAOAB −0.2 and −0.4 K.
