# D136

Recorded 2026-10-03.

**Terms over tuples are given by expressions, as the custom forces of OpenMM over bonds, angles, and torsions: [[energy.bond]] in `r`, [[energy.angle]] and [[energy.dihedral]] in `theta`.** A term has a name, an expression in the syntax of D22, `particles` (lists of 2, 3, or 4 numbers of the topology, from 1), and parameters: any other keyword, a number for all tuples or a list of one for each. The expression is evaluated in the units of the control file: `r` in Å, `theta` in radians (the angle at the middle particle, or the dihedral in (−π, π] with trans at π), the energy in kcal/mol. Each term becomes a tuple set `custom_<name>` with a field for each parameter and one `md.sum_tuples` in the potential, whose forces and virial the differentiation of D2 gives, as for the terms of a topology; the log prints its energy at the start under its name. The terms add no exclusions. The syntax of expressions grows to that of OpenMM [[Eastman2017]](../references.md#eastman2017): the functions `asin`, `acos`, `atan`, `sinh`, `cosh`, `floor`, `ceil`, `step`, `delta`, `square`, `cube`, `recip`, `sec`, `csc`, `cot`, `min`, `max`, `atan2`, and `select`, and definitions of names after semicolons (`k*d^2; d = r - r0`), a definition that refers to itself being an error. The reader of GROMACS gives dihedrals of Ryckaert and Bellemans (function 3) and of Fourier (function 5) as such terms, `ryckaert_bellemans` and `fourier`, which makes OPLS-AA readable; on the peptide of `scripts/validation/gromacs`, every term agrees with GROMACS 2026.3 within 4.3e-6, the dihedrals of Ryckaert and Bellemans within 1.6e-6. *Amended 2026-10-03:* the bonds and the angles of function 2 of GROMOS, $\frac14 k (r^2 - b_0^2)^2$ and $\frac12 k (\cos\theta - \cos\theta_0)^2$, are such terms as well, `gromos_bond` and `gromos_angle`, which makes gromos54a7 readable: on the peptide of `scripts/validation/gromacs` in SPC water, every term agrees with GROMACS 2026.3 within $6.5\times10^{-6}$, the two within $4.4\times10^{-6}$ and $2.9\times10^{-6}$, the angles of the water, 14 kJ/mol in all, within $2.1\times10^{-5}$, and the Coulomb terms of the reaction field within $1.2\times10^{-5}$ (`test/Driver/gromacs-gromos.test`).
