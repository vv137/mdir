# D123

Recorded 2026-10-02.

**Triclinic cells run on the CPU** (docs/triclinic.md, P0 and P1). `md.triclinic_cell` takes the diagonal $a_x, b_y, c_z$ and the tilts $b_x, c_x, c_y$ of the reduced lower-triangular cell of the GROMACS manual; in a module that has one, a cell lowers to `vector<6xf64>`, and `md_exec.cell_edges` gives its diagonal, so the test of validity of D80 is as it was. The kernels of pairs and tuples take the minimum image in one pass along c, b, and a (`kernels::emitMinimumImage`), exact within half of the least of $a_x, b_y, c_z$, which the build requires of the cutoff and, for the matrix, of its reach; the neighbor matrix bins in the fractional coordinates, with the cells of the widths of the cell between its faces; PME spreads and gathers through $H^{-1}$ and convolves with $\mathbf m = H^{-1}\mathbf k$ and the grid from $\lvert\mathbf a\rvert, \lvert\mathbf b\rvert, \lvert\mathbf c\rvert$, in triclinic functions of its template. An orthorhombic run compiles as before. A truncated octahedron of Amber agrees with sander in every term (the electrostatics to the ratio of the Coulomb constants, 1.0000346, to $2\times10^{-8}$); a rhombic dodecahedron agrees with GROMACS to $4\times10^{-7}$ except in Coulomb, $4.5\times10^{-6}$, its single precision. The device and the barostat refuse a triclinic cell until P2 to P4. *Amended by D125: the device runs it with the neighbor matrix.* *Amended by D241: the build of the matrix tests every image within its reach, and the build requires half of the least of $a_x, b_y, c_z$ of the cutoff alone.*
