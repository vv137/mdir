# D259

Recorded 2026-10-16.

**Steepest descent takes a trial only if the constraints took every group back to its shape: a trial whose constrained lengths are off their targets, $\lvert r^2/d^2 - 1\rvert > 10^{-4}$ below double precision and $10^{-8}$ in double, or are not numbers, is evaluated where the step began and given the energy $+\infty$, so that the step shrinks.** Issue #343. As the step of D73 grows toward its cap of 1 Å, a rigid water of a large force turns by tens of degrees in one trial, beyond what M-SHAKE (below double precision) and SETTLE (in double) solve: for one bond of length $d$ moved by $\delta$ across it, the projection along the old bond has a solution only for $\delta \le d$. M-SHAKE then returned a torn water (an O–H of 8.3 Å), whose energy, with no term of its own for its bonds, was lower, and the trial was taken; every later trial carried the change that takes the water back, which does not shrink with the step (as a start off the surface did before D120), and the step shrank to 0. SETTLE gave positions that are not numbers, and on a GPU the run failed at a build of the neighbor structure. On a GPU in mixed precision with `main`, 103,292 OPC waters stalled within 240 steps in four runs of four, and 31,808 within 300 steps; with the check both go 5000 steps in mixed and double precision with every water at its shape, to $-14.37$ and $-14.29$ kcal/mol per water, and NVT from the large box runs. The tolerance lies between the rounding of intact waters in a cell of 150 Å stored in f32 ($4\times10^{-7}$, measured) and a torn water ($> 10^{-1}$); dynamics has no tolerance of its own for M-SHAKE and SETTLE, which run a fixed number of iterations. The energy at $\mathbf x$ keeps every position that reaches the neighbor structure a number. Steepest descent in GROMACS likewise gives a step whose constraints fail an infinite energy [[Abraham2015]](../references.md#abraham2015). `minimize-rigid-water.test` and `-gpu.test`: three OPC waters with a first step of 2 Å, which before tore a water in mixed precision and stopped the run on a GPU in double ([dynamics.md](../dynamics.md), Section 4; paper Section 6.7). *Amends D73.*
