# D198

Recorded 2026-10-06.

**Python draws initial velocities by the CLI's code and takes positional restraints as typed `[[restraints]]` tables.** Maintainer ruling on PR #86 (option A for both) and on PR #94. `InitialState.draw_velocities(system, temperature, seed)` prepares the system as `compile` does (NVE at `temperature`) and calls `assignVelocities`, the draw of `mdir run` when its coordinates give no velocities: a normal draw of variance $k_B T/m_i$ from the generator of `seed`, none for massless particles, the components along SETTLE and SHAKE removed, the motion of the center of mass removed, and a scaling to $\tfrac12 N_\mathrm{df} k_B T$; it returns a new state. The system is an argument because masses, constraints, and virtual sites are in it; the draw has its own seed, and `Ensemble.seed` stays the thermostat's, so repeating `mdir run` takes the same value in both. `System.restraints` holds `mdir.Restraint` values (`selection`, `force_constant` in kJ/mol/nm², the public units of D191, `reference_scaling` of D124), which `compile` turns into the control's restraints and prepares by the same code; the constant goes to kcal/mol/Å² by the value that converts back to it exactly where one exists. `System.restraint_reference` is the reference, `(N, 3)` nm, absent meaning the positions of the compiled state, in the frame of that state's cell; a simulation no longer overwrites a reference it is given. A restraint that selects nothing raises a Python `UserWarning` with the CLI's text. Copies in and out and versions follow D192/D193. *Evidence.* The drawn velocities of the dipeptide in water (1168 particles), with and without constraints, equal those of `mdir run` for the same control file in every bit, and the file and object models give identical restraint constants, scalings, references, fields, and IR for overlapping CENTER and ALL restraints under NPT, on CPU and GPU targets in mixed and double. With those restraints and drawn velocities, NVT and NPT runs of 20 steps match the rows of `mdir run` at step 10 to every printed digit (on the GPU in mixed precision within 1e-6 of each value, a difference with or without restraints that the energy steps of the constraints give, #97, #102) and its checkpoint at step 20 within the budgets of D196. See [python-velocities-restraints.md](../python-velocities-restraints.md). *Amended by D216.*
