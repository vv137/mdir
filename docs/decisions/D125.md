# D125

Recorded 2026-10-02.

**Triclinic cells run on a device, with the neighbor matrix** (docs/triclinic.md, P2). The lowering to a device carries the cell as the CPU does (D123): `vector<6xf64>` in a module with an `md.triclinic_cell`, its diagonal for `md_exec.cell_edges` and the test of validity of D80, and the minimum image in one pass in the kernels of pairs and tuples, which needed nothing more. The matrix of the device has a build of its own, `@mdrt_gpu_build_neighbors_matrix_triclinic`: the particles are binned by their fractional coordinates in cells of the widths of the cell between its faces (`kernels::emitFaceWidths`, shared with the CPU), the search wraps the indices of the cells per axis, which is exact in fractional coordinates, and tests with the minimum image of the cell in f32; its sort of the particles by cell is the function that the orthorhombic build calls (`@mdrt_gpu_matrix_sort`). PME takes the kernels of `PMEGPUTriclinic.mlir`, which the script that writes `PMEGPU.mlir` writes as well and which only a triclinic module parses: the particles are placed by $\mathbf s = \mathbf x H^{-1}$, by the weights of order 4 (D102) as by the other paths; the forces are $-q H^{-1}$ times the derivatives along the axes of the grid; the tables keep the signed numbers $\mathbf n$ of the points and the factors of the B-splines, and the convolution computes $\mathbf k = H^{-1}\mathbf n$ and $e^{-\pi^2 k^2/\beta^2}$ at each point in the type of the grid, since $k^2$ is not a sum of a term for each axis (D104 does not apply). The orthorhombic templates are as they were, to the byte for PME. On the rhombic dodecahedron of `test/Driver/triclinic-gpu.test`, double precision on the device gives the terms, the virial, and 200 steps of the CPU to the printed digits; mixed precision, deterministic mode, and order 6 agree to $10^{-5}$. Amber's truncated octahedron in OPC conserves 10 ps at constant energy as on the CPU ($6.3\times10^{-4}$ against $5.8\times10^{-4}$), at 398 ns/day against 5.7. The structure of groups (P3) and the barostat (P4) refuse a triclinic cell.
