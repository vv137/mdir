# D293

Recorded 2026-10-19.

**A stage's body may be batched: a function of `linalg` on tensors with a static leading dimension, the rows of a tile, which may compute in f32 where the declaration says f64. StableHLO is legalized to `linalg` when a model is exported (StableHLO's `stablehlo-legalize-to-linalg` in `stablehlo-opt`, pinned in `scripts/mlip/painn-stages.py`), so that MDIR's build takes no dependency on StableHLO. `md-inline` leaves the applications of batched stages in the kernels; `md-exec-lower-stage-bodies` lowers the bodies to functions of buffers (elementwise fusion, one-shot bufferization, a kernel for each parallel loop on a device, the bodies' buffers from the runtime's pool); `convert-md-exec-to-gpu` runs a loop whose kernel applies them as a host loop over tiles: for each level of the applications, a kernel packs the operands of the tile's rows and the bodies are called; a last kernel unpacks the results and runs the rest of the kernel. The rows of a loop over pairs are the slots of the pairs of images (D277), and a center adds up its rows in one thread: a gather, without atomics. Wide values (features) go component by component through the kernels.** *Ruled* (the maintainer on #447, 2026-10-19: StableHLO through `linalg` and MDIR's own lowering first, legalized at export). The loops of batched stages run on a device only. Measured with nablaDFT's PaiNN on an RTX 4090: 1.4 to 2.1 kB of device memory a pair of liquid water from 6,144 atoms up, against 61 kB for the opaque call; about 10 µs a pair, about 20 times the opaque call's (the naive lowering of `linalg`; 4b compares AOTInductor). See [ml4-ir.md](../ml4-ir.md#8-painn-in-stages-step-4), Section 8.3.
