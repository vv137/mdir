# D327

Recorded 2026-10-22.

**The Amber suite is measured with MDIR and pmemd.cuda under one protocol, that of each system's `mdin.GPU`: the same steps (`nstlim`), the same output (energies every `ntpr` steps, positions every `ntwx`, restarts or checkpoints every `ntwr`), and the rate over the same window, from the first report to the last step; MDIR with the searched and with the default reaches; and the whole wall time of each run.** #534, at the maintainer's request (2026-10-22). Before, MDIR ran its own short runs (JAC 10,000 steps, Factor IX 4000, Cellulose 1000, STMV 500), timed over their second half, with the energies twice and no trajectory, and only with the searched reaches, while pmemd.cuda ran `mdin.GPU` as it is and was timed over all steps; setup and compilation were in neither rate and not reported. That protocol favored MDIR. `scripts/benchmarks/amber/bench.py run --suite-protocol` takes `nstlim`, `ntpr`, `ntwx`, and `ntwr` from `mdin.GPU`; MDIR writes DCD (f32, uncompressed, the format closest in cost to pmemd's NetCDF) and H5MD checkpoints (Cellulose every 6000 steps for 8000, since MDIR's intervals divide the steps). pmemd's rate is its "Average timings for last N steps" (N = `nstlim` − `ntpr`); MDIR's is timed by the script's clock at the rows of steps `ntpr` and `nstlim` of its log, which MDIR prints after the device has finished the step, so no code of MDIR changed; over MDIR's own window that clock agrees with MDIR's to within 0.05% in all 80 runs. `--energies-only` (pmemd: `ntwx=0`, `ntwr=nstlim`) gives the share of the output. No change of the dynamics of either program; the couplings still differ (MDIR Bussi and stochastic cell rescaling every 25 steps, pmemd Berendsen and a Monte Carlo barostat), and pmemd is not tuned beyond its inputs. *Measured* (one RTX 4090, ada-003, `main` at afc96bf5, three repeats alternated in one job, white paper Tables 10.2 to 10.4): MDIR with the searched reaches at 146.9%, 149.5%, 140.3%, 141.7% of pmemd.cuda on JAC NVE, NVE 4 fs, NPT, NPT 4 fs, 125.6% and 125.8% on Factor IX NVE and NPT, 117.2% and 120.2% on Cellulose, 111.7% on STMV; with the default reaches 141.8%, 143.6%, 137.4%, 138.6%, 125.1%, 126.0%, 114.8%, 117.7%, 105.8%. *History*: the previous protocol, at D285 on the same node (2026-10-18), gave 148.3%, 151.0%, 139.1%, 141.5%, 129.7%, 127.5%, 124.5%, 125.6%, 125.5% in the same order; on an RTX 4090 at D280 with fixed skins, 136% to 143% on JAC, 114% on Factor IX, 99% and 102% on Cellulose, 98% on STMV; at D134 on an RTX 3090 at 300 W, 104.4% to 131.8%. Most of STMV's change is a checkpoint of MDIR inside its window of 1000 steps (10.4% of it; with the energies alone MDIR is at 124.2%; #548); the rest of the changes are within how much both programs' rates moved between the two jobs (pmemd.cuda −3.6% on JAC NVE, +2.4% on Cellulose NVE) and the change of program, not separated. MDIR spends 7.9 to 27.9 s of a run outside its steps, pmemd.cuda 0.6 to 6.9 s, so each run of the suite takes longer with MDIR; the derived wall time of 1 ns is 75% to 96% of pmemd.cuda's.
