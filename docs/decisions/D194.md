# D194

Recorded 2026-10-05.

**On a device, the sums of a loop over tuples evaluated once that reach only a later such loop, through arithmetic, are computed by a block of the kernel of the run of the later loop; a term over the centers of groups then takes no kernel of its own.** After D181 the sums over the centers of a term over centers (D139) were a reduction of one part in a kernel of one block, 4.6 µs a step on JAC, mostly the shuffles of nine sums in f64, and its forces ran in the kernel of the bonded loops. `convert-md-exec-to-gpu` now finds a loop with global sums over tuples evaluated once whose results reach nothing but arithmetic on the host that reaches nothing but the kernel of one later loop over tuples evaluated once, with the same positions and cell, and nothing between them that writes what the sums read. The kernel of the run of that loop gains a block for it, before the blocks of the other loops of the run: the block computes the rows of the reduction of one part, the same rows with the same strides, and adds them by the one-pass reduction of a block (D150), so that the sums are those of the kernel it replaces bit for bit; then it evaluates the arithmetic, which the forces took before, and the tuples of the forces, every 128th per thread. With more than 512 rows the kernels of several parts still run where the loop is, launched only for several parts, and leave the totals in a buffer of the device of their own, which the block reads; the buffers of the parts may then be shared with later loops. The deterministic mode, which does not evaluate tuples once (D84), is unchanged. A loop whose sums the log or the pull file also reads, as at the rows of the energies, keeps its reduction. `fuse-centers=false` turns it off. *Evidence.* JAC (RTX 3090 at 300 W, GPU 0, mixed, groups, 50,000 steps, rate of steps 30,000 to 50,000, two runs each, the bias of #16, a harmonic bias on the distance between the centers of two groups of 20 backbone atoms): main 0.2209 and 0.2212 ms/step without the bias, 0.2301 and 0.2305 with it (+9.2 and +9.3 µs); this branch 0.2209 and 0.2210 without, 0.2237 and 0.2237 with it (+2.7 µs); OpenMM 8.2 +8.6 µs. Kernels in 2,000 steps (nsys): main 47,411 without and 49,386 with the bias, this branch 47,355 and 47,379. In the deterministic mode (matrix, 1,000 steps, mixed and double) the forces and the energies equal main bit for bit. Outside it, after one step, the forces differ from main by at most 6.0e-3 (mixed) and 6.7e-12 kcal/mol/Å (double), against 1.1e-2 and 6.8e-12 between two runs of main (atomics); with groups of 640 and 637 atoms (residues 1 to 40 and 41 to 80, 1,277 rows in three parts) 1.1e-2 and 1.8e-11, against 1.1e-2 and 2.0e-11, and the energy of the bias equals main. `centroid-terms-gpu.test` (three terms fused in one kernel, against the forces of `check_centroid.py`) passes; `Sanitizer/centroid-gpu.test` runs them under memcheck, initcheck, and racecheck, and the several parts on JAC pass memcheck and racecheck.
