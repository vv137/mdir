# D101

Recorded 2026-10-01.

**A loop over groups takes the positions as they are stored; its gather moves them to the frames of the groups in f64 and then converts them.** `md-exec-assign-precision` gave every loop over pairs the positions converted to the type of its kernel, by a loop of its own, so the gather of D95 added the shift of each place to f32 positions that are unwrapped: an error of the rounding of a coordinate as far as the particle has gone (6e-5 Å at 1000 Å), not of one in the cell, which D95 stated wrongly. `md-exec-choose-neighbors` now runs before the precision is assigned, the conversion skips a loop that takes each pair once (the loops over groups), and the gather reads the positions in f64, adds the shift there, and stores the sum in the type of the kernel. The loop over pairs and its kernel are unchanged; the gather reads 24 bytes a position, not 12: 7 µs a step more on Cellulose (3163, from 3156; RTX 3090). The positions in the frames round otherwise: on JAC at the start, Lennard-Jones 9123.525006 kcal/mol (from 9123.524766) and Coulomb −62721.986496 (from −62721.987001; −62721.9880 in double precision).
