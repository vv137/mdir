# D304

Recorded 2026-10-20.

**`[pme] influence = "P3M"` (Python `PMEInfluence.P3M`) takes eq. 2.28 of [[Ballenegger2012]](../references.md#ballenegger2012), the influence function that minimizes the error of the forces of analytic differentiation, with the Gaussian and B-splines, the sums over the aliases at $\max_a\lvert J_a\rvert \le 2$; opt-in, `"SPME"` stays the default; a rectangular cell only (the maintainer's ruling on PR #474; a triclinic one is #475).** #468 step 1. Derived (`docs/pme.md`, Section 1.1): the error of the force between two charges, averaged over their place relative to the grid, is quadratic in $G$, and eq. 2.28 is its minimum; eq. 4.1 is eq. 2.28 without the aliases of its numerator, which MDIR keeps. In a rectangular cell the three sums factor over the edges exactly, $G = (f/\pi V)\prod_aF_a/\sum_a\langle\mu_a^2\rangle$, so the driver writes the weights of the aliases of each index (18 rows of `moduli`, cell-free), the tables of the edges are computed from the cell at every evaluation as the Gaussian's are (D104), and the product without the energy is the kernel of the Gaussian: no table of the grid, and nothing new under a barostat. The virial differentiates every alias, $W_{ab} = \sum E(\delta_{ab} - (2\pi^2/\beta^2)A_{ab} - 2B_{ab}/\mathrm{tr}\,B)$. `md.reciprocal` and `md_exec.reciprocal` take a unit attribute `p3m`; a module with it is another module for the compile cache. The builder (`lib/Driver/Influence.cpp`) takes a splitting and a window, with the direct sums over $\mathbf J \in \mathbb Z^3$ as the reference and the general path (for #467); the moduli of SPME and the factor of `"OPTIMAL"` moved there unchanged. *Measured*: `"OPTIMAL"` is eq. 4.2 to its truncation ($3.2\times10^{-7}$ at order 4); the form of the edges is the direct sum to $1.1\times10^{-14}$, the virial is $-\partial E/\partial\varepsilon$ under nine strains to $1.1\times10^{-10}$; an independent NumPy sum gives MDIR's reciprocal energy of JAC to $3.4\times10^{-10}$; the truncation changes $G$ by at most $4.8\times10^{-5}$ (order 4). $\Delta$ on JAC and Cellulose at $\beta$ = 0.39467 Å⁻¹, CPU and GPU, double and mixed: below `"OPTIMAL"`'s at every grid and order (by 0.05% at about 1 Å, up to 2% at 2 Å), below `"SPME"`'s by 2 to 3% at about 1 Å (order 4) and 2 to 6% at order 6, above it by 3 to 4% on grids of order 4 at 1.5 to 2 Å, where uncorrelated charges in NumPy show it below by 8 to 21%. On one RTX 4090, JAC 0.136 ms a step against 0.134 (`main`, `"SPME"` and `"OPTIMAL"`), Cellulose at constant pressure the same within 0.3%.
