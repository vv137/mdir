# D154

Recorded 2026-10-03.

**The virial of a term of the absolute positions is the derivative of its energy when the barostat scales the positions and the cell, $\mathsf W_{aa} = -\partial U/\partial\varepsilon_a$, and `scaling` in [[energy.external]] says how the term follows the cell: `"NONE"` keeps it fixed in space, `"CELL"` takes its positions in the frame of the cell, $\mathbf x \odot \mathbf L_0/\mathbf L$ with $\mathbf L_0$ the cell of the input; a run at constant pressure must say which (amends D148).** The barostat scales each particle about the origin and each rigid group of the constraints with its center of mass (D72), and the pressure takes the virial of the groups, the virial of the particles less $\sum_j (\mathbf x_j - \mathbf X) \otimes \mathbf F_j$ over the members of each group, from the total forces (D116, D119). For a sum over particles $S = \sum_i k(\mathbf x_i, \mathbf L)$ the derivative of the md dialect is then $\sum_i \mathbf x_i \otimes \mathbf F_i$ from the positions, and on the diagonal $-w\sum_i \partial k/\partial L_a\, L_a$ from the edges of the cell where the kernel reads them (`md_exec.cell_edges`), which the derivative of a kernel takes as an input of their own: fixed in space the virial is $\sum_i \mathbf x_i \otimes \mathbf F_i$, which depends on the origin as the scaling does; in the frame of the cell the two parts cancel and the term has none, as the restraints of `reference_scaling = "ALL"` have none but their displacements (D124). The group correction makes the arm of a member of a group its center. Before, the term added nothing to the virial, its energy fixed in space: the pressure that the log printed left it out, and a run at constant pressure refused it. On the dipeptide of D148 at the start, the diagonal of the virial that the three terms add is $\sum_i x_{ia} F_{ia}$ of Python on the CPU and on a GPU in double precision, and in the frame of the cell the virial does not change. At constant pressure (the dipeptide, its waters flexible, 0.5 fs, 1 atm, $\tau_p$ = 2 ps, 4000 steps, the count of Trotter type of D92), a field of 5 kcal/mol/Å/e on the water and three wells fixed in space change the conserved energy by +6.27 kcal/mol, against +4.86 without them and +18.01 with their virial left out; in the frame of the cell by +5.61, the same without its virial, which is none.
