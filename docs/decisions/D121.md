# D121

Recorded 2026-10-02.

**A topology of GROMACS may carry the terms of CHARMM force fields, and the Lennard-Jones of a topology takes a modifier of its own.** Angles of function 5 add ½ k_UB (r₁₃ − r13)² between their outer atoms (`Urey-Bradley`), and dihedrals of function 2 are harmonic impropers, ½ k (ξ − ξ0)² with the difference taken in [−π, π) (`harmonic impropers`); each is a set of tuples with its own term in the log. `lennard_jones_modifier` now applies to a topology as well, inside the kernel of the pairs and to the Lennard-Jones only, not to the direct sum of PME: `POTENTIAL_SHIFT`, `FORCE_SWITCH` (the cubic polynomial of GROMACS), and `POWER_FORCE_SWITCH`, the force switch of Steinbach and Brooks [[Steinbach1994]](../references.md#steinbach1994), which switches each inverse power on its own and is CHARMM's VFSWITCH. It is not a kind of `truncation` (B4), which applies to any kernel, and it switches the pairs three bonds apart too, as CHARMM does; a modifier takes `dispersion_correction = "NONE"`, and a switch of the potential stays for terms in the control file. Two particles in CHARMM 51b1 showed that VFSWITCH is the switch of Steinbach and Brooks to 2.2 × 10⁻⁹ and that the force switch of GROMACS is not: it differs by a constant below the switching distance and by up to 60% above it, 9.23 kcal/mol on 12,017 particles. On Trp-cage in CHARMM36m with 3900 TIP3P (`scripts/validation/charmm/run.py`), MDIR agrees with CHARMM to its printed digits in the bonded terms, to 2.2 × 10⁻⁷ in the Lennard-Jones (the digits of ε in the topology), and in Coulomb by the ratio of the constants, 332.0716 against 332.0637, 2.38 × 10⁻⁵; 1 ns at constant energy drifts by +0.03 ± 0.23 kcal/mol/ns. ParmEd leaves the special 1-4 parameters of CHARMM out of the topologies that it writes (123.9 kcal/mol here); the validation adds them as `[ pairtypes ]` ([charmm.md](../charmm.md)). `charmm27.ff` of GROMACS, rejected before for its function 5, agrees with GROMACS within 4.3 × 10⁻⁶ in every term (`gromacs-charmm-terms.test`).
