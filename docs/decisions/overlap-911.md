# D[overlap-911]

Recorded at its merge.

**Place the reciprocal-stream join at its first dependency.**
When reciprocal stream assignment is enabled, the direct pair traversal can
run on the main stream while PME writes its own result on the side stream.
This applies to TOWERS and to the MATRIX and GROUPS list paths. Both streams
may read the same positions. The force combination and any use of the
reciprocal energy or virial require the reciprocal stream to finish.

Use D87's storage effects, SSA uses and alias proof for every operation
crossed by the schedule, and check the concurrent interval again at GPU
lowering. Keep the order of force and energy arithmetic, including in
deterministic mode. The scheduling choice needs no new control-file key.

A step loop often yields the results of an inner loop that rotates the
buffers. If one inner iteration applies a permutation and its trip count is
known, follow the orbit of each result by the trip count modulo the orbit
length to identify its initial buffer. Compose this mapping at each level.
A global table may alias an argument or another global handle, while a
live allocation has distinct storage. Count reads of table buffers
captured by the direct kernel as well as its explicit fields and outputs.
An unknown permutation or possible conflict keeps the conservative join.
