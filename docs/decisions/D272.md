# D272

Recorded 2026-10-16.

**`[minimize]` and stochastic cell rescaling take a term of `[[energy.model]]`: the model's call follows every evaluation of the minimizer and of the barostat (the exact work in the scaled cell, and the start of a continued run that scales every step), its energy and virial enter as those of any other term, and the plugin builds its pair list from the cell of each evaluation.** Issue #367, PR #368, stacked on D270. *Ruled* (the maintainer, 2026-10-16): lift both refusals; validate the minimum against ASE's FIRE with the same model, NVE from MDIR's own minimum, the virial against differences of the energy under strain in all nine components, the group pressure of D264, and the density of a short NPT. No key is added. With no constraints every particle is a group of one, and the pressure of D264 is the atomic one with the model's virial. *Validation* (RTX 4090, the `nequip` package's ASE calculator evaluating the Allegro model as the reference; [metatomic-ml1.md](../metatomic-ml1.md#10-minimization-and-constant-pressure-d272)): the virial of a triclinic 81-atom box against central differences under strain with Richardson's extrapolation within 0.43 kJ/mol of components up to 2,627 kJ/mol in `double` and 0.37 in `mixed`, the energy's resolution; the logged pressure against $(2K + \mathrm{tr}\,\mathsf W)/3V$ from the log's columns correlated to 0.9999997, 25 atm against a spread of 5,600 atm; 24 waters in `mixed` over 4 to 10 ps of four runs at 1 atm, $V = 760 \pm 6$ Å³, $0.946 \pm 0.007$ g/cm³; at FIRE's minimum MDIR's energy is FIRE's to 0.01 kJ/mol and stops at the tolerance; from the lattice steepest descent reaches -37,697 kJ/mol in 20,000 steps (the `nequip` package's calculator agrees there) and stalls at -37,771 where FIRE reaches -37,881 through rearrangements, the mass-weighted step along the stiff O-H stretch falling to the resolution of the model's energy ($2 \times 10^{-4}$ kcal/mol); NVE from MDIR's own minimum drifts by $-0.52$ (`double`) and $-1.2$ (`mixed`) kJ/mol/ns per atom over 2 ps.
