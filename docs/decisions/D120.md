**A minimization begins from the positions of the file taken onto the surface of the constraints.** A trial step of D73 moves along the constrained descent from positions on that surface and takes the groups back to their shapes, a change of second order in the step. From positions off it, every trial carries besides the step the change that takes the groups to their shapes, which does not shrink with the step. A bilayer of 126 POPC of Lipid21 in TIP3P from packmol (`scripts/validation/barostat-semi/membrane.py`) has bonds of hydrogen up to 0.021 Å off their lengths and pairs of hydrogens of different lipids 0.13 Å apart; that change brings one pair from 0.128 to 0.097 Å, which raises the Lennard-Jones energy by $5.6\times10^{15}$ kcal/mol, while the step, which the largest force (that of another pair) scales so that no particle moves farther than $h$, takes that pair apart by far less. No trial was taken: the energy stayed at $2.9\times10^{15}$ kcal/mol and $h$ shrank to 0, with the constraints only (GPU and CPU, mixed precision); without them the minimization went down. Now SETTLE (M-SHAKE below double precision, D112) and SHAKE take the positions of the file onto the surface once, with themselves as the reference, and the sites are placed again, before the first evaluation; the energy at the start in the log and the terms are those of these positions. The bilayer then went from $1.05\times10^{16}$ to $-85{,}425$ kcal/mol in 5000 steps. On the dipeptide of `minimize.test`, whose bonds of hydrogen from tleap are also off their lengths, the energy at the start is $-5348.8659$ instead of $-5348.4328$ and after 50 steps $-6587.5294$ instead of $-6587.1388$. `minimize-clash.test`: four lipids of the bilayer with both close pairs (`Inputs/lipid21`), which stayed at $2.9\times10^{15}$ and now reach $-551$ kcal/mol in 200 steps.
