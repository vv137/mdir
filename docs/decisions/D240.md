# D240

Recorded 2026-10-09.

**`mdir.FrameEvaluator(program)` evaluates the potential that the forces sample (D210), the virial, and the columns of `observe` at $K$ stored frames given in the order of the input, each with its cell, in a simulation of its own, with one start of an activation per frame, and gives the vector-Jacobian product in the tunables, $\mathbf g \mapsto \sum_n g_n\,\partial U_{\boldsymbol\theta}(S_n)/\partial\boldsymbol\theta$; `mdir.torch.evaluate` makes it an operation of PyTorch (`torch.library.custom_op` with `register_autograd`).** Issue #249, a step of M2b (#138), [python-frames.md](../python-frames.md); the twelve questions of PR #250 were decided as recommended, but that the lossless store of frames is the H5MD reporter of D239, whose frames `mdir.read_h5md` gives the evaluator one at a time with their cells, and that each frame of a triclinic cell has its tilts, set with the checks of a commit (D238). *Why a new entry.* Through D229 and D230 a script loops a borrow, a commit, and `gradient()` over the frames: two starts of an activation per frame and a copy of the state to the host between them, 2.55 ms on the dipeptide in water and 16.8 ms on JAC (one RTX 3090, mixed precision). `Simulation::evaluateFrame` makes the state of the host the frame and begins one activation with the branch of the derivative: 1.05 ms (952 frames/s, 15 steps) and 6.85 ms (146 frames/s, 31 steps), a little under `gradient()`; a frame inside one activation, at a few steps, is the next slice. *The product.* The cotangent of a reweighting is known only after every energy, so the rows $\partial U_n/\partial\boldsymbol\theta$ are kept on the host while $8KM_\theta$ is at most `jacobian_bytes` (256 MiB), and `vjp` evaluates the frames again above it; the frames stay with the caller and are read in turn (an array, a `numpy.memmap`, an iterable of states or of pairs; a generator only where no second pass is needed). The program must carry `@tunable` (`System.tunable_gradient`), whose energy is that of D210. *Zero and failure.* For each tunable the three outcomes of D230. `depends` states, for each output, the tunables it may read at fixed positions: an output that reads none is a constant; the virial and a column of a term that a tunable enters are returned with a backward that raises `UnsupportedError` unless detached, since their derivative is not implemented and is not zero (#140, T1b); positions and cells that require a gradient are refused; the product of the backward is an operation without a derivative, so a second differentiation is an error of PyTorch. *The reference energies* of the weights are an evaluation by the same evaluator at $\hat{\boldsymbol\theta}$ of the same frames, so that the weights are uniform to the bit there; a DCD's f32 changes $U$ by $2\times10^{-3}$ kJ/mol, an XTC's $10^{-3}$ nm by $48 \pm 6$ kJ/mol with flexible bonds. *Amended (#264):* this row said that such frames are unusable; the rise is of the stiff terms and cancels in the difference of two energies at the same rounded frame, so that frames at $10^{-3}$ nm serve a tunable of a soft pair term or of the charges (error of $\Delta U$ 0.03 and 0.11 kJ/mol, the effective frames unchanged) and fail for a tunable of a stiff term (1.6 kJ/mol) and with reference energies recorded by the run (9 effective frames of 400); f64 stays the default because it serves any term chosen later ([python-frames.md](../python-frames.md#which-stored-precision-serves-which-reweighting)). *Checked* on the CPU and a GPU in double and mixed precision (`python-frames*.test`): each frame against the sampler's `gradient()` and state at that step, and frames under a barostat against a simulation compiled from the frame, to the bit in the deterministic mode, as are 20 frames of a run at constant pressure in a rhombic dodecahedron read from H5MD (`python-frames-h5md*.test`); the product against central differences in $\boldsymbol\theta$ ($2\times10^{-10}$ in double, $1.6\times10^{-4}$ in mixed); the derivative of a reweighted average of a mixture against central differences of the reweighted average ($7\times10^{-7}$) and from PyTorch against the covariance of the arrays ($2\times10^{-13}$); `gradcheck`; a fit of one parameter to a synthetic target; `torch.compile`. `mdir.KB` is Boltzmann's constant of the programs. Found on the way: tunable charges with a tunable constant of a pair term do not lower (#256). *Amended by D252: the argument `gradient=` of `evaluate` is replaced by `gradients=`, a tuple of names, whose default `("tunables",)` is what `gradient=True` was; positions and cells that require a gradient are taken by `mdir.torch.evaluate`; a second derivative raises `UnsupportedError`, not PyTorch's `RuntimeError`.*
