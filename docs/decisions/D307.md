# D307

Recorded 2026-10-21.

**`md-distribute` selects one static Cartesian factor grid for `md_dist.decomposition` from `domains=N box=Lx,Ly,Lz`, or takes the explicit `grid=gx,gy,gz` baseline.** #476, a scoped part of #35. For a positive orthorhombic compile-time box, among positive triples with product $N$, choose the one minimizing $g_x/L_x+g_y/L_y+g_z/L_z$, proportional to the three face areas of one domain; equal scores take the lexicographically smaller triple. The options are exclusive. Edge lengths must be positive and finite, and a grid product must fit in `i64`. This is a geometric estimate, not measured transport cost. The selected grid is a property of the existing decomposition op; its ownership, halo, and transfer semantics and the synchronous in-process transport do not change. The runtime cell can change without reselection. No control-file or Python key is added. Schedule and asynchronous MPI exchange remain in #35. Validation: the automatic and explicit forms of the 6-domain decomposition give byte-identical IR; on a cubic box the chosen triple is [1,2,3]. The grid-independent one-domain oracle agrees with the independent LJ reference energy -226.88590520072805; the distributed versus one-domain energy difference is at most 2.27e-8 in double or 2.27e-3 in mixed; the independent EAM reference energy is 31.860504482882163 and the corresponding bounds are 3.19e-9 and 3.19e-4. Every force and virial component is within 1e-10 (double) or 1e-5 (mixed) of its test scale, on the CPU and an RTX 4090. The focused pass and integration tests pass; the final suite result is in PR #478.
