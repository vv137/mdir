# D275

Recorded 2026-10-17.

**Distribution is a dialect, `md_dist`, whose values are global views of distributed fields: ownership is an SSA value (a layout), a halo is a value built from it with its reach in the type, a ghost value records the owned value it copies, and the owner-to-ghost copy $G$ (`halo_copy`) and the sum of the ghosts' contributions at the owner, $\mathbf a + G^\mathsf T\mathbf b$ (`halo_sum`), are ops declared to each other as adjoints.** Stage 1 of M4 (#31, #35, #38; the ruling on #377 of 2026-10-16); design in [distributed-ml2.md](../distributed-ml2.md). A field's version is its SSA value, so a missing transfer (a field read at neighbors without a ghost copy) and a stale halo (a ghost copy of another version than the owned value beside it) are rejected by the verifier, as are a reach below the cutoff, fields of two layouts, and partial values (contributions not yet summed at their owners) read by a kernel, used twice, or summed with contributions of another traversal. One pair relation has two traversals: `directed` computes every pair at the owner of each particle and needs no reverse transfer; `unique` computes each pair once and returns the ghosts' contributions by $G^\mathsf T$, which needs an exchange contract proven by the code of `md-check-exchange`. Ghosts carry their periodic images, so no domain takes a minimum image. The first executor runs all domains in one process (`--convert-md-dist-to-loops`, `runtime/mdrt_dist.c`) and is the oracle for MPI and GPU transports. `md-distribute` is to run before `md-differentiate`, so that the derivative takes $G^\mathsf T$ as the transpose of $G$; the directed traversal is then an optimization of the derivative's form, with a switch. No control-file key, Python API, or output changes; the keys ruled on PR #379 (`[distribute]` with `domains`, `transport`, `traversal`; `mdir.Distribute`) arrive with the first distributed trajectory. The exchange proof now follows components and lanes of vectors. *Validation* (CPU, `double`; [distributed-ml2.md](../distributed-ml2.md#12-what-the-first-pr-builds)): Lennard-Jones, 64 particles, on grids of 1, 2, 3x2 (uneven), and 16 (8 empty domains, domains narrower than the cutoff) against one domain through `md.evaluate` and `md_exec`: energy within $8.5\times10^{-14}$ of 226.886, force components within $5.2\times10^{-14}$ of a largest 16.997, virial components within $2.0\times10^{-13}$ of a trace of 660.908, on both traversals (tolerance $10^{-10}$ relative); the adjoint identity of $G$ and $G^\mathsf T$ to $4\times10^{-16}$; negative tests of every check of the verifier.
