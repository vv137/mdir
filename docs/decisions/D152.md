**Generalized Born takes the model of Hawkins, Cramer, and Truhlar (`implicit_solvent = "HCT"`), a salt (`salt_concentration` in mol/L), a cutoff of the descreening (`born_radius_cutoff` in Å), and the radii and screening of mbondi2 by element (`born_radii = "MBONDI2"`) for a topology without its own.** HCT takes $B = 1/(1/\tilde\rho - I/2)$ from the integral of D144 [[Hawkins1996]](../references.md#hawkins1996). A salt screens the solvent with the Debye length: $\tau = 1/\varepsilon_{solute} - e^{-\kappa f_{GB}}/\varepsilon_{solvent}$ for a pair and $f_{GB} = B$ for a particle, $\kappa^2 = 2 N_A e^2 c/(\varepsilon_0 \varepsilon_r k_B T)$ for a 1:1 salt from the constants of CODATA 2018 at the temperature of [ensemble], which must be above 0, and $\kappa$ scaled by 0.73 for the layer around the solute that excludes the ions [[Srinivasan1999]](../references.md#srinivasan1999) (0.927 nm⁻¹ at 0.15 mol/L, 300 K, and 78.5). The cutoff $R$ takes the integral over the shells within $R$ alone, $U = \min(r + s_j, R)$, over the pairs within $R$ and the largest screened radius, which must lie within the cutoff, so that a radius does not jump where a sphere crosses $R$; without it the integral takes every pair within the cutoff, as before. The radii of mbondi2 are those of Bondi [[Bondi1964]](../references.md#bondi1964) with 1.3 Å for a hydrogen bonded to a nitrogen [[Onufriev2004]](../references.md#onufriev2004), and the screening factors those of Hawkins, Cramer, and Truhlar for H, C, N, O, P, and S, 0.88 for F, and 0.8 otherwise; a topology of GROMACS or CHARMM needs them, which a topology of Amber has in RADII and SCREEN. On the peptide of D144 (mbondi2): HCT −141.432807 kcal/mol, OpenMM 8.6.1 (CustomGBForce of HCT, Reference) −141.432835, whose Coulomb constant, 138.935485 kJ nm/mol, is larger by $2.0\times10^{-7}$, and pmemd 26 (igb = 1) −141.4282, which is MDIR's −141.42791 with its constant 332.0522 to $2\times10^{-6}$, the series pmemd takes beyond $r > 4 s_j$; OBC II with 0.15 mol/L at 300 K −143.565200, OpenMM −143.565228 with the same κ, again the constant; OBC II with the integral cut at 8 Å −144.112309, pmemd (rgbmax = 8) −144.1073, MDIR's with its constant −144.10732; the radii of mbondi2 give the energies of the topology to the bit. check_born.py gives each value and the forces against central differences. The peptide from a topology of GROMACS that ParmEd wrote, with the radii of mbondi2, gives the energies of the topology of Amber at its rounded coordinates and the forces of generalized Born within $10^{-8}$, the charges of the file having eight decimals; the three options together on a GPU give the forces of the CPU within $10^{-9}$ of the largest in double precision. HCT radii are not bounded: a buried atom whose integral exceeds $2/\tilde\rho$ would have a negative radius, as in the references.
