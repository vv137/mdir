# D69

Recorded 2026-09-30.

**The reciprocal sum of particle mesh Ewald is one op, `md.reciprocal`**, which yields the energy and, when differentiation asks, the forces and the virial from the grid; it is not differentiated through. The direct sum, the excluded pairs, the self term, and the net charge need no new op. The op lowers to templates in IR for spreading, the product with the influence function, and gathering, on the CPU and on a GPU, and to calls of the FFT of the runtime: pocketfft on the host and cuFFT on a device (D64). The driver computes the influence function as a table ([pme.md](../pme.md)). *Amended 2026-10-02:* `md.reciprocal` yields the energy, the forces, and the virial at once, all from the grid; differentiation takes the forces and the virial from it.
