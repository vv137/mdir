# D303

Recorded 2026-10-20.

**On a device, `md-exec-lower-stage-bodies` lowers each sum of rows of a batched body to a kernel of a warp for each element of the result: a generic of one result in f32 or f64 whose one reduced loop has at least 32 iterations and runs along the contiguous dimension of every operand it indexes, and whose payload yields the accumulator (or a constant at the first reduced iteration) plus a term that does not read it. Lane $l$ adds the terms $l, l+32, \dots$ in order, the warp combines its lanes by D301's butterfly (`kernels::emitWarpReduce`), and the first lane stores the start plus the sum, in the type of the result. A dot product of rows is such a sum, not a GEMM.** The maintainer's ruling on #472: the row reductions reuse the shared reduction emitter of D301, with the algebra the sum states. The sums of a body state no `md_exec.algebra` (`algebra::reduces` covers `md_exec` and `md_dist`), so the lowering keeps an order fixed by the lanes: the same bits on every run, valid in every algebra and in the deterministic mode, as the loops' order was; the rounding differs from the loops' (another fixed order), and the accumulator's precision does not change. As IREE gives its row reductions a warp each (`VectorDistribute`). On an RTX 4090, `edge.b`'s sum over 384 took 205 µs of 0.37 ms as a thread a row; `edge.b` goes from 0.363 to 0.143 ms per call of 4,096 rows (XLA 0.073 with 0.05 ms of dispatch), and with the batched products of D302 a step of nablaDFT's PaiNN with #464's tile sums goes from 696 to 488 ms at 6,144 atoms, 1,399 to 982 at 12,288, and 22,222 to 15,623 at 196,608 (`main` at D301: 1,713, 3,392, 54,009). The JAX composition (81 atoms, double and mixed) and SchNetPack in float64 agree as before. Left: the sums over the three components (fewer than 32 terms) and `node.b`'s elementwise kernels.
