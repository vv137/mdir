# D158

Recorded 2026-10-03.

**More warnings of the system (D153), and a selection of nothing is one of them rather than an error.** `unused_parameter`: a parameter of a term over tuples, over centers, over pairs, or of the positions that its expression does not use. `constant_expression`: an expression that uses none of the coordinates of its term (`r`, `theta`, `dx`, `dy`, `dz`, or `x`, `y`, `z`), which adds an energy and no force. `empty_selection`: a term of the positions, a group of a pair term, or a restraint whose mask selects no particle; the term then adds nothing and the run goes on, where it stopped before. `long_time_step`: a step longer than 1 fs while the bonds of hydrogen are free in a topology with hydrogens. Each is printed by `mdir run` and `mdir check` and listed by `mdir check --json` under its code; the control file collects its own and the system adds them to its. A group of a term over centers that selects nothing stays an error, since its center is not defined. Asked by the user (2026-10-03, task #53).
