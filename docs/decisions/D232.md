# D232

Recorded 2026-10-08.

**A term of the Python model given by an expression takes `observe`, the control file's key (D189); a Python simulation writes the file of `[output] observables` through `ObservablesReporter` and gives the same values to a script in `State.observables` at every step of energy; tunables and `observe` share a program, and an observed constant may be a tunable with one entry.** Issue #188, M2a item 8, the last. `PairTerm.observe`, `TupleTerm.observe`, and `ExternalTerm.observe` are `None` or a list of constants of the term (`[]`: the energy alone), a compile input; a parameter of a tuple term is observed when its values are equal for all tuples, the control file's rule. The model hands the builder `Control::observables` in the order pair, tuple, external terms, and the builder emits `@observe<k>` as for the control file, with the conventions of D189, D209, and D210 (the term alone, shifted under a plain cutoff, the tail and shift estimate of a pair term at the volume of the cell); it now decides by the columns and not by the name of a file, and the program of segments (D196) gains the call at its plain step of energy beside those of its start and of its report intervals. The host keeps the values of every step of energy for the state (kJ/mol, per unit of the constant) and writes the rows that are due to the file, whose header and digits are `mdir run`'s (kcal/mol); the reporter has a period of its own, which joins the greatest common divisor of the built-in periods (D207), and its file is backed up, appended across runs, and continued from a checkpoint as the energy file is (D223). `Program.plan["observables"]` lists the columns. As for the control file, nothing of it enters the fingerprint or a checkpoint. Terms may observe without a reporter, since the state gives the values; a program that minimizes refuses `observe`, as `mdir run` refuses `observables` under `[minimize]` (settled on PR #213). *Tunables* (maintainer's decision on PR #213, Q2): the builder's refusal of tunables with `observe` is lifted; the columns follow updates, the tails being computed anew with the other constants of the energy (D213), and the file's last column is `tunables_version`; the value of an observed constant that a tunable takes reaches `@observe<k>` as a value of the entry (`Program::startValues`, D227), not a constant of the text, so an update keeps the program; a tunable with several entries on an observed parameter, or a map that leaves a tuple its value, is an `InputError` naming `gradient()` (D230). *Evidence* (`python-observe.test`, `-gpu.test`; deterministic, CPU and RTX 3090, double and mixed): on the dipeptide in water with a pair term, a term over two bonds, and the two walls of D189, under a plain cutoff and the shift, the observables file and the energy file of 20 steps equal those of `mdir run` on the same model byte for byte, and the values of the state at each row its rows; on the mixture of `pair-dispersion.test` with an NBFIX written as a pair term, the file equals `mdir run`'s byte for byte in NVE under both modifiers and in NPT, and what the correction adds to the columns equals the closed forms of the tail and the shift estimate ($-1.921376$ kcal/mol, $-43.801428$ kcal/mol/nm in $\sigma$, $-1.291884$ per kJ/mol in $\epsilon$) to $10^{-8}$; runs of 7 and 13 steps, a run continued from a checkpoint at step 10, and observables every 6 steps beside energies every 4 write the file of one run byte for byte; a checkpoint that Python wrote with `observe` on a term continues without it, and the reverse; an observed tunable's column equals `gradient()` and a compile with the value to $1.1\times10^{-16}$ in double and $3.5\times10^{-7}$ in mixed precision, and central differences of the observed energy by updates to $2.3\times10^{-8}$ and $9.9\times10^{-5}$. Without `observe` the modules and lowered IR of JAC and Factor IX and the modules, lowered IR, and programs of segments of five Python models equal `main`'s.
