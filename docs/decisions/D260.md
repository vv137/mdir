# D260

Recorded 2026-10-16.

**The step of steepest descent grows to at most 0.1 Å, not 1 Å.** Issue #351; the maintainer's ruling on PR #348. With D259 a trial too long for the constraints is not taken, but near a cap of 1 Å many trials are such, and each shrinks the step by 0.2. On the OPC boxes of #343 on a GPU in mixed precision, the first row whose largest force is below 5 kcal/mol/Å was at step 500 (103,292 waters) and 700 (31,808) with a cap of 0.1 Å, 2400 and 800 with 0.3 Å, and 3300 and 1600 with 1 Å; the energies after 5000 steps agree within 0.03% ($-1{,}483{,}470$, $-1{,}483{,}548$, $-1{,}483{,}814$ kcal/mol for the large box). A first step of `initial_step` longer than the cap is still taken as given; the steps after it are capped. The tests whose rows change: `minimize.test` (step 50), `minimize-gpu.test`, `minimize-tolerance.test` and the four `python-minimize-tolerance-*` tests (the stop at step 90 rather than 100), and `minimize-rigid-water.test` and `-gpu.test`, whose last row is now checked to -16.1 since the GPU trajectories part near the end ([dynamics.md](../dynamics.md), Section 4; paper Section 6.7). *Amends D73.*
