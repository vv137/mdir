# D160

Recorded 2026-10-03.

**Terms over the triplets centered on each particle are a relation of arity 3 that `md.triplets` derives from a neighborhood, summed by the tuple ops; on the CPU its members are written at every evaluation into a buffer as long as the triplets, and the loops over tuples run on it unchanged.** `md.triplets %n cutoff(r3)` is, for each center $i$, each unordered pair $\{j, k\}$ of its neighbors with $r_{ij}, r_{ik} < r_3$ once, as the tuple $(j, i, k)$ with the center at place 1 and the existing orientation `reversal`: the triple sum of Stillinger and Weber [[StillingerWeber1985]](../references.md#stillingerweber1985) and of the mW water [[Molinero2009]](../references.md#molinero2009), and the mode `UniqueCentralParticle` of the custom many-particle force of OpenMM [[Eastman2017]](../references.md#eastman2017); the far leg is not cut. The relation has no tuple set: `md.sum_tuples` and `md.gather_tuples` accept a relation without one from `md.triplets` only, without fields of tuples, and `md-differentiate` gives the forces and the virial unchanged. `!mdrt.incidence` takes its tuple set last and optionally. *Storage.* The number of triplets changes with the positions, so the triplets are not a tuple set of the topology with a size fixed for a run: `md_exec.build_triplets` reads the rows of the neighbor matrix, writes the short row of each center (the entries within $r_3$), counts $n(n-1)/2$ for each, and fills a buffer of the host `memref<?x3xi32>` of exactly that many rows, which `md_exec.build_incidence` turns into the incidence structure as for any tuples; both are freed at the end of the block. There are no empty slots, so no kernel runs on a slot that is not a triplet. *Cutoff.* The test of a leg is that of the loops over the triplets: the difference of the positions in their type, the minimum image and the square in the type of the narrowest kernel that reads the triplets, against the square of the cutoff pulled in in f32 as for pairs (D159); in mixed precision the triplets take the positions in f32 that the loops take. A leg at $a\sigma$ less a few units in the last place of f64, which f32 rounds onto the pole, is left out (`test/Integration/triplets-mixed.mlir` fails without the guard). A structure that the triplets read stays a matrix (`md-exec-choose-neighbors`). *Driver.* `[[energy.triplet]]` without a topology: an expression in `r12`, `r13` (the legs from the center, particle 1, to the ends 2 and 3), `r23`, `theta`, and `t`, with numbers of the term and the parameters of every `[[energy.type]]` by the suffix of the place (`lambda1` of the center); `cos(theta)` becomes the coordinate `cosine(0, 1, 2)`, whose derivative has no $1/\sin\theta$. Its `cutoff` is required and at most that of [energy]; the reader evaluates the expression with the ends exchanged at three points and refuses one that changes. *Not yet:* a device (refused by the reader and by `md-exec-assign-storage`; the GPU design is the survey of many-body terms, Section 5.5), a topology, the selections `center` and `ends`, the exclusion of a far leg, symmetric triplets, and bond order. *Measured* (CPU, double precision): on 64 mW particles the two-body and three-body terms agree with a direct computation to $10^{-9}$ of the largest force, and the three-body forces with OpenMM 8.6.1 Reference to $6\times10^{-15}$; 2000 steps of 2 fs change the total energy by $1.6\times10^{-5}$ of its value, $2.1\times10^{-5}$ in mixed precision. On 4096 particles a step takes 23.7 ms against 3.1 ms for the two-body term alone; not profiled, but the incidence structure is built serially and each triplet is evaluated at each of its three members (owner computes, D49).
