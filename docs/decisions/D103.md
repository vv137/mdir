# D103

Recorded 2026-10-01.

**On a device, a loop over tuples with global sums also evaluates each tuple once.** A loop over tuples that may share particles and has global sums, as the virial of the bonded terms and of the excluded pairs has, ran over the rows of the incidence structure, 16 threads a particle: on Cellulose 400 to 455 µs a term, the lanes idle where a particle has few tuples. It now takes a thread for each particle p, which evaluates the tuples p, p + n, ... (n the particles) as the loops without sums do (D84), adds to the destinations with atomics, and writes the sums of its contributions, each summed in the type it was computed in and widened once, where the loop over rows wrote that of the particle; the reduction of the particles follows unchanged, in an order that does not depend on the threads. The deterministic mode keeps the rows. On Cellulose under NPT the device takes 3387 µs a step, from 3738 (RTX 3090); the virial at the start −545023.8779 kcal/mol, from −545023.8767.
