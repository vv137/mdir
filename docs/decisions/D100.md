# D100

Recorded 2026-10-01.

**A candidate outside the range of the places of the partners of a group is not searched among its excluded pairs.** The partners of the excluded pairs of a group are sorted by place (D89); the first and the last bound them, and a candidate with pairs whose place is outside that range skips the binary search, which a third of the lanes ran, five rounds each (Nsight Compute, Cellulose). The kernel of the lists takes 1439 µs a build on Cellulose (median), from 1508, and the device 3270 µs a step, from 3293 (RTX 3090).
