# D112

Recorded 2026-10-01.

**Below double precision the waters are constrained by M-SHAKE, and `md-exec-approximate` leaves the kernels of the integrators and of the constraints as they are.** Over 2 ns of JAC NVE (2 fs, mixed precision) the total energy rose by 830 kcal/mol, linearly, the temperature by 7 K; pmemd.cuda 26 changed by 10 kcal/mol over the same run, and MDIR in double precision did not drift (0.4 ns). The neighbor structure (groups or matrix) and the fusion of D110 changed nothing. Two causes, found by turning one part to f64 at a time over 0.4 ns: (1) the pass made the f32 divisions of every loop approximate (2 ulp, D90), the inverse masses of the constraints among them, which then weighed the members of a group otherwise than the kicks do: 830 to 286 kcal/mol over 2 ns. The pass now rewrites the kernels of loops over pairs and over tuples that are not disjoint, the terms of the potential, only. (2) SETTLE [[Miyamoto1992]](../references.md#miyamoto1992) in f32: with it in f64 the drift went away, with SHAKE in f64 it did not. SETTLE computes the new positions, of the size of the water, and subtracts the old ones; the rounding of that change in f32, about 10⁻⁷ Å, divided by the step, is an error of the velocities whose square adds kinetic energy at every step, the drift of single precision in [[Jung2026]](../references.md#jung2026). M-SHAKE [[Krautler2001]](../references.md#krautler2001) on the three bonds of the water, the iterations of Newton of the groups of SHAKE generalized from a heavy atom and its hydrogens to any bonds of a group, carries the change itself, a small number, and keeps its digits: JAC changed by −1.9 kcal/mol over 2 ns at 722 ns/day (SETTLE in f64 also conserves the energy, at 581). Double precision keeps SETTLE, exact in closed form. The test of the erfc approximation now runs its kernel over pairs of particles, and `approximate.mlir` checks that loops over particles and over disjoint tuples are left as they are.
