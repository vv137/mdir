# D45

Recorded 2026-09-29.

**The log takes the temperature and the pressure from the kinetic energies at the half steps as well** (Jung, Kobayashi, and Sugita, 2018 and 2019 [[Jung2018]](../references.md#jung2018), [[Jung2019]](../references.md#jung2019)): the temperature from the mean of the kinetic energies half a step before, at, and half a step after the step, the pressure from the mean of the two half steps. The total energy keeps the kinetic energy of the step, because that sum varies least. Leapfrog and velocity Verlet write the same log. A thermostat and a barostat of M1 take these estimates; with constraints the half steps must be measured, not computed from the forces. See [driver.md](../driver.md), Section 2.3. *Amended 2026-10-02:* the couplings take the kinetic energy of the velocities they scale, without that of the center of mass (D67; dynamics.md, Section 2.4), not these estimates; with constraints the log takes the kinetic energy of the step for the temperature and the pressure as well, since the forces do not give those of the half steps. *Amended 2026-10-06 by D203:* with constraints the log measures the half steps from the velocities after the constraints of the positions, and takes the temperature and the pressure as without them; the couplings keep the kinetic energy of the velocities they scale. *Amended 2026-10-16 by D263 and D264:* the thermostats control the optimal estimate, and the pressure of the log and of the barostat is that of the groups that the barostat scales, with the kinetic energy of their centers at the half steps.
