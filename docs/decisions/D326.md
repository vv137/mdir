# D326

Recorded 2026-10-22.

**A design decision; nothing is built yet: the couplings become ops of `dyn`, written in the step program and lowered to what the driver writes today.** #541, step 1 of 4. The thermostats, the barostat, and the removal of the motion of the center of mass, which `lib/Driver/Builder.cpp` writes into `@mdir_run` as sums, host calls, and loops over particles, are to be ops of `dyn` ([dynamics.md](../dynamics.md), Section 2.2): measurements that need global sums (`dyn.kinetic`, `dyn.momentum`, `dyn.half_step_excess`, `dyn.group_kinetic`); arithmetic of the host (`dyn.pressure`, `dyn.scale_cell`, `dyn.scaling_work`, whose `count` is how the work of `TROTTER`, `TROTTER_FIRST_ORDER`, `EXACT`, and `FIRST_ORDER` is counted); draws on the host from the key of A13 (`dyn.velocity_rescaling`, stream 0; `dyn.cell_rescaling`, stream 1, isotropic, semi-isotropic, and anisotropic); `dyn.nose_hoover`, whose chain stays on the host and in the checkpoint; updates of the particles (`dyn.shift`, `dyn.scale`, `dyn.scale_positions`, and `dyn.noise`, the O step of Langevin dynamics and the noise of Brownian dynamics, stream 2 in the kernel); and the state of the host (`dyn.load_cell`, `dyn.store_cell`, `dyn.load_pressure_state`, `dyn.store_pressure_state`, `dyn.to_bath`). `dyn.program` gains `no_global_sums`, which the steps between couplings claim (D67), and its `requires` and `provides` are checked against its body; `dyn.step` takes the effects of its program instead of being `Pure`. There is no `dyn.random`: each op draws the numbers it takes. Each op lowers, by a pass after `md-inline`, to the operations that the driver writes today on the same operands and in the same order of dependence, so a run in the deterministic mode stays bitwise that of `main`, and nothing that a checkpoint holds changes. No key of the control file, default, or number changes. Steps 2 to 4 (the ops, the driver writing them, and the documents) are later PRs.
