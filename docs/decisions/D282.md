# D282

Recorded 2026-10-18.

**On a device, the reciprocal sums of PME may spread their charges through tiles of the memory of a block, one for each bin of $8^3$ points of the grid, into which the particles are binned by their first point at each evaluation, as cuFINUFFT spreads [[Shih2021]](../references.md#shih2021); `md_exec.reciprocal ... tiles(E)` states the choice, which `md-exec-choose-spreading` makes: every sum in f32 on a grid of at least 500,000 points is tiled; a smaller grid, a grid in f64, and the deterministic mode spread as before (the bricks of D108 for the B-splines of order 4 in f32). `MDIR_PME_SPREAD` overrides the choice.** Issue #336 ([pme.md](../pme.md#33-spreading-through-tiles)). *The kernels* (`scripts/generate-pme-gpu-template.py`, the window of D266 in `generate-pme-prolate-gpu-template.py`): the places of D109 for every order; a kernel zeroes the grid and the counts of the bins; a thread an entry (a particle, or an entry of the list of D262) finds its bin, the lanes of a warp with the same bin (`match.any.sync`) take their ranks from one atomic of the first of them, and the particle takes its place in a run of $C = 2\lceil n/n_\text{bins}\rceil + 8$ places of its bin or in a list of the overflow, so that nothing scans or sorts the bins; a block of 128 threads a bin reads its particles into the memory of the block by rounds, a warp a particle evaluates the $3P$ weights once and shares them by shuffles, adds the $P^3$ products to the tile of $(8 + P - 1)^3$ points with atomics of the memory of the block, and adds the nonzero points of the tile to the grid with atomics of the device; one more block adds the overflow to the grid directly. The tiles do not depend on the cell, so a triclinic cell and the sum of the dispersion (D162) take them too. *Preconditions*, checked by the pass: each edge of the grid at least $E + P - 1$ points (a point of a tile wraps once at most); the tile and a round of particles within 48 KiB; and the lowering refuses `tiles` in the deterministic mode. *What was measured first* (Cellulose, RTX 4090, kernels under Nsight Systems): binning by a count, a scan of one block, and a scatter took 46 µs a step, now 24 (16 of them the zeroing of the grid, which the bricks do not need); the tile kernel 76 µs with blocks of 256 threads, 67.6 with 128, 106.6 with 512 (bins of 16: 107.4, 86.1, 66.6), since the atomics of the memory of the block are a loop of compare-and-swap on sm_89 (`ATOMS.CAST.SPIN`) and more warps on one tile wait for each other; staging a round of particles changed nothing; the atomics of the device are not the limit (plain stores in their place: no faster). *Measured* (one job a device, `main` and this branch's `global` and `tiles8` alternated in rounds, the first dropped; the step in µs, and the spreading, binning included, from Nsight Systems; mixed precision): RTX 4090 (ada-002), order 4: JAC NVE 140.0 → 141.0 with tiles (spreading 12.1 → 12.2), the other three JAC rows +0.4 to +0.8%, so JAC keeps the bricks; Factor IX NVE 341.9 → 328.4 (41.5 → 27.7), NPT 365.6 → 350.7; Cellulose NVE 1523.8 → 1443.2 (165.0 → 95.1), NPT 1575.7 → 1500.5; STMV 4967.9 → 4687.2 (538.5 → 279.3). Orders 6 and 8 and the prolate kernel (ada-001, `global` against `tiles8`): JAC 168.2 → 156.1, 202.0 → 184.4, 183.8 → 179.6; Factor IX 443.3 → 378.4, 555.9 → 468.6, 512.7 → 444.0; Cellulose 1928.6 → 1622.6 (spreading 519 → 192), 2432.1 → 1996.5 (940 → 496), 2263.3 → 1904.1 (585 → 209); STMV 6298.1 → 5189.9, 7809.2 → 6160.4, 6485.5 → 5379.9. The prolate kernel at ε = 10⁻³ and 10⁻⁴: JAC 186.4 → 369.7 and 190.0 → 252.7 (24³ and 32³ points, 27 and 64 bins), Factor IX 480.1 → 563.6 and 478.3 → 443.9, Cellulose 2195.7 → 1766.9 and 2184.6 → 1872.2, STMV 6684.8 → 5123.9 and 6603.5 → 5370.9: too few bins leave the device idle, hence the threshold on the size of the grid, 500,000 points (about 1,000 bins): every grid at or above it spread faster with tiles on both devices; below it some steps were shorter on the RTX 4090 (Cellulose at ε = 10⁻³, 230,400 points, 0.80; Factor IX at 10⁻⁴, 0.93; JAC at orders 6 and 8, 262,144 points, 0.91 to 0.93), but JAC took 1.01 to 1.10 at orders 6 and 8 and with the prolate kernel on the RTX PRO 6000.
