# D329

Recorded 2026-10-22.

**Offer opt-in f64 storage for neural stage feature fields and their adjoints in mixed precision while preserving the f32-field default and the exported bodies' declared f32 arithmetic.** #543, part of #462. `[[energy.model]] stage_field_precision = "DOUBLE"` selects it (the maintainer's ruling on #544); `md-exec-assign-precision{mode=mixed stage-fields-f64=true}` implements it. The key is included in checkpoint fingerprints and is also exposed through `mdir.ModelTerm`. Precision follows the semantic stage field role through lowering, including the reverse sweep, rather than the ordinary pair-force role. Conversions remain at the pack/unpack boundary; the final coordinate force follows the existing mode. Single and double precision keep their existing policies. On the same 24-atom PaiNN input against SchNetPack's f64 calculator, the f32-field baseline and f64-field strategy differ from reference by 0.000823 and 0.000625 kJ/mol in energy, 0.013110 and 0.012011 kJ/mol/nm in maximum force, and 0.002051 and 0.001224 kJ/mol in virial trace. At 196,608 atoms on an RTX 4090, f32 against f64 stage storage takes 13,951.3 against 16,927.3 MiB peak and 15,389.638 against 16,206.971 ms/step; the 21.3% memory and 5.3% time costs retain the f32 default. A one-step fit probe completed at 299,997 atoms with f32 fields (21,232.4 MiB peak) but the f64 strategy ran out of device memory; it completed 262,143 atoms (22,504.8 MiB). File format and overwrite behavior do not change.
