# D43

Recorded 2026-09-29.

**A neighbor build searches a copy of the positions that is made for it**: the positions in the cell, in f32, in the order of the cells. It takes a pair that is within the cutoff plus the skin plus a margin for the rounding, so a row may hold pairs that are a little beyond. The width of the cells is chosen when the structure is built, from the density. On a device the search of a small system has one thread for each row of cells of a particle. See [neighbors.md](../neighbors.md).
