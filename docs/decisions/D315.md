# D315

Recorded 2026-10-22.

**The reciprocal sums of a function share their scratch: `md-exec-assign-storage` allocates each buffer of the scratch of `md_exec.reciprocal` once in the body of the function, and every later sum of the function that needs a buffer of the same shape takes it. The shape is the type, the formula of the length with its count (and the number of particles, for the places and the bins), and whether the buffer holds zeros between evaluations (the bricks of D108, shared only with bricks and set to zero once). No sum takes one buffer twice.** #471, step 3. *Why it is correct*: a sum writes each buffer of its scratch before it reads it within one evaluation, except the bricks, which it finds zero and leaves zero; that held before, since each site ran again on what its previous evaluation left, and no result is read from the scratch after the sum. Two sums that share a buffer must not run at once: work on one stream runs in the order it was issued; `md_exec.reciprocal` declares that it reads and writes its scratch, so two sums that share a buffer are not independent, and `md-exec-assign-streams` (D87) puts the join of a sum on the second stream before the next sum that shares its scratch, marks every sum, and is checked again by the lowering; the move of D113 now lets a sum cross another sum that moves with it. *Inventory* (from the IR): a program of dynamics has 7 sites (the start and 6 specializations of the step by what it must produce), 14 with LJPME (a Coulomb and a dispersion sum, on two grids, which share the places); at STMV ×1 (grid 224 × 224 × 240) a site held about 179 MiB: the real grid, the bricks, and the transform, 46 MiB each, the places 24 MiB, the bins 13 MiB, and the rows 4 MiB, each growing with the grid or the atoms. cuFFT's plans were already one per grid, direction, and precision for the process. *Alternatives*: a pass after `md-exec-assign-streams` that merges allocations of the same length (it would match lengths in the IR rather than know the shapes, and need its own rule for the stream); giving a sum on the second stream its own buffers (unnecessary, since the analysis of independence already orders the sums that share). *Generalizes*: to any op whose scratch is unspecified before and after it, as the descriptions of `md_exec.refresh_neighbors` and the loops over pairs state; a pass of memory planning would compute the lifetimes of every buffer. *Validation* (RTX 4090, main ba311990 and the branch in one job): the deterministic mode bitwise identical to main (log rows and final checkpoint) on JAC (2,000 steps, mixed and double; LJPME and the prolate kernel, 1,000 steps, mixed), STMV ×1 (500 steps mixed, 200 double), and the CPU (JAC mixed and double, LJPME double); on the second stream, bitwise identical to main in the deterministic mode, and in the default mode the RMS of the differences of the final positions after 100 steps of STMV is 7.5e-5 nm main against the branch, 6.8e-5 and 7.6e-5 main against main and the branch against the branch. *Measured* (`MDIR_DEVICE_MEMORY=2`, NPT 4 fs, mixed, groups, 1,000 steps, STMV from the GROMACS topology): the device peak 3,665 → 2,567 MiB (×1), 12,939 → 8,639 (×4), 22,883 → 15,367 (×7); ×10 (10.67 M atoms) runs in 20,801 MiB and ×11 (11.74 M) in 22,789 MiB, ×12 runs out of memory, so the largest STMV replica on 24 GB goes from ×7 to ×11. The Amber suite's rates within 0.5% of main (2 rounds, alternated). No control-file key or output change.
