# D137

Recorded 2026-10-03.

**With a topology, [[energy.pair]] adds a pair term given by an expression over the pairs of the topology, in its charges and its Lennard-Jones parameters, and `groups` restricts it to the pairs between two masks: the custom nonbonded force of OpenMM with its interaction groups.** The term is one more `md.sum_relation` over the neighborhood of the topology: the pairs within the cutoff that the topology does not exclude, truncated as its Lennard-Jones is (`lennard_jones_modifier`; `"POWER_FORCE_SWITCH"`, which acts on each power apart, is refused). The expression is evaluated in the units of the control file in `r` (Å), `q1` and `q2` (e), `sigma` and `epsilon` of the pair, those of the topology's table of pairs of types, set-apart pairs (NBFIX) included, `sigma1`, `epsilon1`, `sigma2`, `epsilon2` of the type of each particle with itself, `coulomb` (332.0637 kcal Å/(mol e²)), and the constants of the term; the energy is in kcal/mol. `groups = [mask, mask]`, two masks of Amber, keeps the pairs of a particle of one and a particle of the other, a pair in both once: each mask becomes a field of 0 and 1, and the kernel multiplies by $\min(a_i b_j + a_j b_i, 1)$, so the loop still runs over all pairs of the neighborhood. `mixing`, `dispersion_correction`, [[energy.type]], and [[energy.pair_override]] stay with runs without a topology, which gives the parameters. The energy of a pair must not depend on which particle comes first; the structural proof of `md-check-exchange` compares commutative ops in either order but not regrouped products, such as $(c\,q_1)\,q_2$ against $(c\,q_2)\,q_1$, so the reader evaluates the expression at three points with the two particles exchanged, refuses one whose value changes by more than $10^{-12}$ relative, and the kernel states `exchange(symmetric, asserted)` (ops.md, Section 4.7). The log prints the energy at the start under the name of the term, after the terms over tuples. On the dipeptide (`test/Driver/pair-terms.test`, and on a GPU), the Lennard-Jones written as an expression gives the term of the topology to the printed digit, 386.744746 kcal/mol cut, 400.989688 shifted, and 410.695804 with its force switched from 7 Å (the switch of the IR on the sum of the two powers is the sum of the switches of each, since its polynomial is linear in the force and its derivative at the cutoff); a term in every variable over the pairs of the dipeptide with the first 20 residues, whose groups overlap, gives −29.631865 as a sum in Python over the same pairs, and its forces agree with central differences within $10^{-6}$ of the largest. The hydrogens of water, of $\varepsilon = 0$, give no NaN in `sqrt(epsilon1*epsilon2)`, whose derivative in `r` is zero.
