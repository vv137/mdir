# D156

Recorded 2026-10-03.

**A kernel that takes a value the host computes from global sums computes it itself from the device's copy of the sums, and the host no longer waits for those sums.** The derivative of an energy that is a function of sums, such as a term over the centers of groups (D139), needs the sums before its forces: the weights of the forces are pure arithmetic of them, which the host did after a copy of the results and a wait, every step. `convert-md-exec-to-gpu` now clones each such chain into the kernels that use it, with the loads of the results reading the buffer of the device that the reduction wrote earlier in the order of the stream, and removes the copies whose values the host no longer reads; the energies that the log prints still come to the host at their steps. The work of a second stream (D87) is left as it was. On JAC with the bond over two centers of D139 (RTX 3090, mixed precision), the copies and waits of a step fall from 1.52 and 1.10 to those of the run without the term, 0.52 and 0.10, and the step from 0.233 to 0.224 ms, against 0.210 without the term and OpenMM 8.6.1's 0.209 and 0.214 (~/notes/mdir/openmm-speed); the loop of the forces of the term, whose threads now evaluate the chain, takes 6.2 µs instead of 1.9, which computing the chain once at the end of the reduction would save.
