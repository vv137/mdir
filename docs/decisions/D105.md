# D105

Recorded 2026-10-01.

**The build of the lists gathers the partners of the excluded pairs of a group a particle a lane, and sorts them with the network of the fewest values that hold them.** The warp of a group took the excluded pairs of its 16 particles one particle after another, a lane a pair, about four lanes busy, each round waiting on three loads in a chain (10% of the stalls of the kernel, Nsight Compute, Cellulose); it now takes them at once, the particle u in lane u of each half-warp, the first half its pairs at even k and the second at odd, each written after those of the particles before it (a sum by shuffles over the warp: by segments of 16, shuffles up lost pairs). The partners are sorted by a bitonic network of 64, 128, or 256 values, the fewest that hold them, not always 256. On Cellulose the kernel of the lists takes 1282 µs a build (median), from 1432, and the device 3057 µs a step, from 3100 (RTX 3090).
