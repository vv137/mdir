# D221

Recorded 2026-10-07.

**The Python model gives read-only views of a topology, copies taken when they are read, and evaluates the masks of Amber by the parser of the control file.** Issue #120. `LoadedData.topology`, `System.topology`, and `Program.topology` return an `mdir.Topology` that holds a copy of the topology without positions or velocities, so a view is not affected by later changes to its owner, and every attribute returns a new read-only array (D193) or list of strings: per particle `atom_names`, `atomic_numbers` (int64), `masses` (float64, amu), `charges` (float64, e), `particle_types`, `type_names`, and `residue_indices` (int64), the names and values of D213's `System` arrays; per residue `residue_names` and `residue_starts`; the terms of the potential as rows of particles, `bonds` $(n, 2)$, `angles` $(n, 3)$, `dihedrals` $(n, 4)$, one row for each term, with `improper_dihedrals` (bool), `harmonic_impropers` $(n, 4)$, and `virtual_sites` $(n, 4)$. Constraints depend on the settings of the `System` and are resolved when a program is compiled: `constraints` (the bonds of SHAKE, heavy atom first) and `rigid_waters` (SETTLE, oxygen first) are arrays on `Program.topology` and `None` on the views of loaded data and systems, which tells "not resolved" from "none"; `Program.topology` is the topology as compiled, without the bonds that constraints hold. `Topology.select(mask)` calls `driver::selectParticles`, the parser of `[[restraints]]`, `[[energy.external]]`, groups, parameters of particles, `couple`, and `mdir.Restraint`, and returns the int64 indices from 0 in ascending order; a mask that selects nothing gives $(0,)$, and one that does not parse an `InputError` with the control file's diagnostic. A restraint's particles are `select(mask)` with a mass. `Topology.to_openmm(cell=None)` returns an `openmm.app.Topology`, OpenMM imported only there (as D200): the names of the file, elements from the atomic numbers, the residues, the bonds without the H–H "bond" of three-site water and with those of SETTLE waters and of virtual sites to their first particle, a chain for each run of residues that no bond joins to those before it, and the cell in nm. The maintainer's decisions on PR #183: the converter rather than iteration in the style of OpenMM, and `System.residue_names` (one per particle, D213) kept beside the per-residue `Topology.residue_names` until #184 drops `System`'s per-particle arrays. Validation: the values equal parsers of the Amber, GROMACS, and CHARMM files of the tests and ParmEd 4.3.1; `select` equals the selections of the control file's path (`mdir-model-test --selections`) for restraints, external terms, and `couple`, empty selections and diagnostics included; the constraints give the degrees of freedom that the engine counts; `to_openmm` equals OpenMM's `AmberPrmtopFile` and `CharmmPsfFile` in elements, residues, bonds, and cell. No control keys, file formats, or overwrite behavior change. See [python-topology.md](../python-topology.md). *Amended 2026-10-07 (#184):* `System.residue_names` and the other per-particle arrays of D213 are removed, so the view is the one place of the topology data, and it gives `type_pairs` (D226).
