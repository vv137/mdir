**A Nosé–Hoover chain whose action the canonical factorization no longer follows stops the run: after each action, a scaling factor or a chain variable that is not finite, or a part that met $\lvert s\,v_j\rvert > 3$ ($s = w_kh/n_c$ a part of a Suzuki–Yoshida weight, $v_j = p_{\eta_j}/Q_j$), ends the run with an error before the velocities are scaled. This is a guard on the scheme of D163a, not a change of the integrator: $n_c = \lceil 50h/\tau_T\rceil$ and the order stay fixed before the run, and every run that the guard does not stop moves as before, bit for bit.** Issue #117: 125 atoms of argon in identical harmonic wells, starting at their centers, move as one oscillator. Under velocity Verlet at $\omega\Delta t = 0.5$ with the chain acting every 10 steps ($\tau_T$ = 5 ps, 150 K), the kinetic energy the chain sees swings by its whole size from one period to the next, not by $1/\sqrt{N_f}$ of it. That drives the last thermostat to 15, then 35 times its thermal size $2\pi/\tau_T$. With $n_c$ = 11, a part of a negative weight ($w_3 = w_5 = -1.178$) turns the damping $e^{-s v_{j+1}/4}$ into a growth that the factorization undoes only to its order. The chain then ran to infinity within one action at $\lvert s v_j\rvert \approx 6$, and every column of the log was NaN from step 480. A finely split chain stays bounded on the same input (with 30, 100, or 300 parts it runs 50,000 steps with $v_3$ between 43 and 46/ps), so the NaN was a failure of the factorization; the maintainer chose to stop rather than choose the parts from the chain's state, which would make the map state-dependent and lose the exact time reversibility of the fixed Trotter factorization [[Martyna1996]](../references.md#martyna1996). *The bound.* Near equilibrium $\lvert s v_j\rvert \approx 1.32 \cdot 2\pi/50 \approx 0.2$, and the mixture of `nose-hoover.test`, far from 150 K at its start, meets about 1.3 at most. On the wells, a model of the oscillator with the same 11 parts gives the potential energy within 0.015 kJ/mol of one of 1000 parts while its actions meet at most 2.9 (steps 10 to 50). Beyond 3.3 the difference reaches 0.3 kJ/mol, beyond 4 several kJ/mol, and at 6 the chain diverges. The error names the step, the largest $\lvert s v_j\rvert$, the chain's velocities and their ratio to $2\pi/\tau_T$, and the kinetic energy relative to the bath's, and it suggests a shorter `interval`, a larger `time_constant`, or another thermostat. `@mdrtNoseHooverFactor` takes the step, `(i64, f64) -> f64`. *Evidence.* The wells stop at step 60 ($\lvert s v_j\rvert$ = 3.29), where the model stops too. Their 7 rows before it match the model within 2.5e-5 kcal/mol, and they are identical to those of main, on the CPU and a GPU in mixed and double (`nose-hoover-wells.test`, `nose-hoover-wells-gpu.test`).
