# D296

Recorded 2026-10-19.

**Compaction, scans, and counting sorts are one device emitter each, `CompactionEmitter.h` (`kernels::`, beside `ScatterEmitter.h`), which the lowering calls and the templates call by name, `func.call @mdir.primitive.<name>(...)` with a declaration in the template, expanded by the emitter where the call stands when the lowering parses the template (`expandPrimitiveCalls`; the pass `expand-device-primitives` for the tests).** Issue #432, reopened with a structural scope (#415, #444; [device-primitives.md](../device-primitives.md), Section 4; [primitives-map.md](../primitives-map.md), gap 6; white paper, Section 4). *Ruled* (the maintainer on PR #460, 2026-10-19): the templates call the C++ emitters by name rather than a Python twin, one implementation for each primitive; the block scan and the stable rank built now, with #38's migration and the groups on domains as their next sites; one device scan with its two forms, each site keeping its own (the single pass is #420). *Why, with no speed to gain*: #432 measured no site at 3% of a step, each already in the cheapest form at the level of a warp; the primitives are shared for reuse (the maintainer, 2026-10-19). *The emitters*: the warp compaction (`gpu.ballot`, the rank $\mathrm{popc}(b \wedge (2^\ell - 1))$, the count), the warp scan by shuffles up [[HillisSteele1986]](../references.md#hillissteele1986) over 2 to 32 lanes, the block scan by warp scans [[CUB]](../references.md#cub), the device scan in three kernels (block sums, a scan of them, a scan within each block) and the chunked one (sums of chunks of 256, one thread, offsets), the serial scan, the scatter of a counting sort by an atomic cursor, the insertion sort of bins (by the item or by a value of it), the stable rank in bins, and the device atomic addition; their parameters that are not values are attributes of the call. *Sites moved*: the pruning (fused and in the refresh) and the active units in the lowering; the groups build's queue, entries, and entries of all images (warp compaction), its excluded partners (warp scan of 16 lanes), and its sorts by key and by grid cell and the scan of the column chunks; the matrix build's rows (orthorhombic, triclinic of one pass, of all images), its binning (chunked scan, scatter, stable rank), and the spatial order (chunked scan, scatter, insertion sort by number); the serial scan of `lowerNonzero`. Left: PME's binning (a warp-aggregated atomic, #418), `lowerBuildEdges` (a serial partition a thread), the host's scan of `emitEdgeSlots`, the warp sums (#422). No strategy attribute and no chooser: none was measured faster; a form in `md_exec` for the needs of a site (stable or not, bounded or not, the level) is proposed on PR #460 for a later step. *Validated*: the programs as executed with the PTX of every kernel byte-identical to `main`'s (a6fef8b9, and 075b9411 after a rebase) on the nine systems of the Amber suite with the pruning fused and in the refresh, JAC with the matrix, the triclinic groups of `triclinic-gpu.test`, and the listed particles of `pme-skip-zero.test` on a device (133 to 501 kernels a program); the modules after `convert-md-exec-to-gpu` differ in the place of one constant at three sites. `device-primitives-gpu.test` runs every emitter against results written out or computed on the host (also under memcheck and racecheck); `expand-primitives.mlir` and `expand-primitives-invalid.mlir` check the expansion and its refusals. *Measured*: the Amber suite with `main` and the branch alternated in one job on an RTX 4090, three rounds, step-time ratios 0.999 to 1.003, and 1.007 on JAC NVE within the spread of its runs (1259.7 to 1272.4 ns/day with `main`); no slowdown.
