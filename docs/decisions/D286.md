# D286

Recorded 2026-10-18.

**The baselines of ML4's staged models (#377, steps 1 and 2) are two pretrained checkpoints run as one whole-system call of the model term (`backend = "METATOMIC"`, D270, with MDIR's pair list, D277): nablaDFT 2.0's PaiNN of the split `train_large` (SchNetPack 2; MIT; 6 layers, 5 Å, 128 features, float32, energy in Hartree) and MACE-OFF23 medium (mace-torch; Academic Software License, academic and non-commercial; 2 layers, 5 Å, 128x0e+128x1o, float64), each pinned by an immutable URL and its SHA-256 in `scripts/mlip/fetch-ml4-artifacts.py`, with MACE-MP-0 medium (MIT) in its list as the alternative; weights and artifacts are never committed or redistributed. `scripts/mlip/painn-to-metatomic.py` and `mace-to-metatomic.py` make metatomic artifacts of them without changing a weight, and declare no `mdir::local_centers`: a message-passing atom energy depends on atoms within $L\,r_c$, so the whole system goes in one call.** *Ruled* (the maintainer on PR #436, 2026-10-18): the pair, and the ASL's terms stated where MACE-OFF23 is introduced ([ml4-baselines.md](../ml4-baselines.md), Section 1). *Why*: the only public PaiNN with conservative forces and weights found (no water-trained PaiNN exists; training one needs a ruling, D167), and a MACE with water in its training and $\ell = 1$ features, which step 5 needs; the `AOTI` backend takes the `nequip` package's convention only (D273). *Validated* against the packages' calculators (`MACECalculator`, SchNetPack's `SpkCalculator`) on five water boxes of 24 to 768 atoms, RTX 4090 and RTX PRO 6000, `double` and `mixed`: MACE to $5.4 \times 10^{-12}$ kJ/mol/nm in the forces in `double` ($1.2 \times 10^{-4}$ in `mixed`, the f32 forces), PaiNN within $1.3 \times 10^{-5}$ of the largest force (float32 sums in another order). *Measured* (liquid water, peak of the plugin's allocator): MACE-OFF23 164 kB per pair (float64), PaiNN 61 kB, each within 3% of the package's own calculator; the largest whole systems 1,536 and 6,144 atoms (MACE, PaiNN) on the RTX 4090 and 6,144 and 24,576 on the RTX PRO 6000, where the package fails at the same sizes; 11.5 to 12.3 and 0.50 µs per pair on the RTX 4090, the package's speed or faster. `water-box.py` rounds the cell of its reference to the $10^{-7}$ nm of the `.gro` file, as it rounded the positions.
