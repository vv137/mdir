# D288

Recorded 2026-10-19.

**A neural stage is a symbol of the module, `md.stage @s forward(@f) vjp(@b) : (...) -> (...)`, a pure function of one element (one directed pair, one particle) whose bodies, its forward and its vector-Jacobian product, are functions that the symbols name; `md.apply_stage @s(...)`, or `md.apply_stage @s vjp(...)`, applies it in a kernel of `md.gather_relation`, `md.map_particles`, or `md.sum_particles`, and `md-differentiate` carries the adjoints of the features back through the stages by their products, as rules of the structure: the adjoint of a gather over a relation is a gather over the same relation that evaluates the product from both ends of each pair, and of a sum or a map a map.** #377, step 3 ([ml4-ir.md](../ml4-ir.md)). *Ruled* (the maintainer on PR #441, 2026-10-19): the declaration and the application as proposed; and the derivative of a stage gather emits **one** `md.gather_relation` of several results, the adjoints of the fields it reads, the forces $\mathbf K(m,b) = -[(\bar r_{mb} + \bar r_{bm})\mathbf d/r + \bar{\mathbf d}_{mb} - \bar{\mathbf d}_{bm}]$, and half of $\mathbf d\otimes\mathbf K$ at each center for the virial, two evaluations of the product per directed pair where three separate ops took six; the separate form stays as the reference (`md-differentiate{separate-stages=true}`), and the tests run both. The two-ended scatter (one evaluation, over the privatized scatter of D285 and #391) and store against recompute are step 4. *The IR*: a stage's operands are `f64`, vectors of it, or integers (structural, no cotangent); the form of a body is the form of the function that the symbol names, a `func.func` of `arith`/`math`/`vector` in the module now (inlined into the kernels by `md-inline`), StableHLO with a batch dimension and an AOTInductor declaration in step 4; `DerivativeOpInterface` gains a reverse method, `emitAdjoint`, which the application implements by its product, and its forward rule fails: no JVP is bound and MDIR writes no neural derivative (D167). A *stage kernel* yields the application's value with arguments of the kernel or constants as operands; `md-differentiate` checks it and refuses any other kernel that applies a stage, a field of several components through a kernel that is not a stage kernel, a stage without a product, and a second derivative. `md.gather_relation` may give several fields from one kernel (with `exchange(none)`), lowered to one `md_exec.pair_for`. *Validated* on a synthetic two-layer PaiNN-like model (`test/Integration/Inputs/synthetic_mp.py`, 16 components, two species, 10 particles in a periodic cell): the adjoint identity of each stage, MDIR's forward rules on the forward body against the bound product, to $1.3 \times 10^{-15}$ relative (tolerance $10^{-12}$), and a product scaled by 1.001 fails; the energy, forces, and virial against one reverse sweep of the whole energy in the positions and a strain to $1.8 \times 10^{-15}$ relative on the CPU and $5.2 \times 10^{-16}$ on an RTX 4090 in double, both forms of the derivative, $1.3 \times 10^{-5}$ in mixed; forces and six strains against central differences of MDIR's energy (D184).
