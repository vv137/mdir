# D266

Recorded 2026-10-16.

**Particle mesh Ewald may take the prolate spheroidal wave function of order zero (PSWF) as its splitting and spreading kernel, "Ewald summation with prolates" (ESP), as an option beside the Gaussian splitting with B-splines, which stays the default** [[Liang2026]](../references.md#liang2026). The split is $1/r = (1 - \Phi(r/r_c))/r + \Phi(r/r_c)/r$ with $\Phi(y) = \int_0^y\chi$, $\chi = \psi_0^c/C_0$, $C_0 = \int_0^1\psi_0^c$; the spectral factor is $\hat\chi(2\pi r_c\lvert\mathbf m\rvert)$ with $\hat\chi(k) = \lambda_0\psi_0^c(k/c)/(2C_0)$ for $k \le c$, normalized to $\hat\chi(0) = 1$ (the factor $\tfrac12$ that the paper's code applies); the spreading is $\phi_1(x) = \psi_0^{c_1}(2x/(Ph))$ on $P$ = 5 to 8 points with $B = \prod_a\lvert\hat\phi_1(2\pi k_a/K_a)\rvert^{-2}$. $\psi_0^c$ is a series of Legendre polynomials from the eigenvector of the tridiagonal matrix of the prolate operator [[Xiao2001]](../references.md#xiao2001), normalized to $\int_{-1}^1\psi^2 = 1$; $c$ solves $\psi_0^c(1) = \varepsilon$, which gives the paper's Table 2 to its digits (9.5392, 12.024, 14.471 at $10^{-3}$, $10^{-4}$, $10^{-5}$). *Ruled* (the maintainer, 2026-10-15): a selectable option, on the CPU and a GPU. *Measured* (issue #334, prototype kernels in `scripts/benchmarks/prolate-ewald`, f32 grid, RTX 4090 and RTX PRO 6000): at the same relative force error $\Delta$ (eq. 16 of the paper, against an Ewald sum agreeing with itself to $2\times10^{-12}$), ESP takes 1.6 to 2.7 times as long as the Gaussian of order 4 in `md.reciprocal` at $\Delta \le 10^{-3}$ and $3\times10^{-4}$, the accuracy of MDIR's default ($\Delta = 3.3\times10^{-4}$) and near that of the suite ($1.1$ to $1.8\times10^{-4}$); 0.83 to 1.46 times at $10^{-4}$; and 0.37 to 0.61 times the best Gaussian (order 6 on the prototype's bricks) at $10^{-5}$ on Cellulose and STMV, 0.91 to 1.03 times on JAC. The spreading of $P^3$ points into a grid 8 to 25 times smaller is what costs: global atomics contend, and tiles in block memory (#336) halve it on large systems. *Ruled* (the maintainer on PR #337, 2026-10-15): `[pme] kernel = "PROLATE"` (default `"GAUSSIAN"`), `bandwidth` (c), `window_bandwidth` (c₁, default c(ε/2) as the paper's code), `tolerance` shared as the tail of the kernel at the cutoff (the documents say that the same number gives very different force errors), default ε = 10⁻⁵, the grid by eq. 15 raised until the aliasing estimate of eq. 10 relative to the sum it perturbs is at most ε, default order 6, `order` and `grid` given explicitly; `beta` and `influence` refused with it, `bandwidth` and `window_bandwidth` without it; LJ-PME Gaussian and combinable; the deterministic mode implemented; the CPU in the same change; Python `System.pme_kernel`, `pme_bandwidth`, `pme_window_bandwidth`. *Implemented*: ψ₀ᶜ on the host (`Prolate.cpp`) against the eigenfunction of the integral operator in mpmath to 1–4×10⁻¹⁶; the pair terms through tables of 1 − Φ and Φ/x (to 10⁻¹⁴ in f64, 4×10⁻¹⁰ in f32); the window as Chebyshev series in the fraction of the place; on a device (`PMEGPUProlate.mlir`, generated) the coefficients in the type of the grid in a buffer term by term, each weight evaluated once a particle by 3P lanes and shared by shuffles, the spreading into the bricks of D108 or in fixed point, the gathering a warp a particle, the influence as the dispersion's (D104, D162) with χ̂; orthorhombic and triclinic cells. *Validated*: three OPC waters against the Ewald sum over the wave vectors to 1.9×10⁻⁶ at the default and to the printed precision at 10⁻⁷; the virial's trace and its diagonal against finite differences of the energy under strain to 2.2×10⁻⁶ and 6.6×10⁻⁶; a rhombic dodecahedron against the Gaussian kernel at a tight setting to 1.8×10⁻⁷ and 2.5×10⁻¹⁰ at 10⁻⁹; a GPU in double equal to the CPU to the log's digits; the deterministic mode to the bit; JAC's relative force error 1.2×10⁻⁵ at the default against 1.3×10⁻³ for the Gaussian default (over all forces, against a reference itself off by 1.9×10⁻⁶; superseded below). *Ruled* (the maintainer on PR #337, 2026-10-15, after the measurement): $\Delta$ is stated over the Coulomb forces of the topology (its exclusions and 1-4 scaling included), against the Gaussian at erfc 10⁻¹³, 0.33 Å, order 8, which agrees with the prolate kernel at 10⁻⁹ to 1.3×10⁻⁷; the grid is the smallest at one spacing for the three edges whose eq. 10 estimate is at most ε/2, since that estimate lies 2.5 to 3 times *below* $\Delta$ near its floor of 0.6 to 0.9 ε and the bound ε left $P$ = 5 and 7 at 2.7 to 2.9 times that floor; $P = 6$ stays the default. *Implemented*: that grid (JAC 50³, Cellulose 210×98×96, STMV 180³ at the default; $\Delta$ = 1.04 and 1.21×10⁻⁵, every ε from 10⁻³ to 10⁻⁵ and $P$ from 5 to 8 within 1.3 times its floor, $\Delta \le 1.7\,ε$ at 10⁻⁶), refused with the estimate and what to do where twice the grid of eq. 15 leaves it above ε/2 (8 points at 10⁻⁹); and the window less its value at the edge of its support, $\psi_0^{c_1}(2d/P) - \psi_0^{c_1}(1)$, MDIR's own, so that the energy of the mesh is continuous when a particle crosses a plane of the grid (the paper's window drifted JAC in NVE by 75 kcal/mol/ns at 10⁻³, now 5.1), at a cost of 2% of $\Delta$ at the default and 12% at 10⁻³. *Validated* as well: $\partial H/\partial\lambda$ of [free_energy] and the derivative in the charges against central differences (2.9×10⁻⁵ kcal/mol; 7.6×10⁻¹¹ relative in double); the nine components of $\partial U/\partial\boldsymbol\varepsilon$ in a sheared cell to 1.5×10⁻⁸ in double, the off-diagonal virial included; JAC's drift over 2 ns of NVE −0.06 kcal/mol/ns (halves 0.87, 0.29) against −0.01 (0.13, 0.86) for the Gaussian default, its NPT volume 234,301 ± 113 Å³ against 234,470 ± 101; mixed precision adds about 10⁻⁴ to $\Delta$ with either kernel. *Measured* (MDIR's whole step, mixed precision, RTX 4090 and RTX PRO 6000, JAC, Cellulose, STMV): 1.2 to 1.4 times the Gaussian's time at the accuracy of MDIR's default, 1.04 to 1.2 times order 6 at $\Delta \approx 10^{-4}$, 0.85 to 1.07 times the faster of orders 6 and 8 at $10^{-5}$, with 6 to 7 times fewer grid points than order 6 there (10 to 12 at 10⁻⁴), what a distributed transform moves;
