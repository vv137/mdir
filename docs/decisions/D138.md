# D138

Recorded 2026-10-03.

**[[energy.function]] gives a function of one argument by its values at evenly spaced points, which every expression of the control file calls by its name: the continuous tabulated function of OpenMM.** A function has `name`, `min`, `max`, and `values` (at least two), the first at `min` and the last at `max`; between them a natural cubic spline, zero outside, as OpenMM's `Continuous1DFunction`; with `periodic = true`, a periodic spline, whose first and last values must be equal (at least four), and the argument taken modulo `max - min`, as for a dihedral. The functions are read before the terms, so a term may call one that the file gives after it; a name of the syntax (`sin`, `select`) or of another function is an error, and so is a call with other than one argument. The spline is solved once on the host (the tridiagonal system of the second derivatives, for a periodic one the cyclic system by the formula of Sherman and Morrison) and kept as the cubic of each interval in the place $t \in [0, 1]$ in it, a table of four numbers an interval; the kernel takes $u = (x - \min)/h$, the interval $\lfloor u\rfloor$ clamped to the table in floating point before it becomes an index, $t = u - \lfloor u\rfloor$, four lookups, and Horner's rule, and a selection gives zero outside the range. Only $t$ carries the derivative of D2: the floor, the index, the lookups, and the comparisons have none. The argument is in the units of the expression, Å, radians, or $r/\sigma$ as the user writes it, and the values in those of the energy, kcal/mol. A function of the distance that holds the floor stays in the kernel in mixed precision, in f32, and is not tabulated again (D94, amended). On the dipeptide (`test/Driver/tabulated-functions.test`, and on a GPU): a pair term over two groups in a function of $r$ that ends at 7 Å, before the cutoff, gives 14.826807 kcal/mol as a sum in Python over a spline solved there on its own as a dense system, and a dihedral term in a periodic function, called a turn beyond its range at a dihedral within $2\times10^{-6}$ of π, gives 4.100384; the forces agree with central differences within $10^{-6}$ of the largest, across the end of the periodic function as well; in mixed precision 14.826813 and 4.100383. mixture.toml with its Lennard-Jones as a function of $r/\sigma$ at 381 points, a term without a topology, keeps its energy and virial at the start to the printed digit, −497.7298 and −1117.7782 kcal/mol, and its conservation. Discrete functions and functions of two and three arguments, and a file of values for long tables, are not yet read.
