# D295

Recorded 2026-10-19.

**The ops of `md` with a kernel declare the transpose of their structure through `AdjointOpInterface`, and `md-differentiate` takes it from them instead of building the op itself; a check, `--md-check-transposes`, refuses an op of the structure of a potential without one.** #451, part of #415 (the primitives map, D292, Section 1.3). A sum or gather over a relation or tuples, or a sum or map over particles, is a gather, a map (its kernel), and a reduction; the gather and the reduction are linear and the kernel is not, so what the op declares is the transpose of its structure: `isKernelOperand` and `createTranspose`, which creates the op of the transpose (a gather over the same relation, with the kernel copied, or evaluated from both ends of each pair with the adjoint read at both; a gather over the same tuples; a map over the same operands) and returns its kernel, which the pass completes with the reverse derivative of the source kernel by the scalar rules of D183. The exchange contract of the new op is the caller's (antisymmetric for forces, none for the adjoint of a field), and the virial's form of a two-ended gather is the same op summed over the pairs. The complete transposes of linear ops (`isLinearOperand`, `emitTranspose`, D276) keep their form, now with default implementations; `transposeTransfer` takes the ops of `md_dist` only. The tangent copies of a sum (the virial and the derivative in a parameter) are not transposes and stay in the pass. `md_exec.scatter_add` (D291) declares its transpose in the destination, the identity; that in the values, a gather by the indices, is refused until `md_exec` has the op (the ruling of 2026-10-19 on #459, option b). `md.gather_tuples` and the kernel ops of `md_dist` have none yet: the check names them; those of `md_dist` follow in the next PR (ruling, option b). Behavior-neutral (the ruling of 2026-10-19): no control-file key, Python API, output, or generated code changes. *Validation* (CPU, `double`): the output of `mdir-opt --md-differentiate` (also with `separate-stages`, with the passes of the semantic level, and after `md-distribute` on the directed, owner-computes, and unique traversals) for every test that differentiates, 195 outputs, and `mdir emit --stage=module` and `--stage=lowered` of the control files of 11 driver tests (Amber, ff19SB, OPC with virtual sites, CMAP, generalized Born and its models, SETTLE, PME, centroid terms, analytic bonds, virtual sites) and of the 4 examples, as written and on the CPU and GPU targets, 384 outputs (180 successful, the rest the inputs' own diagnostics), identical to `main`; the adjoint identity of each pair through `md-differentiate`, $U(q) = \langle q, \partial U/\partial q\rangle$ for a field in which the op is linear and $\delta U[\mathbf v] = -\langle \mathbf F, \mathbf v\rangle$ for the positions with $\delta U$ written by hand: `sum_relation` within $2.2\times10^{-15}$ and $2.2\times10^{-16}$, `gather_relation` $1.3\times10^{-16}$, `map_particles` $2.3\times10^{-15}$, `sum_tuples` 0 and 0, `sum_particles` $1.4\times10^{-16}$ and 0 (relative; tolerance $10^{-12}$), and a one-ended force, not a transpose, off by 0.68.
