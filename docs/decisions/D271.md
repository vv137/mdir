# D271

Recorded 2026-10-16.

**Particle mesh Ewald on the host runs in parallel loops of the compiled code, and its result is the same to the bit for any number of threads, in every mode (#359, step 2 of D267).** The spreading takes a particle an iteration and adds its 64 values (order 4) to the fixed-point grid (D70) with integer atomics, which a program on one thread replaces by plain additions (`atomic-grid=false`), the same integers; the grid is zeroed and converted in parallel, and a point wraps around the grid by a subtraction, not a division. The FFT stays pocketfft's [[Reinecke2019]](../references.md#reinecke2019), in `runtime/mdrt_fft.c`, called for blocks of lines from parallel loops over the lines of each of its three passes (a plane of rows along $z$; tiles of 8 columns of $z$ along $y$ and $x$, copied to contiguous lines), each line transformed in f64 by the same code whatever thread takes it. FFTW, the other FFT library on the machine, is GPL-2.0-or-later and was not linked into the MIT runtime. The product with the influence function is a parallel loop over the rows of the grid, the Gaussian influence of a rectangular cell from the factors of its edges as on a device (D104); its energy and virial are summed along a row in the type of the grid and over the rows in f64 in the fixed order of D171, and a step that writes neither calls `pme_scale`, which only multiplies, as on a device. In the mixed mode the B-splines, the grid and its transform in memory, the factors of the edges, the product, and the sums of the gathering are in f32, where the mixed mode of a device takes f32; the places stay in f64, with the fraction rounded once for the B-splines, so that the mixed modes of the host and a device differ in this step, and pocketfft computes each line in f64 (the maintainer's rulings on PR #366). The double mode stays in f64. *Measured* (EPYC 9354, 16 cores of a Slurm job, mixed precision, alternated rounds, the first dropped; the parts from the sampler of D267): on TIP3P with 309,876 sites, 253.9 ± 0.5 ms a step on `main` after D269 against 138.7 ± 0.2 (1.83 times), PME from about 131 ms (spreading 48, FFT 53, splines 10, the serial product and the rest 20) to 13.8 (spreading 6.6, FFT 3.5, gathering 2.1, B-splines 1.3, product 0.3) against GROMACS's 7.2; on JAC 16.95 ± 0.05 against 9.26 ± 0.02 (1.83 times), PME from 9.1 to 1.3 ms. On one thread JAC takes 125.2 against 128.8 ms. From 1 to 16 threads the step of TIP3P 103k now gains 14.8 times (2071.5 to 139.8 ms, with #363's O3), against 8.0 before #363; JAC 13.3. The spreading is bound by the atomics, about 20 cycles each, and on JAC gains little beyond 4 threads. *Results:* in double precision the potential energy, virial, and forces at step 0 agree with `main` to 3e-14, 8e-14, and 8e-17 (relative, RMS of the forces); in mixed precision the branch differs from `main`'s double by what `main`'s mixed did (forces 1.9e-5 relative RMS on JAC, 9.8e-6 on TIP3P); the energy, virial, and forces at step 0 are the same to the bit with 1, 2, 4, 7, and 16 threads on both systems, and the state after 50 steps under NPT with 1, 4, and 7 threads, in both modes (`pme-threads.test`).
