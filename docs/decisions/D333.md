# D333

Recorded 2026-10-22.

**Use a matched, reproducible NVE CPU baseline before selecting host primitive strategies** (#552, under #549). On JAC, TIP3P 103k, and Cellulose, compare MDIR mixed and double at one and 16 threads with GROMACS 2026.3 mixed at one and 16 threads (default and exact 3 Å list) and serial pmemd 26 double, respectively, on one 16-core node class. The protocol fixes 2 fs, PME order 4, $\mathrm{erfc}(\beta r_c)=10^{-5}$, the cutoff and grid of each system, and the input hashes; it alternates engines for four rounds, discards round zero, and reports paired ratios from the other three. The measured MDIR executable reports commit `89535e1f` with no uncommitted-change suffix; it differs from main `a8a5f7ff` only in the CPU benchmark README and runner, not compiled MDIR source. GROMACS NVE keeps `nstlist=10`, with `rlist` 0.805, 0.901, and 0.807 nm on JAC, TIP3P, and Cellulose, respectively; this nearly bufferless list is distinct from D267's thermostat-enabled default. The paper and checked-in JSONL records give the full conditions and engine-specific step counts. JAC and Cellulose starting positions match to `.gro` rounding; TIP3P cross-engine rates are contextual because its starting states differ. The measured MDIR mixed/GROMACS NVE-default time ratios are 12.34, 11.00, and 11.35 at one thread, and 7.35, 7.79, and 7.97 at 16 threads, respectively; MDIR double/serial pmemd ratios are 6.92, 7.11, and 6.68. The JAC double/pmemd estimate includes an outlying third round: paired ratios 6.757, 6.745, and 7.270, with the first two about 6.75. This records a baseline and changes no kernel, numerical method, control key, format, or default.
