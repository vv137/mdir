# D200

Recorded 2026-10-06.

**Every setter of the Python model whose value has a unit also takes an `openmm.unit.Quantity`, converted at the boundary to the unit the setter declares; outputs stay plain floats and NumPy arrays.** Issue #95; the maintainer chose inputs only. A quantity is recognized by duck typing (`value_in_unit_system`), so OpenMM is no dependency and is imported only when a quantity is seen, and converted with its `value_in_unit` to the declared unit: nm (cutoffs, `pme_spacing`, `minimize_step`, positions, restraint reference, cell), 1/nm (`pme_alpha`), nm/ps (velocities), ps (`timestep`, `tau_t`, `tau_p`), K (temperatures, also of `draw_velocities`), bar and 1/bar (`pressure`, `compressibility`), kJ/mol/nm² (`Restraint.force_constant`), and s for the wall-clock `Simulation.part_seconds`. These are the units of `md_unit_system` but for pressure, where that system gives $10^{-25}$ for 1 bar. A plain number keeps its meaning in the public units of D191. A wrong dimension raises `InputError` naming the setter and the unit, with OpenMM's message. A quantity whose magnitude is a sequence (a list of `Vec3`) becomes a float64 array; every magnitude then passes D193's checks, and a plain list stays refused. Values with no declared unit (`pme_tolerance`, counts, seeds, the constants and per-tuple parameters of custom expressions, whose units the expression gives) take plain values only. A quantity is recognized before D193's DLPack probe, to which it forwards: before, a NumPy-backed quantity was stored with its unit dropped. *Evidence.* Thirteen scalar setters with a quantity in Å, fs, 1/Å, kcal/mol/Å², atm, and 1/Pa equal the plain values within 1 ulp (the rounding of the factor; e.g. 9.5 Å gives 0.9500000000000001 nm, 1 atm gives 1.01325 bar exactly); the dipeptide's positions (1168 particles) in Å as `Vec3` and NumPy, velocities in Å/fs, reference and cell in Å, within 1 ulp of each element; a model of quantities compiles to the IR of the plain model on CPU and GPU targets in mixed and double; and with `openmm` hidden the module imports and its plain setters work. See [python-units.md](../python-units.md).
