# D247

Recorded 2026-10-11.

**The Python model selects the coupling and the work of the barostat with the keys of the control file: `Ensemble.barostat_coupling` (`BarostatCoupling.Isotropic`, the default, `SemiIsotropic`, `Anisotropic`) and `Ensemble.barostat_work` (`BarostatWork.Trotter`, the default, `TrotterFirstOrder`, `Exact`, `FirstOrder`), with the refusal of `mdir check`; each runs to the bit of `mdir run`.** Issue #275 ([python-segments.md](../python-segments.md#the-coupling-and-the-work-of-the-barostat-d247)). `mdir.Ensemble` had no key for `[barostat] coupling` (D119, D163c) and `work` (D77, D92), so a Python simulation ran isotropic stochastic cell rescaling with the default work alone; `mdir run` takes the others, in a triclinic cell as well (D127). The model hands the builder the control structure of the control file: the fields set what the reader of `[barostat]` sets. With the couplings come their other keys (the maintainer on #270): `Ensemble.compressibility` takes three numbers for the anisotropic coupling, and `Ensemble.compressibility_z`, `surface_tension`, and `surfaces` are those of the semi-isotropic one, in the units of the Python model, 1/bar and bar nm (1 dyn/cm is 10 bar nm), refused without their coupling with the words of the control file. `FirstOrder` under the semi-isotropic or the anisotropic coupling is an `InputError` with the words of the control file; a work of Trotter type with a coupling period of 1 stays refused by a simulation, and the exact and the first-order work, which close a period with one step, run with it. `Program.plan["barostat"]` has the two values; the fingerprint has `[barostat] coupling` and `[barostat] work` in the group `coupling`, so a checkpoint continues in either front end with the same keys and is refused with others. *Validation* (`python-barostat*.test`), in the deterministic mode: the 10 cases of a coupling and a work that it takes, in an orthorhombic cell (the dipeptide in water, SHAKE and SETTLE) and in a triclinic one (403 waters in the rhombic dodecahedron), on the CPU and a GPU, in double and in mixed precision, 80 runs: the positions, the velocities, the forces, and the cell with its tilts equal those of `mdir run` to the bit, the energy file and the DCD trajectory byte for byte, in parts, from a checkpoint of `mdir run`, and into `mdir run --continue`; the axes strain together, x and y together, or each by its own; and with a coupling period of 1 the exact and the first-order work give the state of `mdir run` to the bit. The other keys run to the bit of `mdir run` as well: three compressibilities with one of them 0, whose axis has a strain of exactly 0, and a compressibility of z of 0 with a surface tension on one surface. | Decided
