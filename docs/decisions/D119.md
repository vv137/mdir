# D119

Recorded 2026-10-02.

**With `coupling = "SEMI_ISOTROPIC"` the barostat scales x and y together by the strain of the area and z by that of the height, each from its own pressure and its own noise (eqs. 9a and 9b of [[Bernetti2020]](../references.md#bernetti2020)); the strain, the virials, and the kinetic energy are kept axis by axis.** The pressure of axis $a$ is $P_{aa} = c\,(2K_a + W_{aa} + C/(3V))/V$, with $K_a$ the kinetic energy along $a$ without the center of mass and $\mathsf W$ the virial of the step with that of the constraints, as the trace was (D72). One step of Euler and Maruyama over the period, in $\varepsilon_{xy} = \ln A$ and $\varepsilon_z = \ln L_z$: $\Delta\varepsilon_{xy} = -\tfrac{2f}{3}\big(P_0 - \gamma/L_z - \tfrac12(P_{xx} + P_{yy})\big) + \sqrt{4k_BTcf/(3V)}\,R_0$ and $\Delta\varepsilon_z = -\tfrac{f_z}{3}(P_0 - P_{zz}) + \sqrt{2k_BTcf_z/(3V)}\,R_1$, with $f = \beta N_P\Delta t/\tau_P$, $f_z$ the same with `compressibility_z` ($\beta$ by default), $R_0$ and $R_1$ the first two normal numbers of stream 1 of the step (the first is the one isotropic coupling draws), and $\gamma$ the tension `surface_tension` (dyn/cm, $1\,\text{dyn/cm} = 10$ bar nm) times `surfaces` (2 by default, the two faces of a bilayer). The scales are $\mu = (e^{\Delta\varepsilon_{xy}/2}, e^{\Delta\varepsilon_{xy}/2}, e^{\Delta\varepsilon_z})$. Without tension the two sum to the step in $\ln V$ of eq. (5); the stationary density of the pair is $A L_z\,e^{-(P_0V - \gamma A + F)/k_BT}$, the ensemble at constant normal pressure and tension. The noise depends on the volume here, unlike the step in $\lambda = \sqrt V$ of isotropic coupling (D72): the two agree to first order in the period. With `compressibility_z = 0` the height is kept to the bit and the area takes the change of the volume. The positions scale by $\mu_a$ along each axis (each rigid group with its center of mass), the velocities by $1/\mu_a$, and the reference positions of restraints by the edges over those of the file, axis by axis. The work of a scaling is a sum over the axes: of Trotter type $\sum_a(\mu_a^{-2} - 1)K_a - \sum_a\ln\mu_a\cdot\tfrac12(\mathcal W_{b,aa} + \mathcal W_{a,aa})$, with $\mathcal W_{aa}$ the element $a$ of the virial of the groups (D116), $\sum_g X_{g,a}F_{g,a}$, which is $-\partial U/\partial\ln\mu_a$, and $C/(3V)$; exactly, the energy of the scaled positions with $\sum_a(\mu_a^{-2} - 1)\alpha^2K_a$. The count to first order takes the trace of the virial of the step with twice the internal kinetic energy, an identity of the trace that holds for each axis only in the mean, and is rejected with semi-isotropic coupling. With a period of one step the state kept on the host and in checkpoints is the three diagonals (the virial, that of the groups, the kinetic energy), nine numbers; a checkpoint with another number has the state evaluated once. Isotropic coupling takes the same code with $\mu$ the same on the three axes. `barostat-semi.test`: the cell of the mixture of `barostat.test` stays square in x and y; with `compressibility_z = 0` its height is 2.32 nm after 2000 steps, as in the file; counted exactly, the conserved energy changes by $10^{-5}$ of its value; 50 dyn/cm on each surface widens the area. **Measured** on 1039 OPC waters (2 ns each from the same state, 300 K, 1 atm, $\tau_P$ = 2 ps, a period of 25 steps, GPU, mixed; `scripts/validation/barostat-semi/water.py`), where a liquid must sample the same volume however its cell is coupled: density $0.99682 \pm 0.00045$, $0.99664 \pm 0.00044$, and $0.99667 \pm 0.00054$ g/cm³ (isotropic, semi-isotropic, height kept), and the compressibility from the fluctuations of the volume $5.11 \pm 0.40$, $4.66 \pm 0.37$, and $4.78 \pm 0.46 \times 10^{-5}$/bar; the conserved energy changed by $2.9$, $2.0$, and $1.7\times10^{-3}$ of its value. **Measured** on the POPC bilayer of `examples/popc-bilayer` (126 lipids of Lipid21 [[Dickson2022]](../references.md#dickson2022), 4932 TIP3P waters, 303 K, 1 bar, $\tau_P$ = 5 ps; `scripts/validation/barostat-semi/membrane.py`) against GROMACS 2026.3 with its semi-isotropic C-rescale on the same model (the terms within $10^{-6}$ once the pairs 1-4 of Lipid21, scaled by 1/2 or 1/6, were written with their own parameters, which ParmEd's single `fudgeLJ` could not hold): over 20 ns each from one state, the area per lipid $66.70 \pm 0.63$ Å² against $63.62 \pm 0.37$, the height 71.48 against 74.75 Å, and the volume $300.25 \pm 0.03$ against $299.53 \pm 0.03$ nm³ (densities 1.0211 and 1.0235 g/cm³). The area is correlated over 3 to 4 ns, so each run holds four to seven independent samples, and GROMACS's own area ran from 62.3 to 65.7 Å² in blocks of 2.5 ns. In one cell over 1 ns (the barostat held by a compressibility of $10^{-12}$), the difference of the lateral and normal pressures, which sets the area, is $-5.4 \pm 3.7$ bar in MDIR and $-5.4 \pm 4.4$ in GROMACS: the split by axis agrees. The pressure itself is $+16.9$ against $-25.7$ bar, which accounts for the volumes. The diagonal of the virial of the forces, which the log now gives at the start, agrees with GROMACS's to $10^{-5}$ on the same positions (in double precision on the host and in mixed precision with groups), and the kinetic parts agree (69,909 degrees of freedom in both), so the difference is in the dynamics of the constraints: GROMACS's pressure in that cell is $-25.7$ bar with LINCS of order 4 (its default), $-17.2$ with order 8 and two iterations, and $-3.8$ with the bonds of hydrogen flexible at 1 fs, while MDIR's volume is the same with them flexible ($300.22 \pm 0.07$ nm³). The rest is the step: on 2972 TIP3P waters in one cell (`scripts/validation/barostat-semi`, 0.5 ns a point, 303 K, the same molecules in the same cell in each program), the pressure at 2, 1, 0.5, and 0.25 fs is 52.7, 53.4, 41.7, and 45.1 bar in MDIR (velocity Verlet and RATTLE), 34.4, 32.8, 43.3, and 46.9 in GROMACS (leapfrog), and 61.6, 50.0, 49.1, and 43.8 in pmemd.cuda (leapfrog), each $\pm 3$ to 4: the three agree as the step goes to zero, near 45 bar, and at 2 fs the estimators of the pressure of their integrators, with the forces of the rigid waters in them, err by $+7$, $-11$, and $+16$ bar. The density of the bilayer differs from GROMACS's by that, not by the coupling. The ensemble test of two pressures [[Shirts2013]](../references.md#shirts2013) on the 1039 OPC waters (1 and 300 atm, 5 ns each) gives the slope of $\ln P_2(V)/P_1(V)$ within 0.08 standard errors of $-\beta\Delta P$ with semi-isotropic coupling and within 0.36 with the height held (`scripts/validation/ensembles/run.py --semi-only`). The end of a run with semi-isotropic coupling reports the mean pressures of x and y and of z that the barostat took (`mdrtGetSemiPressures`).
