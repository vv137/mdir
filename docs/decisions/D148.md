# D148

Recorded 2026-10-03.

**`[[energy.external]]` adds a term of the absolute positions of single particles, given by an expression in `x`, `y`, `z` in Å, the charge `q` in e, the time `t` in ps, and parameters, over the particles of a mask of Amber (`selection`) or of their numbers (`particles`), as the custom external force of OpenMM [[Eastman2017]](../references.md#eastman2017).** A parameter is a number for every particle or a list of one for each, in the order of the particles. The potential sums the expression over the particles with `md.sum_particles`, which reads the positions themselves and a flag of each particle, 1 for those of the term, on which the kernel selects; differentiation turns such a sum into the forces $-\nabla k(\mathbf x_i)$, a map over the particles whose kernel takes the gradient a component at a time along its unit vector. The positions are never wrapped (D74), so the term is continuous along a trajectory; without a periodic cell (D142) they are those of the file. Such a term has no virial: its forces do not come from displacements between particles, and $\sum_i \mathbf x_i \otimes \mathbf F_i$ depends on the origin and on the images, so the pressure leaves it out and a run at constant pressure refuses it. On the dipeptide, a flat-bottomed wall in $z$ on three residues, a uniform field on the water, and harmonic wells of their own for three particles give the energies and the forces of Python to $10^{-9}$ of the largest force on the CPU and on a GPU in double precision, and within $10^{-6}$ in mixed precision, where the kernel takes the positions in f32 (absolute positions of 100 Å to $8\times10^{-6}$ Å); the virial does not change. Two argon atoms in harmonic traps whose centers move at 30 Å/ps take the positions of velocity Verlet in Python to the last bit, on the CPU and on a GPU in double precision. Walls, fields, and restraints of any shape are such terms; the restraints of positions keep their own table (D74), whose references follow a barostat. *Amended by D154: the virial is the derivative of the energy under the scaling of the barostat, and `scaling` says whether the term is fixed in space or in the frame of the cell; a barostat takes it.*
