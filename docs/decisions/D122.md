# D122

Recorded 2026-10-02.

**MDIR reads the files of CHARMM: a PSF of the XPLOR kind, its CRD, and the files of topology, parameters, and streams that give its parameters** (`format = "CHARMM"`, `parameters`; the cell from `[boundary] box`). The rules that the documentation of CHARMM 51b1 leaves open were settled against CHARMM's energies: a set of dihedral terms is the lines of the same types that follow one another, and a later set replaces it whole; impropers try A X X D, X B C D, X B C X, X X C D after the four types, in either direction, and are periodic for a multiplicity other than 0; the pairs exactly three bonds apart in the graph of the bonds take their own Lennard-Jones and NBFIX's 1-4 values and the Coulomb term times E14FAC; a stream file stops at its first `return`. MDIR keeps the Coulomb constant of CODATA, 2.38 × 10⁻⁵ below CHARMM's. On Trp-cage in CHARMM36m with 3900 TIP3P, the Lennard-Jones is CHARMM's to 5 × 10⁻¹⁰ and every bonded term to its printed digits, against 2.2 × 10⁻⁷ through a topology of GROMACS, whose ε has six digits; two POPC of CHARMM36 agree as well, and a synthetic system with wildcards, NBFIX, and CMAP is `charmm.test`. A PSF with types as numbers, lone pairs, and the Drude model are refused ([charmm.md](../charmm.md), Section 4).
