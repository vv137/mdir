# D162

Recorded 2026-10-03.

**Particle mesh Ewald for the dispersion: `lennard_jones = "PME"` sums $-c_ic_j/r^6$ over all pairs and images on a grid of its own, with $c_i = 2\sqrt{\varepsilon_i}\,\sigma_i^3$ of the type of each particle with itself, and the direct terms give each pair within the cutoff its own Lennard-Jones.** The sum is that of Essmann et al. for $1/r^6$ [[Essmann1995]](../references.md#essmann1995): the share $g(\beta r) = e^{-\beta^2r^2}(1 + \beta^2r^2 + \beta^4r^4/2)$ stays in the direct terms, the rest goes to a reciprocal sum with the influence function $-\pi^{3/2}\beta^3/(3V)\,F(\pi\lvert\mathbf m\rvert/\beta)\,B(\mathbf m)$, $F(b) = (1-2b^2)e^{-b^2} + 2\sqrt\pi b^3\mathrm{erfc}(b)$, the term $\mathbf m = 0$ included, and its virial $E_m\delta_{ab} - \tfrac12 w\,\lvert G\rvert^2 B\,6(\pi^2/\beta^2)(e^{-b^2} - \sqrt\pi b\mathrm{erfc} b)\,m_am_b$; the self term is $\beta^6/12\sum_i c_i^2$ and the excluded pairs take $c_ic_j(1-g)/r^6$ back, from its series below $\beta^2r^2 = 1$, where the closed form cancels. The direct terms add $c_ic_j(1-g(\beta r))/r^6$ to the pair's own Lennard-Jones, so that a pair of the Lorentz–Berthelot rule or set apart (NBFIX) has its own potential within the cutoff and the geometric $C_6$ beyond it, as Wennberg et al. propose and OpenMM and GROMACS do [[Wennberg2013]](../references.md#wennberg2013). `md.reciprocal` takes a unit attribute `dispersion`, and the templates of the host and the device (scripts/generate-pme-gpu-template.py) an influence function of their own; the spreading and the gathering are those of the charges, with the coefficients. Keys: `lennard_jones = "CUTOFF"` (default) or `"PME"` in [energy], with `lennard_jones_modifier` `"NONE"` or `"POTENTIAL_SHIFT"`, which shifts the whole direct term; a switch is refused, since the grid would not hold what it takes away. `dispersion_correction` is off with it, and `"ENERGY_PRESSURE"` given explicitly is an error, so nothing is counted twice. `[lj_pme]` has `tolerance` $= g(\beta r_c)$ (default $10^{-3}$, as GROMACS's `ewald-rtol-lj`), `beta`, `grid`, `max_spacing` (1.2 Å), and `order` (4, 6, 8). The log adds "Lennard-Jones excluded", "Lennard-Jones reciprocal", and "Lennard-Jones self". Validation: a pure-Python Ewald sum of 22 particles of three types with an NBFIX pair (test/Driver/lj-pme.test) to the printed digits in every term; the dipeptide in water against OpenMM 8.6.1 (Reference, order 5): Lennard-Jones 379.054457 against 379.054454 kcal/mol, the change of the forces from a plain cutoff to 3e-5 of its rms; Trp-cage in CHARMM36m with NBFIX (12,017 particles) against a rerun of GROMACS 2026.3 (single precision): 7657.5315 against 7657.5287 kcal/mol, the reciprocal −1721.250 against −1721.24; the virial of three OPC waters against $-dU/d\lambda$ to 2e-7; on the device, double precision gives the CPU's log to its digits, mixed precision the forces to 5e-5 of their rms, and both sums run on a second stream (D87) to the same state. JAC (23,558 particles, RTX 3090, mixed): 0.210 → 0.270 ms a step with the default grid of 54³, where OpenMM goes 0.210 → 0.285. Task #59.
