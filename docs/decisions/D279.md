# D279

Recorded 2026-10-17.

**The derivative of a potential on domains goes through the fields it computes from the positions: `md-differentiate` keeps the adjoint of each such field on domains, carries it back through maps, sums over particles, $G$, and $G^\mathsf T$ by their rules, and a gather of a density gives its forces through a copy of the density's adjoint to the ghosts, of exactly the version computed in the derivative, and a two-ended gather of a kernel antisymmetric by construction.** Stage 1 of M4, part 3 (#31); design in [distributed-ml2.md](../distributed-ml2.md), Section 15. For EAM [[Daw1984]](../references.md#daw1984), $\bar\rho = F'(\rho)$ is copied to the ghosts between the two passes, and the force of a pair is $-(\bar\rho_i f'(i,k) + \bar\rho_k f'(k,i))\mathbf d/r$, the density's kernel inlined from each end; its ghost contributions return by $G^\mathsf T$ with those of the pair sums, and `md-dist-owner-computes` applies on the directed traversal. A pair sum that reads a field computed from the positions takes its adjoint by the gather at the owners on the directed traversal, a checked optimization (coverage, V4; a symmetric kernel, V8; proof in Section 15.4); on the unique traversal it is refused. No control-file key, Python API, or output changes. *Validation* (CPU, `double`): an EAM potential of the analytic form of Finnis and Sinclair [[Finnis1984]](../references.md#finnis1984), 64 particles, on grids of 1, 2, 3x2 (uneven), and 16 domains (half empty), unique, directed, and directed with owner-computes (12 runs): against one domain, energy within $1.8\times10^{-15}$, force components within $3.1\times10^{-15}$, virial components within $4.4\times10^{-16}$ (relative; tolerance $10^{-10}$); one domain against a Python reference of the closed-form forces within $3.6\times10^{-15}$; six force components and the three stretches of the cell against central differences of the distributed energy (step $10^{-5}$) within $2.3\times10^{-9}$ and $6.0\times10^{-11}$ (tolerance $10^{-7}$); the adjoint identity $\langle\mathbf F, \mathbf v\rangle = -\langle\mathbf c, J\mathbf v\rangle$ of $U = \langle\mathbf c, \rho\rangle$ against a direct sum within $6.9\times10^{-15}$ (tolerance $10^{-12}$); the many-body energy of D276 on the directed traversal (6 runs) within $2.1\times10^{-15}$ of one domain; negative tests of a stale and of a missing copy of $F'(\rho)$, and of the refusals.
