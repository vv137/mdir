**The spreading of the force on a virtual site can be derived as the transpose of its placement: with `MDIR_SITE_SPREAD=derived` the builder writes, for each set of sites, a potential $\sum_s \mathbf f_s\cdot\mathbf p_s$ of the force on each site against its place from its owner, and `md-differentiate` gives its forces through the declared transpose of `md.sum_tuples` (D295) and the reverse derivative of the placement; `written`, the transpose written out (`emitSpreadSites`), stays the default.** #453, part of #415 (the primitives map, Section 3.4). A site is $\mathbf x_s = \mathbf x_o + \mathbf p(\mathbf d_j, \mathbf d_k)$; the force $\mathbf f$ on it moves to the atoms by $J^\mathsf T\mathbf f$, the gradient of $\sum_s \mathbf f_s\cdot\mathbf x_s$ at a fixed $\mathbf f$: the owner takes $\mathbf f$ whole (the transpose of the translation, written as a gather over the sites) and the atoms the forces of the potential, negated. The placement's kernel is one function that `emitPlaceSites` and the potential share. The virial changes by $\sum_k(\mathbf x_k - \mathbf x_s)\otimes\mathbf F_k = -\mathsf W_{\mathbf p} - \sum_s \mathbf p_s\otimes\mathbf f_s$, with $\mathsf W_{\mathbf p}$ the virial of the potential, derived for every set (zero to the rounding for a linear site, which the written form leaves out). The switch is an environment variable, named by the maintainer; another value than `derived` or `written` is refused. The default stays `written`: on 16,329 OPC waters with alanine dipeptide (65,338 particles, PME, SETTLE, 2 fs, `mixed`, one RTX 4090 job, `--idle`, two repeats in turn), NVE ran at 621.9 and 638.2 ns/day written against 635.6 and 633.9 derived (no difference beyond the noise), and NPT with stochastic cell rescaling, which needs the virial, at 549.1 and 549.9 against 546.1 and 543.1 (derived 0.9% slower). No control-file key, Python API, or output changes; with `written` the programs are those of `main` (the `mdir emit` of #459's driver inputs identical). *Validation*: the two forms against each other, the forces of a checkpoint after a step relative to the largest force and the energies, the virial among them, of every step: three OPC waters with Amber's extra point $8.7\times10^{-17}$, the linear sites of `gromacs-sites.test` $8.9\times10^{-17}$, alanine dipeptide in 387 OPC waters $2.3\times10^{-16}$ on the CPU in `double`, $1.1\times10^{-16}$ on a GPU in `double`, and $9.1\times10^{-8}$ in `mixed`; the rows of the log equal to their printed digits; the trace of the virial of the three waters against $-dU/d\lambda$ by central differences (`check_virial.py`) within $6.1\times10^{-6}$ in both forms (tolerance $2\times10^{-5}$); `virtual-sites-spread.test` runs both forms for 50 steps (forces within $1.8\times10^{-16}$, the columns of the energy file to their digits) and the refusal of another value.
