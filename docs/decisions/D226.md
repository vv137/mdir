# D226

Recorded 2026-10-07.

**A Python model may declare the Lennard-Jones table tunable by pairs of types, `mdir.Tunable(name, "sigma_pair")` and `"epsilon_pair"`, whose sites are the unordered pairs of types $(a,b)$, $a \le b$, in the order of the flat upper triangle, $s(a,b) = aT - a(a-1)/2 + b - a$, listed by `System.type_pairs`; a pair that a map takes has its entry's value after the combining rule of per-type tunables.** Issue #160, a follow-up of D213 (maintainer's decision Q2 on PR #159). Each pair is one site, so the gradient has the structure of the table and no entry twice, and a value sets both $\sigma_{ab}$ and $\sigma_{ba}$. `map` and `values` are those of D213 ($-1$ keeps the model's value); values are finite and at least 0. Pair and per-type tunables may be declared together, and the pair tunable wins for the pairs its map takes: the rule builds the table from the types' values (NBFIX pairs keeping theirs) and the pair tunables then set the pairs their maps take; a diagonal pair $(a,a)$ that a pair tunable takes does not redefine the per-type $\sigma_a$, $\epsilon_a$ that the rule uses; compile warns only of NBFIX pairs that still keep a value of their own (maintainer's decision on PR #191, Q1). Maps over pairs are the ordinary map built from `System.type_pairs`, with no map by names of types (Q2). The 1-4 pairs keep their own parameters; tunable 1-4 parameters would be a separate item (Q3). An NBFIX also sets the 1-4 Lennard-Jones of its pair of types in all three formats (CHARMM: its 1-4 values, or the same ones; GROMACS with `gen-pairs`: generated after `[ nonbond_params ]` and scaled by `fudgeLJ`, unless `[ pairtypes ]` gives the pair; Amber: the same table divided by `SCNB_SCALE_FACTOR`), so a pair tunable moves only its interactions beyond the 1-4 pairs: a fit of ion NBFIXes is unaffected, one of an NBFIX between types that meet as 1-4 pairs keeps their 1-4 values. No kernel changes: the table is a buffer of the program (D213), and $\langle C_6\rangle$, its shift estimate, and the classes and tails of pair terms (D209) are rebuilt on the host from it. *Evidence.* On propane and water (`Inputs/gromacs`, an NBFIX of CT-OW), an update at step 6 that doubles $\epsilon$ of CT-HC off the rule and gives OW-HW Lennard-Jones where the model has none, then 10 steps, equals a compile with the new values from that state to the bit on the CPU and a GPU, double and mixed (deterministic); its change of the energy, $-208.013$ kJ/mol, equals a NumPy sum over the table to $3.0\times10^{-12}$ kJ/mol; with per-type and pair tunables together, $1.0\times10^{-11}$ of $135.9$; the correction for the dispersion at the new pair values equals its formula to $7.4\times10^{-13}$ relative. See [python-tunable.md](../python-tunable.md#the-table-by-pairs-of-types). *Amended 2026-10-07 (#184):* the pairs are listed by `Topology.type_pairs` (D221), with the other data of the types; `System.type_pairs` was removed before any release.
