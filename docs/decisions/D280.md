# D280

Recorded 2026-10-17.

**The direct sum of the prolate kernel in a loop over pairs is a function of $r^2$ that a table takes as it takes the Gaussian's (D94): $f q_i q_j (1 - \Phi(x))/r = f q_i q_j (1 - x) \bar\chi(x)/r$, with $\bar\chi(x) = (1 - \Phi(x))/(1 - x)$, the mean of $\chi$ over $[x, 1]$, a Chebyshev series in $2x - 1$, $x = r/r_c$ (`md.chebyshev`, a new op of the md dialect whose derivative is the series of the derivatives), which `md-exec-simplify-distance` takes into a function of $r^2$ and `md-exec-expand-radial` makes a table of cubics in f32, under `fast_math`, in kernels of f32.** Issue #380. The series holds $\bar\chi$ to $10^{-14}$ of its largest term (20 to 27 terms from $\varepsilon = 10^{-3}$ to $10^{-9}$, `prolate::getFarSeries`, by Gauss–Legendre over $[x, 1]$), and the table follows from it by the fit and the test of D94. The factor $1 - x$ keeps the relative accuracy of the function near the cutoff, where $1 - \Phi$ is of the order of $\psi(1)(1 - x)$: written as $1/r - (\Phi(x)/x)/r_c$, a polynomial in $x^2$, the same function cancels there to below the noise of f64, and its energy failed the test of D94 at 12 bits. The cells of `pme_far` (D266) stay where a table does not serve: kernels of f64 (the double mode, where the function would be evaluated as itself, and the cells take less), the pairs of [free_energy] whose distance is held away from 0 by a maximum, the excluded pairs (loops over tuples), and `MDIR_PROLATE_CELLS=1`, the switch for comparing the two (principle 3). A series that no table takes becomes Clenshaw's recurrence [[Clenshaw1955]](../references.md#clenshaw1955) in its kernel. The coefficients are an array of `FloatAttr`, which prints each to the bits it reads back (a dense array of f64 prints six digits). *Measured* (mixed precision, a cutoff of 9 Å, JAC, TIP3P 34k and 103k, Cellulose at $\varepsilon$ = 10⁻³, 10⁻⁴, 10⁻⁵, alternated against `main` in one job a system and tier; before the change the loop over pairs took 1.02 to 1.12 times the Gaussian's on one RTX 4090): the loop over pairs takes 0.98 to 1.02 times the Gaussian's on the CPU (16 cores of a Slurm job; 1.51 to 1.83 before) and 0.99 to 1.02 times on the RTX 4090; the prolate default's step on the CPU at 16 threads is 0.68 to 0.75 of `main`'s, and 1.10 to 1.24, 1.05 to 1.10, and 0.96 to 1.03 times the fastest Gaussian's at the three tiers (1.61 to 1.74, 1.46 to 1.59, 1.28 to 1.47 before; at 1 thread 1.12 to 1.15, 1.06 to 1.09, 1.04 to 1.09); on the GPU the step falls by 1 to 4%. The tables at 9 Å: the force's 7, 7, 8 bits (20 to 40 KB, the Gaussian's 7), the energy's 8, 9, 9, the cubics in f32 within 1.1 to 2.3 × 10⁻⁷ of the function. The compile time grows by up to 0.5 s. *Validated*: the forces of the table and of the cells differ by 3.4 to 4.1 × 10⁻⁷ of the RMS Coulomb force on JAC and TIP3P 34k at every tier, CPU and GPU, and $\Delta$ in mixed precision is unchanged to three digits; the Gaussian, the prolate kernel in double precision, and `MDIR_PROLATE_CELLS=1` give `main`'s module byte for byte on both targets and its lowered program on the CPU; the series against the quadrature to 2 × 10⁻¹⁴ and its derivative to 2 × 10⁻¹¹ (`prolate-function.test`); the forces with the switch both ways on the CPU and a GPU (`prolate-series.test`, `-gpu`) ([pme.md](../pme.md#7-the-prolate-kernel-d266)).
