# D76

Recorded 2026-09-30.

**Leapfrog takes the steps of velocity Verlet and differs in what it stores**: the velocities half a step behind the positions. It carries the forces of the positions and evaluates once per step, at its end, as velocity Verlet does. Its step with energies begins from the velocities of the time of the positions, `P(v_{n−½} + h f_n/m)`, so that the virial of the constraints has the meaning it has with velocity Verlet, and returns the velocities of the time of the new positions for the kinetic energy. The constraints bring both integrators to the same positions, so their logs agree. The barostat couples the velocities of the time of the positions and stores them half a kick behind, which counts the energy it gives exactly; the thermostat alone scales the stored velocities ([dynamics.md](../dynamics.md), Section 6). *Amended 2026-10-12 (#285):* the constraints bring a kick of a whole step and the half kick from the projected velocities to the same positions and velocities only while the velocities do not change within the drift. Under Langevin dynamics (D135) and in the step that scales the cell (D92), with constraints, every program of a leapfrog step therefore begins from `P(v_{n−½} + h f_n/m)`, the plain step too, which had kicked a whole step and left the stored velocities off by $(1 - c)/2$, or $(1/\mu - 1)/2$, of the part of the kick along the constraints. Elsewhere the step with energies leaves the state of the plain step to the bit, and takes the drift from `P(v_{n−½} + h f_n/m)` on the side, for the virial of the constraints alone.
