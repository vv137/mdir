# D276

Recorded 2026-10-17.

**A global potential runs on domains through the compiler: `md-distribute` rewrites its body into `md_dist` before `md-differentiate`, which carries the adjoints of the transfers back by transposes declared through an op interface (the transpose of $G$ is $G^\mathsf T$, of $G^\mathsf T$ is $G$), and the directed traversal is an optimization of the derivative's form, `md-dist-owner-computes`, with a switch.** Stage 1 of M4, part 2 (#31); design in [distributed-ml2.md](../distributed-ml2.md), Section 14. `md-distribute{grid, traversal}` gives a potential a decomposition, a layout, the owned rows of its fields, one halo, a copy to the ghosts of every field read at neighbors, and the pair ops on the chosen traversal; it refuses, with the reason, exclusions, tuples, reciprocal sums, terms of the absolute positions, and contracts it cannot prove. `AdjointOpInterface` declares the transposes of `halo_copy`, `halo_sum`, `distribute`, `collect`, and `all_reduce` (each domain's part takes the cotangent once); layouts, halos, and relations are inactive (piecewise constant). The derivative of a pair sum is a gather with partial results at owned and at ghost rows, completed by `halo_sum`; over a directed relation each pair adds half at each particle, a new form of `gather_pairs`, which `md-dist-owner-computes` replaces by the gather at the owners after checking, with the verifier's code, that the relation is directed, covered (V4), and of a proven contract (V8). `md_dist.map_particles` is added. The proof of exchange contracts moves to the library of `md`. No control-file key, Python API, or output changes. Not yet: the derivative through fields computed from the positions (EAM, the next part) and in parameters. *Validation* (CPU, `double`): Lennard-Jones, 64 particles, derived through `md-distribute` and `md-differentiate`, on grids of 1, 2, 3x2, and 16 domains, unique, directed, and directed with owner-computes: against one domain, energy within $3.8\times10^{-16}$, force components within $3.0\times10^{-15}$, virial within $3.0\times10^{-16}$ (relative; tolerance $10^{-10}$); against the hand-written program of D275 within $1.0\times10^{-15}$; six force components and the three stretches of the cell against central differences of the distributed energy within $2.1\times10^{-9}$ and $1.4\times10^{-10}$ (tolerance $10^{-7}$); the adjoint identity $\langle\mathbf c, \mathbf F\rangle = -\langle G\mathbf c, G\mathbf c\rangle$ through the derived rules of $G$ and $G^\mathsf T$ within $2.4\times10^{-15}$ (tolerance $10^{-12}$); a many-body energy (a gather, a map, a sum over particles, and a field read at neighbors) on three grids and both traversals within $2.4\times10^{-16}$ of one domain; negative tests of the pass, the derivative, and the new forms of the dialect.
