# D243

Recorded 2026-10-10.

**A frame evaluator made with `terms="dependent"` evaluates the terms that a tunable enters alone, $U_\text{dep}$ of $U = U_\text{fixed} + U_\text{dep}(\boldsymbol\theta)$, in a second program that it compiles from the model of the program it is given; its energy is named `dependent_energy`, and `energy` and `virial` raise in that mode.** Issue #264. A reweighting takes $U_{\boldsymbol\theta}(S_n) - U_{\hat{\boldsymbol\theta}}(S_n)$ and the derivative in $\boldsymbol\theta$, in which the fixed terms cancel. *Which terms.* A constant of a pair term keeps that term with its tail and shift estimate; a parameter of a tuple term that term; $\sigma$ or $\epsilon$ the Lennard-Jones of all pairs with the correction for the dispersion; the charges the electrostatics whole, since the reciprocal sum is one quadratic form of all the charges (`Control::dependentTerms`, `getDependentTerms`; `Program.plan["terms"]`). The bonded terms, the 1-4 Lennard-Jones, the terms of the positions, and, without tunable charges, PME with its mesh are left out, with what the host adds for them; the observed columns of terms left out are absent and listed in `unavailable`. *A second program, not a branch*: `@tunable` is 6% to 11% of an evaluation, the rest is the start of an activation with the forces of the whole potential. *Measured* (one RTX 3090, mixed precision, ms per frame, the whole potential against the dependent terms): the dipeptide in water, a constant of a pair term 1.04 against 0.51, the charges 1.13 against 0.78, a pair $\sigma$ 1.07 against 0.58; JAC, 7.44 against 2.97, 7.68 against 4.12, 7.27 against 3.23. *Accuracy.* In double precision the differences of the two modes agree within $1.5\times10^{-11}$ kJ/mol and the Jacobians to the bit. The mode saves time and does not change the accuracy in mixed precision: each term is accumulated in f64 from f32 contributions, so the fixed terms cancel exactly in either mode and the error of a difference (up to 0.09 kJ/mol for the charges of 23,558 particles) is that of the contributions of the dependent terms. *Without frames.* Where the potential is linear in its tunables (a pair term in $C_{12}$ and $C_6$, a prefactor), the sums that `observe` records at each frame give the differences, the weights, and the derivative of a reweighted average of the evaluator on the stored frames within $2\times10^{-13}$ kJ/mol and $10^{-14}$; it is a shortcut beside stored frames, since such a run can be reweighted in those tunables and no others. The six questions of PR #266 were decided as recommended. *Next:* the fixed cost of a frame, 6.2 of 6.99 ms for the whole potential and 2.4 of 2.72 ms for the dependent terms on JAC, is the start of an activation (copies and host work), which the frames inside one activation would leave out.
