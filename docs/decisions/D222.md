# D222

Recorded 2026-10-07.

**A Python pair term takes `dispersion`, the control file's `dispersion_correction` of a pair term with a topology: Python's `None` (the default) follows the system, `DispersionCorrection.None_` leaves the term out of the correction for the dispersion, `EnergyPressure` asks for its tail; and setting `System.dispersion` makes the system's correction explicit, as giving the key of `[energy]`, with `None` restoring the default.** Issue #161, left open by D209. The model records whether the correction was given (`System.dispersion_given`) and hands the builder the control structure of the control file, `topologyDispersionGiven` and each term's `dispersionGiven` included, so a pair term whose tail diverges or that reads `t` is an `InputError` naming it when the system or the term asks for the correction, and is left out with the warning `pair_tail_left_out` (now a `UserWarning` of `mdir.compile`) under the default; a term's opt-out is silent. As in the control file, a term that asks for its tail while the system's correction is off is refused, the default is off without a periodic cell, and an explicit correction with a switch is refused; with a switch the default correction is off and `mdir.compile` warns `dispersion_switched` (the maintainer's choice on PR #187: the control file refuses it, but the default Python `System` has a switch and must still compile). The builder's messages about a tail name `PairTerm.dispersion`, and a value of another type is a `TypeError`. `Program.plan["dispersion"]` gives the correction, whether it was given, and for each pair term whether its tail is in it. A tunable (D213) of a term left out may take any value. No numbers change: on the mixture of `pair-dispersion.test` with $-c_8/r^8 - ae^{-r/l}/r^4$, under a plain cutoff and the shift, on the CPU and the GPU in double and mixed precision, the potential, the virial and the pressure equal those of `mdir run` to its printed digits for the default, an explicit correction, the term's request and opt-out, and the correction off, and the default less the opt-out is the term's tail against Simpson's rule in $\ln r$: −0.033825540 kcal/mol (−0.088177850 with the estimate of the shift), virial −0.270926137 kcal/mol, pressure −0.191478708 bar, within 4e-13 relative (2e-12 for the pressure). The derivatives of the tails in $\boldsymbol\theta$ stay with the M2b evaluator (D213). See [python-model.md](../python-model.md#the-correction-for-the-dispersion).
