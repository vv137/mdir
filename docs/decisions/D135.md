# D135

Recorded 2026-10-03.

**Langevin dynamics by the middle scheme is the second thermostat: `method = "LANGEVIN"` of `[thermostat]` with `friction` in 1/ps.** The friction and the noise act on the velocities between the two halves of the drift of every step, $\mathbf v \leftarrow c\mathbf v + \sqrt{(1-c^2)k_BT/m}\,\mathbf R$ with $c = e^{-\gamma\Delta t}$ ([[Zhang2019]](../references.md#zhang2019); BAOAB, [[Leimkuhler2013]](../references.md#leimkuhler2013)), before the constraints of the positions, in every program of a step, and before the scaling of `step_trotter`. The normal numbers come from Philox in the kernel: one block for each particle and step, keyed by the seed, the step (a counter on the host that each step advances from the first step of the run), the number of the particle in the input, and stream 2 (A13), turned into three normal numbers by Box and Muller; the generator in IR agrees with that of the host to the last bit, on the CPU and on a GPU. The momentum is not kept, so the degrees of freedom are $3N - N_c$ unless `center_of_mass_interval` removes the motion; `interval` paces that removal and the barostat; the log has no conserved energy, since the run does not count the energy of the bath under Langevin dynamics. `time_constant` belongs to `V-RESCALE` and `friction` to `LANGEVIN`; either in the other is an error. `mdir checkpoint --print` writes a field of each particle, which the tests read. *Amended 2026-10-03:* with constraints, the virial of their forces is that of the forces that keep the bonds at their lengths at the end of the step, solved from its positions, velocities, and forces, $\mathbf e\cdot(\mathbf a_q - \mathbf a_p) = -\lvert\mathbf v_q - \mathbf v_p\rvert^2/d$, not the mean of the changes that SHAKE and RATTLE make within the step, which hold the friction: those changes are smaller by $\gamma\Delta t/2$, and on 1039 OPC waters at 2 fs the pressure at one volume rose by 39 bar per 1/ps of friction (80.8, 116.4, 269.2 bar at 0.2, 1, and 5/ps against 77.5 with velocity rescaling), the potential energy staying the same; Langevin dynamics with cell rescaling then gave a density 0.15% low. With the forces of the constraints at the end of the step: 100.8 ± 7.0, 77.2 ± 5.3, and 82.6 ± 6.4 bar. *Amended 2026-10-16 by D265:* with constraints the step is g-BAOAB, which projects the velocities after each part and constrains the positions after each drift of half a step; the friction and the noise act between the two drifts as before, and the log reports the temperature of $K_\text{half}$, from the half steps of the two drifts.
