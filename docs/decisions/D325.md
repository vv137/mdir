The deterministic final checkpoints of both tests are bitwise identical to main; the lowered GPU programs of Gaussian order-4 mixed, Gaussian order-8 double, and prolate order-6 mixed are byte-identical to main. Whole-step timing varied across concurrent jobs and showed no consistent change; the byte-identical GPU programs establish the same kernel work.
