# D163a

Recorded 2026-10-03.

**A Nosé–Hoover chain is the deterministic thermostat: `method = "NOSE-HOOVER"` of `[thermostat]`, with `time_constant` $\tau_T$ in ps and `chain_length` $M$ (3 by default), acting at the end of each period of coupling as velocity rescaling does (D67).** The chain of [[Martyna1992]](../references.md#martyna1992) with $Q_1 = N_fk_BT_0/\omega^2$ and $Q_j = k_BT_0/\omega^2$, $\omega = 2\pi/\tau_T$, so that the kinetic energy exchanges with the first thermostat with the period $\tau_T/\sqrt2$. Its action over the period $h = N_T\Delta t$ is the two halves of consecutive periods of the symmetric Trotter splitting joined, factorized by the Suzuki–Yoshida weights of order 6 [[Martyna1996]](../references.md#martyna1996) in $n_c = \lceil 50h/\tau_T\rceil$ equal parts (a chain they no longer follow stops the run, D206); with one part, the mixture of `thermostat.test` at $h$ = 40 fs and $\tau_T$ = 0.5 ps, far from 150 K at the start, drove the later thermostats beyond what the factorization follows and the run failed, at 2, 4, and 8 parts it ran. The chain is $2M$ numbers on the host, which `mdrtNoseHooverFactor` moves from the kinetic energy without the center of mass and which returns the factor of the velocities, so the programs of the steps are those of velocity rescaling with another call; the bath takes the change of the energy of the chain, $\sum_j p_{\eta_j}^2/2Q_j + N_fk_BT_0\eta_1 + k_BT_0\sum_{j\ge2}\eta_j$, instead of the change of the kinetic energy, so the log's conserved energy is the invariant $H'$ of the chain less its value at the start. The checkpoints keep the chain (`/parameters/mdir/thermostat_state`), and a run that continues ends in the state of one that does not stop. `friction` with it, and `chain_length` with another method, are errors. Validated on the mixture of 256 particles at 150 K ($N_f = 765$, $\tau_T$ = 1 ps, $N_T = 10$, $\Delta t$ = 4 fs, 400 ps after 80 ps): the mean kinetic energy $0.9997\,\bar K$, its standard deviation 1.017 times the canonical $\sqrt{N_f/2}\,k_BT_0$, the samples those of $\Gamma(N_f/2, k_BT_0)$ by Kolmogorov–Smirnov ($p = 0.11$, every 20th of 8001), and $H'$ drifting by $1.9 \times 10^{-4}\,k_BT_0$ per particle per ns; with the barostat of `barostat.test` over 80 ps the conserved energy changed by $2.5 \times 10^{-4}$ of its value; on a GPU in mixed precision as on the CPU (`nose-hoover.test`, `nose-hoover-gpu.test`). A `time_constant` below 20 periods of coupling, $\tau_T < 20N_T\Delta t$, is warned of (`short_thermostat_period`, D158), the ratio below which GROMACS's grompp warns for its Nosé–Hoover thermostat: the chain acting once a period follows a shorter period poorly even where the parts keep it stable.
