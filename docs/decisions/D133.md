# D133

Recorded 2026-10-02.

**The reader of Amber takes the format before Amber 7 and the convention of a negative periodicity, and the suite runs from its own files.** A topology without `%FLAG` lines is the format before Amber 7: a title, 30 pointers in lines of 12, and the sections in a fixed order, of lengths that the pointers give (a section of no items is a blank line), then the solvent pointers, the molecules, and the box when IFBOX is set; it lacks NUMEXTRA, SCEE/SCNB (1.2 and 2.0), and atomic numbers (from the masses). In both formats the reader takes the conventions of pmemd 26, read from its source for compatibility (driver.md, Section 2.10): a dihedral type of negative periodicity continues to the next type, so that the dihedral has the term of each type up to the first of positive periodicity and one 1-4 pair with the factors of that last type; a type of periodicity 0 carries no 1-4 pair; a phase within 1e-3 of ±π is ±π and a cosine or sine of a phase below 1e-6 is 0 (JAC's phases are 3.141594: dihedrals 965.2152 kcal/mol, as pmemd's, against 965.2154); and without extra points the exclusions are those of the file. Factor IX of the suite has 2283 such terms. `bench.py` had written its topology again with ParmEd, which kept the signs but merged the types (75 to 54), and MDIR and GROMACS had read only the first terms: dihedrals of 1338.6 kcal/mol at the start against 2259.2 for pmemd from the file of the suite, so the rates of Factor IX up to D132 are of another model. MDIR and pmemd now read the files of the suite; ParmEd writes only the topology of GROMACS, with the terms made explicit. `scripts/validation/suite` compares the terms at the start and the forces of all nine systems with pmemd 26 on the CPU (and JAC with sander): every term within 3.4e-8 relative, the forces within 4.0e-7 of the rms force, rms, on grids of even sizes (on odd sizes pmemd's aliasing factor takes another frequency at one index, pme.md, Section 1.1).
