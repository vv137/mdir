# D171

Recorded 2026-10-03.

**On the CPU every reduction of a parallel loop is added over a fixed partition of its iterations into chunks, each folded in order, and the chunks folded in their order; the result does not depend on the threads or their number, in every mode.** `fixed-order-reductions` rewrites each one-dimensional `scf.parallel` with results before `convert-scf-to-openmp`: at most 256 chunks of contiguous iterations, chunk $c$ of $p$ holding $[cn/p, (c+1)n/p)$, a partial result for each in a buffer at the entry of the function (D117), and a serial fold of the initial value with the partial results. Cause: the OpenMP runtime combined the partial sums of the threads in the order in which they finished, so the kinetic energy and the virial differed in their last bits from run to run; with a thermostat or a barostat the coupling carried the difference into the positions. On the mixture of 256 particles, 4 threads, double precision, 200 steps of 4 fs: anisotropic C-rescale with V-rescale and a Nosé–Hoover chain with leapfrog and with velocity Verlet differed run to run in 111 to 398 position values (up to 1.3e-15), against one thread in 121 to 396, and across a checkpoint in 108 to 357, even with `deterministic = true`, which on the CPU did nothing; after the change all are identical, with 1, 4, and 7 threads (`threaded-determinism.test`). The order is applied in every mode rather than only the deterministic one, since it costs nothing measurable: JAC NPT on the CPU, 16 threads, mixed precision, steps 500 to 1000, 28.2 and 28.5 ms a step against 33.3 and 36.7 before on a shared machine under a load of 8 to 16 (a third pair under a load of 79 gave 38.9 against 36.7). A run on one thread now adds in this order too, so its sums differ in their last bits from those of the serial loop before; no test changed. The partition has at most 256 chunks, so at most 256 threads take part in one reduction; a loop of fewer iterations has one chunk for each. A reduction over more than one dimension, or inside another parallel loop (whose iterations would share the buffer of partial results), is refused rather than left unordered or raced; the lowering produces neither today. Amends the account of D84 for the CPU: the deterministic mode remains for the device.
