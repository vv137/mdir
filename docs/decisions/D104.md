Recorded 2026-10-01.

**The influence function of PME on a device comes from factors along each edge, computed in f64; the energy and the virial are summed by blocks on the device.** The influence function is separable but for $1/m^2$: a kernel computes, at each evaluation, for each point of each edge the wave number $m_a$, $m_a^2$, and $\exp(-\pi^2 m_a^2/\beta^2)$ times the factor of the B-splines of the edge (with the prefactor along $x$) in f64, each rounded once to the type of the grid; `scale` and `convolve` multiply three of them and $1/m^2$. The wave numbers were the index times $1/L$ rounded to f32, which shifts every $m_a$ the same way: on Cellulose the reciprocal energy in mixed precision was 30206.42238 kcal/mol against 30206.42041 in double precision, and is 30206.42227 now, the virial after 10 steps 0.002 from that of double precision, from 0.005. `convolve` takes a thread for each point, not for each row of the grid (34 000 threads computing in f64), sums the energy and the virial of a block of 128 threads by the tree of `gpu.all_reduce` in the type of the grid and stores them in f64; one block adds the blocks in f64 and the host copies seven numbers, not the 2.2 MB of the rows (158 µs). The order of every sum depends on the size of the grid alone. On Cellulose under NPT the product with the virial takes 30 µs, from 655, and the device 3304 µs a step, from 3387; under NVE the tables add 3 µs a step and the product takes 4 µs less.
