# D140

Recorded 2026-10-03.

**`electrostatics = "REACTION_FIELD"` takes a dielectric continuum beyond the cutoff, of the relative permittivity `reaction_field_dielectric` (0 for a conductor), whose field acts on every pair of charges within the cutoff, those of one molecule as well, as GROMACS has it.** A pair that is not excluded takes $f q_i q_j (1/r + k r^2 - c)$, an excluded pair within the cutoff $f q_i q_j (k r^2 - c)$, and each charge the self term $-c f q_i^2/2$, with $k = (\varepsilon - 1)/((2\varepsilon + 1) r_c^3)$, $1/(2 r_c^3)$ for a conductor, and $c = 1/r_c + k r_c^2$, so that the potential is 0 at the cutoff [[Barker1973]](../references.md#barker1973), [[Tironi1995]](../references.md#tironi1995). For a neutral molecule the terms in $c$ of its excluded pairs and its self terms cancel, and what remains is the field of its own dipole. The log prints the three as `Coulomb`, `Coulomb excluded`, and `Coulomb self`; the pairs 1-4 keep their plain Coulomb. The engines differ: on two waters (`omm.py` and a run of GROMACS 2026.3, in the notes) GROMACS gives −9.230793 kJ/mol, the sum of all three to the printed digit, and OpenMM 8.6.1 −8.844445, the pairs that are not excluded alone, which is MDIR's `Coulomb`. `reaction_field_dielectric` has no default, and a run without PME or the field keeps its plain cutoff (`CUTOFF`). On the dipeptide (`test/Driver/reaction-field.test`, and on a GPU) the three terms agree to the printed digit with sums in Python over the pairs, with ε = 78.5 and with a conductor, and the forces with central differences within $10^{-6}$ of the largest. On the peptide of `scripts/validation/gromacs` in flexible water, with ε = 78.5, MDIR's Lennard-Jones, Coulomb, and pairs 1-4 agree with OpenMM 8.6.1 in double precision (the Reference platform, its reader of GROMACS topologies) within $1.5\times10^{-9}$, and the three Coulomb terms with GROMACS's Coulomb (SR) within $2.4\times10^{-5}$ for every force field, 2.3 kJ/mol of 103,000, where the other terms agree within $5\times10^{-6}$: GROMACS sums in single precision. JAC at 2 fs with ε = 78.5, 10,000 steps, mixed precision on an RTX 3090: 0.16 ms a step against 0.21 with PME, and the total energy changed by $9.8\times10^{-5}$ of its value.
