# D299

Recorded 2026-10-19.

**`md-exec-lower-stage-bodies` fuses a batched body before it bufferizes it: the unit dimensions are folded, a pad of a constant becomes an elementwise gather of its source, a reduction from a `linalg.fill` of a constant starts from that constant at its first reduced iteration, other fills become generics, an elementwise producer is fused into a consumer that reads it through a permutation of its loops (or, cheap, into a consumer without reductions), a producer of several uses by evaluating it again in each such consumer, never into a contraction; and each result is written in the caller's buffer. The option `fuse=false` keeps D293's lowering.** #462 (#377, step 4b), the first of its parts. Upstream fuses a producer of one use only, and the legalization of StableHLO leaves unit dimensions, pads, and fills around its ops; IREE recomputes only producers that cost nothing (fills, splat constants, scalar arithmetic) and fuses another producer of several uses into the consumer that dominates the others; a body's intermediates are rows of a tile in global memory, so recomputing an elementwise producer saves the write and the read of its result. Contractions keep their operands so that their schedules (4b) match them and do not evaluate a fused operand again per column. nablaDFT's PaiNN: 933 kernels to 532, `edge.b` 1.29 to 1.22 ms and `node.b` 4.4 to 4.2 ms per call of 4,096 rows (RTX 4090); the profile of 4a at 6,144 atoms puts half of the step in the per-tile sums over the centers and most of the bodies' time in two contractions and a row reduction, which the next parts of #462 take. Validated against the JAX composition (81 atoms, full virial) and SchNetPack in float64, double and mixed (docs/ml4-ir.md, Section 8).
