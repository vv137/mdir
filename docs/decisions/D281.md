**The skins of the neighbor lists are chosen by the run where they are `"AUTO"`, which a key not given is: the defaults of D261, the choice an earlier run of the same key cached, or, for a long run of `mdir run` with the groups on a GPU outside the deterministic mode, candidates of a cost model measured on the system, each compiled anew, of which the fastest is kept unless it is within the noise of the defaults.** Issue #406; exact mode only (the maintainer, 2026-10-17): a list is rebuilt, and an inner list pruned, when a particle has moved half its skin, so every pair of skins takes the same pairs within the cutoff and the choice changes the speed, not the forces; no tolerance-based buffer (#59, #111). *Ruled* (the maintainer on PR #407, 2026-10-18): a reach not given means `"AUTO"`, and a run that does not search takes the reaches of before; the cache is a user cache directory, `$XDG_CACHE_HOME/mdir/skins` (else `~/.cache/mdir/skins`), moved by `MDIR_SKIN_CACHE_DIR` and turned off by `MDIR_SKIN_CACHE=off`; a Python simulation does not search, and `mdir.Auto` resolves at `mdir.compile` from the cache or the defaults; the deterministic mode does not search, since the order of a row of the matrix follows its cells and its sums agree across skins to the rounding only (positions within $1.8\times10^{-9}$ nm after 500 steps of a water box at 10.0, 10.6, and 11.5 Å), and sums independent of the reach are #409. *The keys*: `pairlist_distance` and `pruned_distance` of `[energy]` take a number, fixed as before and the switch off, or `"AUTO"`; Python takes `mdir.Auto`. *Where a run searches*: a GPU, not deterministic, the groups (the matrix is not searched), no minimization, `rebuild_interval`, or `[[energy.model]]`, no entry of the cache, and at least $5\times10^{12}$ particles times steps, about five hours on an RTX 4090 (`MDIR_SKIN_SEARCH=always\|never` overrides the length); after the reference, the run affords the candidates whose compilation and steps fit within 2% of its time, at least 3, at most 7. *The search* (`lib/Compiler/Skins.cpp`): the reference, D261's dual list, measured first, whose intervals of builds and prunings calibrate a model of the step, $t_\text{step}/N = a + c_1\rho' R_\text{in}^3 + c_2\rho' R^3/n_\text{prune} + (c_3 + c_4\rho' R^3)/n_\text{build} + L/(N n_\text{build})$, fitted to the 480 runs of #406 on an RTX 4090 (a root mean square of 1.0% in the relative residuals; $n_\text{build} \propto s^{1.57}$, $n_\text{prune} \propto s_\text{in}^{0.76}$); the model's best pair and its best pair at least 1 Å of outer skin from that one and from the reference; a climb from the fastest of 0.5 Å in the outer skin and 0.1 Å in the inner one while it gains. Each candidate is a simulation (D196) from the state the run begins with: two warm-ups of 200 steps and six blocks of about 0.5 s, the median and the standard error of their mean; the fastest is kept if it gains more than twice the standard error of the difference and 0.3%. The trials' counts and handler of failures are reset before the run (`mdrtResetCounts`). *The cache*: a JSON file a key, whose text names the version, the particle count, a hash of the types, masses, and charges, the cutoff, the structure, each reach as given, the device's name, the precision, the time step, the ensemble, and its temperature; a file of another key is not taken. *The record*: `MDIR: skins:` in the log with a line a candidate, `pairlist_distance_source`, `pruned_distance_source` (`given`, `default`, `cached`, `measured`) and `skin_search` in the manifest, `skins:` in `mdir check`, the sources and `skin_reason` in `Program.plan`. *Measured* (Amber suite, mixed precision, the groups, twice the suite's steps, two rounds in one job, µs a step of the second half of the runs): on an RTX 4090 `"AUTO"` against the grid's best, JAC 2 fs 1.012 (its round from the cache 1.002; the first follows the search on a device it heated, 1.0% slower after 105 s of steps), JAC 4 fs 1.003, Factor IX 1.008 (no candidate gained beyond the noise; the default kept), Cellulose 0.991, STMV at 4 fs 0.993; against the default of D261 0.959 to 0.999 on the nine systems; the search 94 to 286 s, 848 s on STMV, whose compilation takes 86 to 130 s a candidate. On an RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell (Max-Q), three rounds, on a node shared with other jobs where the rounds of one setting differed by up to 4%: against the grid's best 0.983 to 1.032 (JAC at 4 fs, where the search kept the default within its noise), against the default 0.868 to 1.003. `skin-auto.test`, `skin-auto-gpu.test` (the search, the cache hit on a second run and a continuation, a miss at another step, the run checked by `MDIR_CHECK_PRUNING`, the energies at the start of the defaults to every digit, the deterministic mode not searching and repeating its bits), `python-skin-auto.test`, `neighbor-auto-gpu.test`.
