Recorded 2026-10-03.

**What the run does that its user may not intend is a warning of the system, which `mdir run` prints and `mdir check` lists by its code; the first is the waters of a topology of Amber or CHARMM that run flexible because [constraints] does not say how (`flexible_water`). Without a periodic cell the spread of the particles is checked at every row of the log as well as at the frames, the checkpoints, and the end (amends D142).** A topology of GROMACS with [ settles ] states that its waters are rigid, and a run that says nothing stops (D63); a topology of Amber or CHARMM does not tell, its waters are flexible as its engines run them without SHAKE, and a step of 2 fs with them diverged within ten steps on the dipeptide (Appendix C.2): the residues of `water_residues` (WAT, TIP3) that are an oxygen and two hydrogens are counted and named in the warning unless `rigid_water` is given either way, without changing the run. Without a cell, a run that writes no frames and no checkpoints checked its spread only at its end, after the particles had passed the bound by far (two atoms flying apart: step 100 and 1.5 × 10⁷ Å, against step 25 and 29.0 Å at its rows of every 5 steps); the check at the rows copies the positions to the host at each row, which a run without a cell, of thousands of particles, does not feel.
