# D189

Recorded 2026-10-04.

**A term given by an expression observes its energy and its generalized forces along its own constants, written at every energy; the selection lives in the term (`observe = [constants]`) and the columns in one file, `[output] observables`.** The osmotic pressure of a solution between walls that act on its ions [[LuoRoux2010]](../references.md#luoroux2010) needed the force on each wall, which 0.1.0 gave only by making the wall position a component of `[free_energy]` and reading dH/dλ, mixing an observable into alchemy and summing both walls. The identity of thermodynamic integration holds for any constant $\xi$ of a term, $\partial F/\partial\xi = \langle\partial U/\partial\xi\rangle$, so the builder emits for each observed term a potential `@observe<k>` holding that term alone, with its observed constants as scalar arguments in place of constants, and evaluates it at every energy with `request [energy, derivative(i)...]`, the mechanism of `@alchemical` (D161): exactly 0 for a constant that the energy provably does not depend on, an error naming the op for a dependence without a rule. The step itself is unchanged, so it costs nothing between energies. `observe` is accepted by pair terms, terms over tuples and over centers, compound terms, and terms of the positions, for constants given as one number; a value per particle or tuple, an unknown name, or a name twice is an error, as are `observe` without `[output] observables`, the file without an observing term, and either in a minimization. The columns follow the terms in the order of the file, then `observe` (`<term>.energy`, `<term>.d_<constant>` in kcal/mol per unit of the constant); the file is a column file of D149 (protected, continued, listed by `mdir check`), and neither `observe` nor the file enters the fingerprint of D172 or the checkpoint. `observe` is reserved in every term table; triplet terms refuse it for now. A constant 0 observes a sum over the term's particles without changing the dynamics (a count beyond a wall; 217 waters' atoms below a wall on the dipeptide, as counted by hand). Asked by the maintainer, issue #16, which keeps profiles, RDF and coordination, RMSD and the radius of gyration, adaptive biases, and a bridge to PLUMED for later phases; the placement in the term is the maintainer's choice on PR #70. *Evidence.* On the dipeptide in water, the energies and derivatives of two walls, a flat-bottomed bond, a bond over centers, a pair term, and a compound term (one constant unused, written 0) agree with values written out by hand to the six printed decimals on the CPU in double precision and on a GPU in double, and within $10^{-5}$ relative in mixed precision (`observables.test`, `observables-gpu.test`); the derivative in the position of a wall equals dH/dλ of the same wall through `[free_energy]` at every row to the printed digits. Section 6.8, Appendix C.7.
