# D227

Recorded 2026-10-07.

**The values of a program that depend on the state it starts from are arguments of its entry, not constants of its text: the tilts of a triclinic cell, the edges of the cell that the restraints' reference positions belong to under a barostat, the barostat's virial and energy of the dispersion correction and the PME background times the volume, and the barostat state of a continued run that scales every step (`Program::startValues`). The PME grid and the neighbor capacity stay structural; the estimate of the capacity is rounded up to four significant bits, and `Execution.neighbor_capacity` gives it from Python.** Issue #162. Generalizes D213, under which only a program with tunables took the barostat's two constants as arguments (`takesConstants`, removed). The host computes each value as the constant was computed, so a run is unchanged to the bit, and two programs that differ only in the cell they start from have one text and one compile-cache entry (D212). The audit ([compile-cache.md](../compile-cache.md#values-of-the-start-d227)) found every other value that depends on the start already outside the text: the edges are arguments, the $1/V$ constants of the log are the host's, and the factors of the influence function are buffers. The grid, an attribute of `md.reciprocal`, and the neighbor capacity, the `width` of `convert-md-to-md-exec`, shape loops and buffers and stay in the key. The capacity only sets the room at first (a build that finds more neighbors makes room), but its estimate, $\lceil 1.5\,n_\text{max}\rceil + 16$ rounded up to a multiple of 8, moved from one equilibrated state to the next (888, 896, 888 for three states of ala3), which alone made a continued stage miss. By the maintainer's decision on PR #192 the estimate is rounded up to a multiple of an eighth of the power of two below it, at most 12.5% more room, and `Execution.neighbor_capacity` is `[execution] neighbor_capacity` of the control file (0 estimates; `Program.plan["neighbor_capacity"]` is the capacity taken); the grid stays as it is, and `System.pme_grid` fixes it. *Evidence.* Deterministic, `mdir run` on ala3 at constant pressure with restraints and on its continuation from the checkpoint, with a barostat that scales every step and its continuation (`%bstate*`), and on a triclinic water box at constant volume and pressure (`%tilt_*`), give the positions, velocities, cell, and energy file of main to the bit on the CPU and a GPU, in mixed and double precision; so does the Python program of segments of the ala3 production stage, with and without restraints, from an equilibrated state (on the CPU also in the default mode); the capacities of these runs differ from main's. `neighbor_capacity` 888, 896, and 1024 give one result to the bit. The program of segments from three NPT-equilibrated states of ala3 (edges 3.517 to 3.551 nm) differed on main in `%baro_constant` and `%baro_energy_constant`, and with restraints in `%rest_edge*`; now its text is the same, the capacity is 896 for the three, and the second and third hit the entry of the first. Three runs of the ala3 example (`--steps-scale 0.01`, GPU, default mode) on one directory: every stage of the second and third hits, the production stage included (capacity 896, grid $32^3$; 12.1 s instead of 19.3 s), where a multiple of 8 gave 864, 872, and 864 and a miss. `compile-cache-cell.test` and its GPU variant check one text and pipeline for a cell 1% larger, a hit, the run of a hit equal to one without the cache, a hit by `neighbor_capacity` for a cell 2% larger, and its run equal with either capacity, bit for bit. The Amber suite on one RTX 3090 runs at the rate of main within 0.3% (capacities 960 to 1152, up to 48 more than on main).
