# D344

Recorded 2026-10-25.

**The shards of a suite are taken in the lexical order of the tests (`--order=lexical`), and `lit.cfg.py` refuses a shard without it; the check of the expected unsupported tests compares a shard's own tests.** lit sorts the tests before it takes every n-th of them for a shard, and its default order, `smart`, follows the times and failures recorded in each build tree (`.lit_test_times.txt`; `--skip-test-time-recording` stops the writing of the file, not its reading). The two trees of D309 had different records, so their shards overlapped and left tests out: in the train of 2026-10-26, 04:30, nine unsupported tests ran in both shards (among them `Driver/model-term-gpu.test` and `Sanitizer/tuple-block-sums-gpu.test`), the two shards together showed 20 distinct unsupported tests where 32 were expected, and passed tests were missed the same way. In the lexical order the selection of a shard depends on the names of the tests alone, so the shards of any two trees of one commit are disjoint and cover the suite. `test/mdir_lit.py` reads `--num-shards` and `--run-shard` from the command line, `LIT_OPTS`, or `LIT_NUM_SHARDS` and `LIT_RUN_SHARD`, and stops with an error before any test runs when the order is not lexical. Under a shard, the check of #608 takes the shard's tests by the same rule and notes "expected unsupported: N of M tests of shard k/n". One shard cannot see whether another shard ran a test twice or not at all; the lexical order is what excludes it. Amends D309. Issue #608 (follow-up).
