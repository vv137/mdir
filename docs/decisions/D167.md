Preserve ownership/image maps, support, saved activations, accumulation, and completion through AD and until backward finishes. Check communication adjoints, coordinate/cell finite differences, and one/two-domain equivalence before optimizing; reordering and overlap require the checked-premise argument and baseline switch of the principles. Extend the validated interface to MACE. Pin artifacts, licenses, and model/backend/adapter versions; architecture support does not imply arbitrary checkpoint compatibility or aqueous-salt accuracy. torch-mlir is an optional later bridge for generated graphs, not an AD engine or a prerequisite for the first deliverable; preserve particle semantics before generic tensor lowering and verify actual import coverage. Do not build a general neural AD engine or tensor compiler, or require training/full-trajectory derivatives. All three models are not required to complete ML3; ML5 can begin with the first validated staged model. Differentiate the research by verified same-model execution alternatives and whole-step measurements, not merely by MLIR use or differentiable communication, which already has precedents.
