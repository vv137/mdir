# D331

Recorded 2026-10-22.

**On a device, a structure of groups of neighbors starts with one block of 64 entries a group, and its first build sizes the pool of blocks: the build counts the blocks it takes even where they do not fit, and the existing growth (`mdrtGroupsGrow`) gives the pool a quarter more than that count, after which the build is made again. A later build that needs more grows the pool the same way, never leaving out a pair, and `mdir run` logs the growths after the first build of a structure.** #471, fix 5 of its list. Amends D89, whose pool started with $\lceil w/64\rceil + 1$ blocks a group from the width $w$ of the matrix (D227, D316: half as many again as the most neighbors of a particle, plus 16, rounded): 12 blocks a group at STMV, of which the lists took at most 54%. *Bound*: the list of a group holds the particles at later places within the reach $R$ of any of its members, so its length follows the mean neighbors of a particle, $\bar n = \tfrac43\pi\rho R^3$ beyond the range of the correlations; the pool's need, a sum over all the groups, follows the density of the cell, whose volume fluctuates at constant pressure by $\sqrt{k_BT\kappa_T/V}$ ($4\times10^{-4}$ at STMV, $3\times10^{-3}$ at JAC for water), and scales as $V^{-1}$. So the count of the first build $B_0$ gives the need of every build of an equilibrated state, and the margin $\lfloor B_0/4\rfloor$ covers the fluctuations and a compression to 0.8 of the volume. *Why the count and not an estimate*: the ratio of a list's length to $\bar n$ depends on the shape of the groups (at most 1.2 at STMV, $R$ = 9.5 Å, and 0.77 at JAC, 11 Å), while the count is exact. An estimate carried to the lowering would be a second constant of the lowered program that depends on the start, which D227 and D316 avoid for the width (stages that differ only in their start would compile anew), and would pass through `md_exec.build_neighbors`, `md-exec-reuse-neighbors`, and the shape rule of D330; with the count, the lowered program is the same for every input. Starting from the old size and shrinking would hold the large buffers at the start, where ×15 ran out of memory. *Overflow*: a growth waits for the device, frees the buffers to the driver (D312), allocates a quarter more than needed, and builds again in the same refresh; stopping instead would fail an NPT equilibration that compresses a box. The runtime notes only complete builds (`mdrtNoteGroups`), so the log's capacity is that of a build that fit and the groups of D106 are not counted twice, and it counts the growths of a structure's lists after its first complete build (`mdrtGroupsBuilt`; a growth of the places alone is not counted): `MDIR: the lists of groups grew N times after the first build of their structure, which sizes them`, printed only when N > 0. `neighbor_capacity` keeps its meaning for the matrix and no longer sets the groups' room; no key is added. With D330, the steps take the room that the start's first build gave. *Validation* (RTX 4090, base 039478cf against the branch in one job): the deterministic mode (the matrix) is bitwise identical in the log rows and every numeric dataset of the checkpoints on JAC (2,000 steps, mixed and double), STMV ×1 (500 mixed, 200 double), and a checkpoint followed by `--continue` (JAC 1,000 mixed and 400 double, STMV ×1 200 mixed); `mdir emit --stage=lowered` of the deterministic GPU program and of the CPU program is byte-identical on JAC and STMV ×1, mixed and double. Default mode: the RMS of the final positions after 100 steps of STMV ×1 is 7.4e-5 and 9.7e-5 nm base against the branch, against 8.9e-5 base against base and 7.8e-5 the branch against itself, with the same 35 builds; JAC after 500 steps is decorrelated alike.
