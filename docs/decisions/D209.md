# D209

Recorded 2026-10-06.

**With a topology, the correction for the dispersion includes the tail beyond the cutoff of every `[[energy.pair]]` term, over the pairs its `groups` select and the topology does not exclude, in the energy, the virial, the λ-state energies, `dH/dλ`, and the columns of `observe`.** With a uniform density beyond $r_c$ [[AllenTildesley2017]](../references.md#allentildesley2017), each unordered pair adds $\nu\,(4\pi/V)\,I_{ij}$, $I_{ij} = \int_{r_c}^\infty r^2u_{ij}(r)\,dr$, to the energy and $\nu\,(4\pi/V)(3I_{ij} + r_c^3u_{ij}(r_c))$ to the trace of the virial (the work of the pairs beyond the cutoff, $-\int r^3u'\,dr$, by parts), with $\nu = N^2/(N(N-1) - 2N_\text{excluded})$, the factor of the topology's own $\langle C_6\rangle$, so that both follow one convention; for $-C_6/r^6$ this is $E_\text{disp}$ with $\mathrm{tr}\,\mathsf W = 6E$. The pairs are counted in classes of particles equal in all that the expression reads (type, charge if it reads `q1`/`q2`, per-particle parameters, the two group flags), less the excluded pairs. In $s = r_c/r$, $I = r_c^3\int_0^1u(r_c/s)\,s^{-4}\,ds$, whose integrand is a polynomial for a sum of powers $r^{-k}$, $k \ge 4$, which the adaptive Gauss–Kronrod rule of 7 and 15 points [[Piessens1983]](../references.md#piessens1983) integrates exactly on its first interval (degree up to 13 there): the tail of a Lennard-Jones or of $r^{-8}$ is its closed form to round-off; other expressions are integrated to $10^{-13}$ relative. A pair term follows the correction of `[energy]`; with a topology it now accepts its `dispersion_correction`, whose `"NONE"` leaves it out (a refusal lifted, not a new key; `"ENERGY_PRESSURE"` with the correction of `[energy]` off is an error). A term whose $r^3u(r)$ does not fall by a decade per decade at $10^3$ to $10^6\,r_c$ (a tail that diverges, as of a $1/r$ Coulomb-like term) or that reads the time `t` is left out with the warning `pair_tail_left_out` under the default correction, so that inputs that ran before still run (only the long-range estimate of such a term is omitted; its energy, forces, and virial within the cutoff are as before, and a tail of $1/r^3$ or slower needs a lattice sum such as Ewald, not this correction), and is an error, naming the term and suggesting its own `dispersion_correction = "NONE"`, when `[energy]` or the term gives `"ENERGY_PRESSURE"`; `mdir check` reports both. The classes, the counts, and this test are made with the system (System.cpp), the integrals by the builder. The tail is evaluated at each λ state; its derivatives in a component of λ and in an observed constant are Richardson-extrapolated central differences on the host, exactly 0 when the expression does not read the parameter. All of these scale as $1/V$ with the cell, as the topology's correction does. A pair term's tail is its whole expression, while the topology's correction leaves its repulsion out (Amber, GROMACS `EnerPres`): an NBFIX written as a correction term differs from the same pair in `[ nonbond_params ]` by the tail of $\Delta C_{12}$, $\nu\,(4\pi/V)\,N_AN_B\,\Delta C_{12}/(9r_c^9)$. With a `lennard_jones_modifier`, `POTENTIAL_SHIFT` included, the topology's correction stays refused (D121), and the tails of pair terms with it. Validation (`pair-dispersion.test`, `pair-dispersion-gpu.test`, 60 A + 60 B in 32 Å, $r_c$ = 12 Å): the dispersion line against Simpson sums in $\ln r$ to $4\times10^{-7}$ kcal/mol (the printed digits) for the topology alone, `[ nonbond_params ]`, the NBFIX correction term, and $-c_8/r^8 - a\,e^{-r/l}/r^4$; the NBFIX correction term against `[ nonbond_params ]` in the total energy, the diagonal of the virial, and the pressure to $2.4\times10^{-8}$ kcal/mol, $9.7\times10^{-8}$ ($1.9\times10^{-6}$ in mixed precision on a GPU), and $6.5\times10^{-5}$ bar after the $\Delta C_{12}$ tail of $3.26\times10^{-4}$ kcal/mol; the tails of `dH/dλ`, the λ-state energies, and the energy and both derivatives of an observed NBFIX against their closed forms to $6\times10^{-7}$, on the CPU and a GPU in double and mixed precision; in NPT (500 particles, 250 A + 250 B, 100 K, 1 bar, C-rescale, 2 ns, four seeds each, the first 0.5 ns dropped) the mean volume with the correction term is 24026.9 ± 11.4 Å³ against 24031.0 ± 13.8 with `[ nonbond_params ]` (−0.2 SE), where the correction without its tail gave 24288.7 ± 17.4 (+1.07%, +11.7 SE). Found by the study of #140 (0.80 kcal/mol missing at σ' = 3.7 Å on 300 particles). Issue #145. *Fixed by #155:* the quadrature's error is relative to the integral or to the size of the terms that the expression sums (every sum and difference over the absolute values of its operands), whichever is larger, and the test of divergence counts a value within $10^{-12}$ of that size as 0: an NBFIX correction at the force field's own σ and ε, where a fit begins, had a tail at the level of the rounding of its two terms, which no error relative to its value reached, and was refused. Its tail is now 0 and its derivatives in `observe` take their tails (`pair-dispersion.test`). *Fixed by #238:* a term left out with the warning ended the collection of the tails, so a pair term after it had no entry and the builder aborted; each term now has its entry, empty when it is left out, whatever its place in the control file or in `System.pair_terms` (`pair-term-order.test`, `python-pair-order.test`).
