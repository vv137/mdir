# D330

Recorded 2026-10-22.

**On a device, `md-exec-assign-storage` gives a neighbor structure the storage of an earlier structure of the same shape that nothing uses any more, with a `md_exec.reset_neighbors` where the later one begins. The shape is the type of the structure, its kind and width, the number of particles, and the type of the positions. In every program of dynamics, the structure of the evaluation at the start and the structure of the steps now share one storage. The steps still begin with a structure of their own in the sense of D218 and build at step 1.** #471, the item after D315. Amends D218, whose stated cost was "a storage for the structure of the start besides that of the steps". *Inventory* (main 1817dc5d, `MDIR_DEVICE_MEMORY=2`, the IR): `mdir_run` had two storage-form `md_exec.empty_neighbors` of one shape. The start's came from the `build_neighbors` of the evaluation at the start and was last used by the energies of step 0; the steps' came from the empty structure that `md-exec-reuse-neighbors` gives the loop marked `mdrt.fresh`. Neither was freed before the end of the program. The same pair exists with checkpoints (the steps' storage is outside the loop of segments, which resets it), in a Python simulation, and with the matrix of the deterministic mode. At STMV ×1 each structure held 465.8 MiB: 390.8 of entries and masks, 22.1 of the other buffers of the runtime, and 52.9 of `placeOf` and the two reference configurations; at ×11, 5,123.8 MiB. The dual list's inner lists and the edges of D277 were absent there, and the incidence structures of the tuples (D253, D255) are one for each set and not duplicated. *Why it is correct*: the reset makes the structure valid for no configuration, as at the start of every segment, so the first refresh builds whatever the storage held. Its test of displacement now compares with the earlier structure's last configuration instead of zeros, and it is ignored as before. A build writes what its loops read, and a dual list prunes its inner list from the outer list it has just built. Liveness is traced in the value form, through refreshes, the loops that carry a structure, and `md_exec.reference_positions`, whose result is the storage's own buffer. All ops on structures are on the main stream in program order, and the reset writes the structure (`Independence.h`). A structure that `build_edges` or a stage reads, or one built for centers or images of its own (D290), keeps its storage. On the host nothing changes. The capacity carries over: lists that the start grew would give the steps their room, with the same forces; the start did not grow in any run here. *Alternatives*: an op that releases the start's structure when it is last used. That needs a release in the runtime, the bookkeeping of the activation's handles, and blocks of the same size for the pool to hand them on; the two `block of` buffers already differ. Chaining the start's structure into the steps was rejected by D218. *Validation* (RTX 4090, main 1817dc5d and the branch in one job): the deterministic mode, with one matrix instead of two, was bitwise identical to main in the log rows and every numeric dataset of the final checkpoint. This held on JAC (2,000 steps, mixed and double) and on STMV ×1 (500 steps mixed, 200 double), and with a checkpoint at half the run followed by `mdir run --continue` to twice the steps (JAC 1,000 mixed and 400 double, STMV ×1 200 mixed), at both checkpoints. The CPU program of JAC (`mdir emit --stage=lowered`, mixed and double, groups and the matrix) is identical to main's. In the default mode, the RMS of the differences of the final positions after 100 steps of STMV ×1 was 9.7e-5 and 8.4e-5 nm main against the branch, against 6.4e-5 main against main and 7.7e-5 the branch against itself, with the same 35 builds. The Python simulations of several parts and the checkpoint tests (`python-groups*`, `python-deterministic-run*`, `python-checkpoints-*`, `continue-gpu.test`) pass. *Measured* (`MDIR_DEVICE_MEMORY=2`, NPT 4 fs, mixed, groups, 1,000 steps, STMV from the GROMACS topology): the device peak went from 2,521 to 2,045 MiB at ×1, from 8,455 to 6,579 at ×4, and from 22,283 to 17,151 at ×11, which is 469 to 476 MiB a replica, as derived. ×12 (12.81 M atoms) runs in 18,681 MiB, ×13 in 20,183, and ×14 (14.94 M) in 22,985; ×15 runs out of memory after the row of step 0. The largest STMV replica on 24 GB goes from ×11 to ×14. The Amber suite's rates (one RTX 4090 to itself, main and the branch alternated): seven systems within 0.3% of main in 2 rounds; Factor IX NPT 0.35% and Cellulose NPT 0.5% slower in every round, over 10 rounds in either order. The lowered programs of Cellulose NPT differ only by the one allocation, so the difference likely comes from where the buffers lie on the device, not from the work. No control-file key or output change.
