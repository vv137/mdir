# D145

Recorded 2026-10-03.

**The time `t` in ps may enter the expressions of terms over tuples, over the centers of groups, and over the pairs of a topology, and `pull_coordinates` in [output] names a file of the coordinates of the terms over centers, with their energies and forces, at every energy of the log.** A reference that moves, `k*(r - (r0 + v*t))^2`, pulls at a rate, and `-f*r` at a constant force. A term that uses `t` makes the programs take the time at which their forces are computed, $n\,\Delta t$ after step $n$ (from the step the run begins at; 0 for a minimization), so that velocity Verlet takes the force of the end of each step at the time of that end; a parameter named `t` is an error. On two argon atoms on a spring whose rest length grows at 20 Å/ps, 100 steps of 1 fs: the term as a bond over the centers of two groups of one atom, as a bond over the pair, and as a term over the pairs of the topology gives the positions of velocity Verlet in Python to the last bit, on the CPU and on a GPU in double precision. The file holds in one what GROMACS writes to its files `pullx` and `pullf` [[GromacsManual2025]](../references.md#gromacsmanual2025): a header of names and units, then at each energy the step, the time, and for each term its coordinates (`r`, `dx`, `dy`, `dz` in Å for two centers, `theta` in radians otherwise), its energy in kcal/mol, and its force: for two centers the force on the second, $\mathbf F = -(\partial E/\partial r\,\mathbf d/r + \partial E/\partial \mathbf d)$ with $\mathbf d$ from the first, and its component along $\mathbf d$, `f_r`, which for an expression in `r` alone is $-\partial E/\partial r$, in kcal/mol/Å; for an angle or a dihedral $-\partial E/\partial\theta$ in kcal/mol/rad. The program computes the centers once for each line, in its own body, and the derivatives from a potential of the coordinates as scalar arguments (`derivative(n)` of `md.evaluate`). A continued run keeps the lines up to its checkpoint and appends: continued from the checkpoint before the last, the file is that of the run without a stop, byte for byte. On the dipeptide of D139 the first line agrees with the coordinates, the energies, and central differences of each term to the printed digit. It needs a term over centers and `energy_interval`, in a run of dynamics. At the start of a run on a GPU the sums of the centers of a dihedral for the energy and for the file, 42, fuse into one loop, whose first reduction kernel passed the descriptors of their buffers, 632 parameters and 5056 bytes, past the 4352 that PTX before ISA 8.1 allows: the lowering now gives a pair of reduction kernels to each run of the sums of a loop whose descriptors fit 3 KB, and each sum keeps its tree, so the results are those of before to the bit; the loops of the suite before this change stay one run, and the output of pulling with a dihedral takes two. Packing the sums into one buffer would make the kernels independent of their number, but the time of the reductions is in their trees, one for each sum (D139), which a packed buffer keeps; it waits for the reduction of the sums of a block together.
