# D126

Recorded 2026-10-02.

**Groups of 16 run in triclinic cells, built in the brick of the cell with lattice shifts** (docs/triclinic.md, P3; neighbor-groups.md, Section 5.1). `NeighborsGroupsGPUTriclinic.mlir`, which the script of `NeighborsGroupsGPU.mlir` writes and only a triclinic module parses, wraps the positions into the brick $[0,a_x)\times[0,b_y)\times[0,c_z)$ along c, b, and a, and keeps its compact order and grid of candidates. Across a face of z the row of the grid $t_z$ cells beyond is that of $t_z\mathbf c$ away, x and y with it, and across a face of y that of $t_y\mathbf b$: the window of y of each row of z, and of x of each row of y, moves by those tilts, and the window of x by whole periods of a back into the grid, which the dodecahedron needed (its windows moved by more than the cell is wide and visited 438 pairs twice). A range of z wider than the grid makes those of y and x the whole grid, and one of y that of x, so that every cell is visited once. The minimum image is taken in one pass, which gives the lattice shift $\mathbf n$ with it; the shifts of the frames and of the entries are lattice vectors, applied as $\mathbf n H$ (`kernels::emitLatticeShift`), and that of an entry takes five bits a vector, $(e + 16)$ in bits 16 to 30, as the brick spans up to 3.5 periods of a in lattice coordinates. The images of D115 are those a lattice vector away, by 27 codes: $\Delta n_c$ from −1 to 1, then y rounded again and $\Delta n_b$ from −1 to 1, then x and $\Delta n_a$; every nonzero lattice vector of a reduced cell is at least as long as the least of $a_x, b_y, c_z$, and the reach is at most half of it, so no image further away is within the reach of a box, and D115's condition on $a_x, b_y, c_z$ is exact. `test/Runtime/neighbors-groups-triclinic-gpu.mlir` checks every pair against f64 in an octahedron, a dodecahedron, a hexagonal cell, and a cell of D115; on the dodecahedron and Amber's octahedron, double precision gives the log of the matrix over 200 steps, also with a dual list, and 10 ps of the octahedron at constant energy conserve as the matrix does, at 1012 ns/day against 398. compute-sanitizer, now run on the groups, found two defects of the orthorhombic build, fixed: the images kernel read the box of a group past the last before testing it, and the scan of a block let lanes read a sum that another lane overwrote, with no barrier between. *Amended by D242: the 27 codes are every image within three halves of $a_x, b_y, c_z$ of the center of a box, which holds every image within the reach of a box for a reach below the least of $a_x, b_y, c_z$, not only half of it; the reason given here for them was not the one that holds.*
