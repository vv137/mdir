# D242

Recorded 2026-10-09.

**The lists of the groups hold every image of every pair within a reach below the least edge of the cell, in an orthorhombic and in a triclinic cell; the builder, a commit, and the runtime refuse a longer one** (#263, #258, docs/neighbor-groups.md, Section 5.2). In an orthorhombic cell the images kernel of D115 took, along each axis, the nearest image of a candidate or the one on the other side of the boundary, 8 combinations, which are every image within the reach of a box only while $h_a + R \le L_a$; the builder asked an orthorhombic cell for twice the cutoff alone, so a long skin lost pairs silently: 216 argon atoms in a cube of 17.2 Å lost a pair at 126 of 2000 steps with $R$ = 15 Å; in a cube that a barostat takes from 22 to 19.4 Å with $R$ = 17 Å the potential was not the sum over all images in 23 of 79 frames; the lists of 320 particles in a cube of 4 lacked 27, 416, 2402, and 5673 images of 46,145 to 198,183 at $R$ = 0.6, 0.75, 0.9, and 0.975 of the edge. The kernel now takes the 27 images a cell away or none along each axis, as the triclinic one does along the lattice vectors, the code in bits 26 to 30 of the queue (places are fewer than $2^{26}$). These are every image with $\lvert r_a\rvert \le \tfrac32 L_a$ from the center of a box; an image within the reach of a box has $\lvert r_a\rvert \le h_a + R$; and $h_a \le \tfrac12 L_a$, the particles of a group being taken in the minimum image of its first: so the lists are complete for $R < \min_a L_a$, whatever the positions. The triclinic lists, which had the 27 codes, were bound to half of the least of $a_x, b_y, c_z$ by the builder; the same proof gives them the whole of it. The builder refuses `pairlist_distance` above 0.999 of the least edge with the groups (the margin of the builds in f32), a commit of a writable borrow does, and the runtime stops a run whose barostat takes the cell below `pairlist_distance`/0.999, as at twice the cutoff: a refusal where exactness cannot be had, rather than more images, since a reach of the whole cell has no use and the shifts of the entries have their bits. The dual list is pruned from these lists and inherits. The tests of the templates now count the bits of each pair against its images within the reach in f64 and compare the sums of the squared displacements in the frames, at reaches up to 0.975 of the least edge (0 wrong of up to 198,183 bits, 50,644 pairs with several images), and `group-reach-gpu.test` runs a cube and a tilted cell under a barostat with the groups and the dual list against the sum over all images, with the two refusals. The rates of the Amber suite, whose cells are wider than twice the reach, are those of before: no group there reaches the images kernel.
