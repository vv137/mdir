# D176

Recorded 2026-10-04.

**A build of groups on a device also counts as beyond any cell a position whose wrap does not land in the cell, and clamps the cell of every key on both sides.** Amends D107, whose test counted a position only beyond 10¹⁰⁰. A finite position beyond about 10¹⁶ cells wraps with an error larger than the cell, of either sign; its key, clamped only from above, could be negative, and its atomic add landed before the table of counts: compute-sanitizer showed it on the ff19SB dipeptide of `not-numbers-gpu.test` at a time step of 0.5 ps (an atomic 92 bytes before the table, one run in six), and the device then failed at the next copy, so that the run aborted instead of stopping with its word (#22). Clamped from below as well, the key stayed inside its table, but the wrapped position, far outside the cell, sent a later stage of the build 8 bytes before another table (three runs in six). A position whose fraction of the cell after the wrap lies outside [−10⁻⁶, 1 + 10⁻⁶] now gets no key and no place and is counted with the positions that are not numbers, and the run stops with the same word; a position within 10⁶ Å of the cell wraps with an error of 10⁻¹⁰ Å, far inside the tolerance. The test of a number takes the largest coordinate, not the sum of the three, which opposite coordinates cancel. The build of a matrix takes the fraction minus its floor, which lands in [0, 1] whatever the position, and clamps on both sides already.
