# D241

Recorded 2026-10-09.

**The neighbor matrix of a triclinic cell takes a pair if any of its images is within the reach; the build, a commit, and the runtime ask I2 alone of the cell** (#258, docs/triclinic.md, Section 2). The build tested a candidate with the image of the one pass along $\mathbf c$, $\mathbf b$, $\mathbf a$, the image in the brick, which is the nearest image only within $\tfrac12\min(a_x, b_y, c_z)$; the builder refused a longer `pairlist_distance`, but the runtime under a barostat tests only I2, so a run from an accepted input lost pairs once the cell had shrunk: 216 argon atoms with every tilt on its bound, $r_c$ = 9.2 Å, $R$ = 11 Å = $\tfrac12\min$ at the start, 20,000 bar, had a potential that was not the sum over all images in 9 of 79 frames (up to 0.019 kcal/mol, 8 pairs), on the CPU and on a device; at constant energy with $R$ 16% past the bound a pair was missing at 24% to 43% of the steps (#258). A guard in the runtime or a reach clamped at a build were the alternatives; the maintainer chose exactness. $H$ being lower triangular, $z'$ of an image depends on $n_c$ alone, $y'$ on $n_c$ and $n_b$, $x'$ on all three, so the images within $R$ are in three nested ranges of integers, exactly the images of the cube $[-R, R]^3$; from the image of the pass they are that image alone for $R \le \tfrac12 L_a$, and at most 8 up to $R = \min(a_x, b_y, c_z)$. Where the reach is at most 0.49 of the least of the diagonal a build is the function of before, which tests the image of the pass alone (`..._triclinic_pass`); in a narrower cell it is a second function, the first with the test replaced, which `scripts/generate-matrix-images-template.py` writes into the template (`..._triclinic_images`, checked by `generated-templates.test`): it tests the image of the pass first and runs the ranges for a candidate beyond the reach (`@mdrt.image_within_triclinic`, `@mdrt_gpu_matrix_image_within`). A first version put the test in the loops of the one function under a branch; never taken, it still cost 6% of a build on a device (19,072 particles in an octahedron: 0.505 against 0.477 ms a build, 616 against 622 ns/day), so the build of a cell of ordinary width is now the code of before, and the rate is unchanged (see the PR). A row holds a particle once, and the pass of the loop over pairs finds the one image that can be within $r_c$, two images being at least $2 r_c$ apart. The cells of the search and the test of D80 are unchanged. The ranges count at most 64 images a side (a cell that a failed run has blown up must give loops that end, #168), exact for `pairlist_distance` up to 127 times `cutoff` under I2, which the builder asks of the matrix of a triclinic cell; the bound on `pairlist_distance` of half of the least of the diagonal goes for the matrix, in the builder and in a commit (D238), and stays for the groups until their own decision (#263). `test/Runtime/neighbors-matrix-triclinic.mlir` checks the rows against every pair and 343 images in f64 at reaches from half of the least of the diagonal to more than the whole of it (the old build lost 42 of 43,790 pairs at 1.02 times the bound and 53,850 of 102,430 in a flat cell), its `-gpu` twin the device against the host, and `triclinic-reach.test` and its `-gpu` twin the run under the barostat.
