# D312

Recorded 2026-10-21.

**The device pool does not grow with the number of builds: a loop over the groups gathers its positions and fields (and a pruning check the frames of its places) into buffers of the room of the structure's places, the length of its `order` buffer, not of the number of places that the last build occupied; the kernels still run over the occupied places. A buffer of the runtime's neighbor structures that a growth replaces (the groups' buffers and `blockOf`, the rows of a matrix, the slots of the pairs of images) goes back to the driver, not to the pool. The pool still reuses a block only for a request of the same size.** #471, the first of its fixes. The occupied count is $N$ plus up to 63 empty places a chunk of 64 and moves by multiples of 64 from one build to the next; each new count left its three blocks (12, 4, and 4 B a place) in the pool for the rest of the run. With the room, these buffers take one size for each room the structure has had, which changes only when it grows by a quarter over what a build asked for; every growth waits for the device anyway, so returning the replaced buffers costs no wait. A tolerance rule in the pool (a larger free block within some fraction) was not taken: it would change every allocation and need its own bound of waste. *Bound (derived)*: the gathered buffers of a loop over the groups are $(12 + 4k)$ B a place of room for positions in f32 (mixed) and $k$ fields of 4 bytes, one set for each room the structure has had; without a growth of the places, one set (25.4 MiB at STMV, room 1,333,936 places, $k = 2$). The cost: while in use they are larger by the room less the occupied places, about 4.7 MiB at STMV (measured: the peak in use did not change). *Validation* (RTX 4090, `--idle`, main and this branch in one job, mixed, NPT at 4 fs, groups): the allocator's peak with the pool less its peak in use from 725.8 to 20.4 MiB at STMV and from 2,076.0 to 43.0 MiB at ×2 after 1,000 steps, and 959.0 to 20.4 and 2,417.9 to 43.0 after 4,000 steps; the device peak (`nvidia-smi`) from 6,529 to 5,641 MiB and 12,963 to 10,707 MiB after 1,000 steps, 6,823 to 5,643 and 13,345 to 10,707 after 4,000; the rates of the second half of each run within 0.4% of main's, either way (81.5 and 81.8 ns/day at STMV, 36.9 and 37.0 at ×2, after 1,000 steps). STMV ×4 (4,268,380 atoms), out of memory before, runs in 20,771 MiB at 17.5 ns/day; ×3 takes 15,717 MiB (19,813 before). `test/Driver/pool-retention-gpu.test` compares the blocks held from the driver after 100 and 3,000 steps of the dipeptide in water. No control-file key or output change.
