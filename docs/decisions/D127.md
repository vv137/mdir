# D127

Recorded 2026-10-02.

**The barostat scales a triclinic cell as $H\,\mathrm{diag}(\boldsymbol\mu)$, and CHARMM's hexagonal cells run** (docs/triclinic.md, P4). The positions scale as $\mathbf x\,\mathrm{diag}(\boldsymbol\mu)$, as they do for an orthorhombic cell, so the cell keeps its lattice coordinates: each column of $H$ scales by its $\mu_a$, the tilts $b_x$ and $c_x$ with $a_x$ and $c_y$ with $b_y$ (I3), which keeps the cell lower triangular and reduced, isotropic or semi-isotropic. The pressure of an axis is the diagonal of the virial over $a_xb_yc_z$, and the work of a scaling, from the diagonals of the virials, is that of an orthorhombic cell, since $\mathbf x\,\mathrm{diag}(\boldsymbol\mu)$ is a diagonal strain of the positions; the test of validity of D80 and the reference of restraints (D124) take the diagonal. The barostat keeps the six numbers of the cell on the host and the loops build `md.triclinic_cell` from them; `mdrtSetTilt` gives the tilts to the trajectory and the checkpoint. TIP3P water in a truncated octahedron of 1589 waters and in a box of 1391, 400 ps at 1 bar and 300 K, have the densities 0.9850 ± 0.0007 and 0.9848 ± 0.0008 g/cm³ over the last 300 ps; the shape of the cell stays to the printed digits; a run that continues from a checkpoint gives the log of one that does not stop (`test/Driver/triclinic-npt.test`). 1417 TIP3P waters in a hexagonal cell of CHARMM (36, 36, 38 Å, γ = 120°), minimized and evaluated in CHARMM 51b1 with PME, and evaluated by MDIR from the PSF and the CRD with the cell given by its lengths and angles, agree in the bonds and angles to the printed digits, in the Lennard-Jones to $10^{-9}$, and in each term of the electrostatics, real space, excluded, reciprocal, and self, to $4\times10^{-8}$ after the ratio of the Coulomb constants (`scripts/validation/charmm/run.py`, phase `hexagonal`).
