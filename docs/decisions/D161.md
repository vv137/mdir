# D161

Recorded 2026-10-04.

**`[free_energy]` decouples a selection of whole molecules through states of $\boldsymbol\lambda$, every component a parameter of the expressions, and writes $\partial U/\partial\lambda$ and the energy of every state at every energy, for thermodynamic integration and MBAR.** The Lennard-Jones of a decoupled pair is $(1-\lambda_\text{V})u_\text{LJ}(r_A)$, $r_A^6 = \alpha_\text{sc}\sigma^6\lambda_\text{V}^p + r^6$ [[Beutler1994]](../references.md#beutler1994); the charges of the selection scale by $1-\lambda_\text{C}$ while the pairs within it keep full strength: under PME a tuple term over its pairs that are not excluded adds back $(1-(1-\lambda_\text{C})^2)fq_iq_j\mathrm{erf}(\beta r)/r$, and the self term and the background follow the scaled charges; the correction for the dispersion is linear in $\lambda_\text{V}$. `@energy` holds the state of the run as constants; `@alchemical` takes $\boldsymbol\lambda$ as arguments, holds only what depends on it (pairs masked by $c_{ij}$), and is evaluated at every energy with `derivative(m)` at the state and its energy at every state in one `scf.for`. Differentiation gains parameter derivatives of sums over particles and of `md.reciprocal`, and three outcomes for every value: independent of the parameter only by a proof that models every path (operands, values that kernels take from outside, arguments of blocks of known meaning), then exactly zero; dependent with a rule; or dependent without a rule or of independence not proved (an unknown op, effects on memory, a region of unknown meaning), an error that names the op and says which (`--md-differentiate=remarks=true` lists the ops taken as zero; `differentiate-parameters*.mlir`); and the derivative of `md.reciprocal` by the identity of its quadratic form, $(E(\mathbf q+\boldsymbol\delta)-E(\mathbf q-\boldsymbol\delta))/2$, rather than site potentials from the gather, which would change the four templates of PME; in mixed precision it is within $5\times10^{-6}$ kcal/mol of double. `md-exec-assign-storage` releases the buffer of a field that nothing uses. A decoupled pair without σ takes 0.3 nm in $r_A$, and the Coulomb of a scaled kernel takes a distance of at least $10^{-4}$ nm, so that overlapping decoupled particles give no $0\cdot\infty$; `soft_core_alpha` must be positive. Against OpenMM 8.6.1 on ethanol in TIP3P: $4.7\times10^{-7}$ kcal/mol with the reaction field, $2.8\times10^{-3}$ with PME on a grid of 32 (B-splines of order 4 against 5), $5\times10^{-5}$ on a grid of 80. `scripts/free-energy.py` gives TI and MBAR [[ShirtsChodera2008]](../references.md#shirtschodera2008). Checkpoints record the states and the state of the run; the fingerprint of D172 holds `[free_energy]`, so `--continue` refuses another state and a run from the checkpoint of another state evaluates its forces anew. At the state of the run the charges of the reciprocal sum are a field that the host computes, since a map over particles in the loop of the steps broke on a device where the particles are put in order (issue #26); `@alchemical` keeps the map, tested there (`free-energy-reorder-gpu.test`). The decoupled Lennard-Jones takes a plain cutoff or the potential shift, validated against OpenMM with the shift of the soft-core at the cutoff in σ; a switch is refused until it is validated, and at λ = 0 a run gives the potential energy of the run without `[free_energy]` to the bit, with a cutoff, PME, or the reaction field, with and without the shift.
