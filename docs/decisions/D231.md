# D231

Recorded 2026-10-08.

**The derivative of the energy in the charges with particle mesh Ewald: `md.reciprocal` yields, as an optional fourth result, the potential of the grid at the particles, $\partial E_\text{rec}/\partial q_i = \sum_{\mathbf k}\theta_i(\mathbf k)\phi(\mathbf k)$, gathered with the weights of the B-splines in the three templates of PME, only in a program compiled with the derivative; the host adds the derivatives of the self term and of the background.** Issue #203, after D230 (maintainer's decisions on PR #205: this route, Q4, and a decision of its own). $E_\text{rec}$ is a quadratic form of the charges, so its derivative is the potential, the sum that gives the force with the weights in place of their gradient and without the charge; D161's identity gives one number for a direction of $\mathbf q$ from two more sums, and $N$ charges need the gather. The differentiation in the field of the charges (D230) replaces the op of three results by one of four, which yields the same energy, forces, and virial; `@energy` and the steps keep the op of three. `md_exec.reciprocal` with `potential` yields the field in place of the forces, stored in f64 in every mode, and the grid of that sum is computed in f64, on a device as well. `@mdrt.pme_potential` and its `_triclinic` in `PME.mlir`, `@mdrt_gpu_pme_potential` in `PMEGPU.mlir` and `PMEGPUTriclinic.mlir`; a module takes them only where a sum asks, so a program without the derivative in the charges lowers to what it did. On the host, $-2f\beta q_i/\sqrt\pi$ of the self term and $-f\pi Q/(V\beta^2)$ of the background, the second proportional to $1/V$; the direct sum (shifted, D210), the excluded pairs, and the 1-4 pairs are sums that gather the charges. The GPU templates were extended as they are in the repository, not regenerated, since their script no longer generates them (#211); the script gains the kernel too. *Evidence.* On the CPU and a GPU, double and mixed: 25 tied entries on the dipeptide with a net charge of 0.5 e against central differences of the energy to $4.5\times10^{-10}$ and $8.5\times10^{-6}$, and in a triclinic cell (403 waters in a rhombic dodecahedron) to $1.0\times10^{-9}$ and $9.7\times10^{-6}$ (`python-tunable-gradient-pme*.test`); every one of 1,168 charges against torch-pme 0.5.0 [[Loche2025]](../references.md#loche2025) at the same positions, cell, charges, $\beta$, cutoff, and grid, by torch autograd, to $3.4\times10^{-5}$ of the largest at 64 points, within three times what the two change by with 128 (MDIR $1.3\times10^{-5}$, torch-pme $2.9\times10^{-5}$), and to $1.3\times10^{-6}$ at 128; the change of the energy for other charges, 117.66 kJ/mol, to $8.6\times10^{-4}$ (`python-tunable-gradient-torchpme*.test`, the feature `torchpme`). JAC without tunables runs at main's rate (NVE 852.0 against 852.1 ns/day, NPT 784.4 against 784.3, main at a529e28) and the modules and lowered IR of five programs without the derivative equal main's; with all 23,558 charges tunable `gradient()` takes 10.0 ms against 9.1 ms for `run(0, energy=True)`.
