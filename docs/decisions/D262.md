# D262

Recorded 2026-10-16.

**The reciprocal sums of PME spread only the particles whose coefficient can be nonzero, from a list of their places that a field of the particles holds and that is made again wherever the particles take a new order; a device gathers over the list where at most half the particles are in it, and every particle otherwise (`gather_all`).** Issue #339. *The list.* A field of `i32`, `pme_listed` for the charges and `ljpme_listed` for the coefficients $c_i$ of the dispersion (D162): the places of the listed particles in ascending order, then $-1-j$ for each other particle $j$ in ascending order. The builder makes it from the topology (`collectListed`); `emitReorder`, at the start and at each segment of checkpoints (and at any sort that #125 adds, which goes through it), makes it again with `md_exec.nonzero` from the permuted field instead of permuting it; `md.reciprocal ... listed(%l)` takes it. On a device `md_exec.nonzero` counts the listed particles of chunks of 256 places, sums the counts in one thread, and writes the entries of each chunk: integers only, so the list is the same in every mode; it takes 58 µs on 127,232 particles and 69 µs on 413,168 (RTX 4090), once a segment. *What is listed.* $q_i \ne 0$ of the topology, never of $q_i(1 - a_i\lambda)$: a zero charge is zero at every $\lambda$ and its share of $\partial U/\partial\lambda$ is zero (D161); a particle that $\lambda = 1$ decouples stays listed. $c_i \ne 0$ for the dispersion. A value that is not a number is listed. A program has a list where the topology has a coefficient of zero and no tunable may change one: a tunable charge disables the list of the charges, a tunable $\sigma$ or $\epsilon$ (of a type or a pair) that of the dispersion, since an update keeps the text of the program; tunables of other kinds leave it (conservative: a tunable charge that the map never puts on a particle of charge zero disables it too). Without a list the module is that of before: JAC, Factor IX, and STMV of the Amber suite, and TIP3P water, run the same kernels. *Kernels.* The listed kernels (`_listed`, generated after the others) take an entry where the others take a particle; a warp of the gathering whose first entry is past the last listed place skips the sum, before its shuffles, and stores the forces of zero of its entries, so that the gathering writes every force. The potential at the particles (D231) is gathered at every particle: it is not zero where the charge is. The host spreads and gathers over the list. *`gather_all`.* Where more than half the particles are listed the builder marks the sum, and a device spreads the listed particles and gathers every particle with the kernel without a list, whose force on a particle of charge zero is the product with zero. Measured first (127,232 sites of OPC, mixed precision, RTX 4090, the same job): the gathering over the list took 10.9 µs a step against 10.1 over every particle; the oxygen sits beside its virtual site in the order of the particles, a warp reads the grid points of both at once, and the oxygen costs almost nothing, while a warp of the list reads those of more waters. Over all entries of the list, in its order, it took 16.6. For the dispersion, where the oxygen alone is listed, the list pays: 8.5 µs against 10.1. Rejected: a test of each particle in the kernels (the oxygen is one site in four, so no warp is idle); the weights of order 4 at the entries of the list (no faster than at the places, 47.1 against 47.6 µs for the bricks, and a kernel of its own). *Validated* against `main` ae971477 (scripts in the PR): tri-alanine in 1,218 OPC waters (examples/ala3) and ethanol in TIP3P (Inputs/fep), 20 steps of NPT across a segment, with and without `[free_energy]` (decoupling, λ of the Coulomb 0.5), with LJPME, with `spatial_order` on and off: on the CPU in double and mixed precision and on a GPU in the deterministic mode in both, the checkpoints, every row of the log (energies, virial, pressure, volume), and the file of $\partial H/\partial\lambda$ are the same to the bit; outside the deterministic mode the first rows are the same to their digits and the states after 10 steps differ from `main` as two runs of `main` differ from each other (positions within 1.1e-7 nm against 8.5e-8 in mixed precision, 5.8e-15 against 3.3e-15 in double). `pme-skip-zero.test` pins the rows of `main` on the CPU. *Measured* (RTX 4090, mixed precision, 2 fs, NPT; 6 rounds alternated in one job, the first dropped; PME the kernels of the sums and the transforms under nsys), main → this: examples/ala3 110.0 → 109.9 µs a step (PME 24.4 → 24.0); ubiquitin in OPC, 26,031 particles, 223.3 → 219.9 (38.4 → 35.0), with LJPME 277.0 → 267.9 (73.9 → 65.0); 31,808 OPC waters 1075.6 → 1065.5 (105.8 → 94.3), with LJPME 1180.3 → 1138.8 (202.5 → 162.7); 103,292 OPC waters 3177.6 → 3151.0 (274.0 → 241.3, against 225.2 for 103,292 TIP3P waters); Cellulose 2552.4 → 2553.2 (321.8 → 317.4; 7.4% of its atoms have no charge, `gather_all`); JAC 147.4 → 147.9 and TIP3P 1926.9 → 1926.0 with the same module as `main`, the spread of two builds of one code.
