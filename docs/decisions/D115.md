# D115

Recorded 2026-10-02.

**The lists of groups keep an entry for each image of a candidate within the reach, not only the image nearest to the center of the group.** An entry carries one shift for its 16 members, and the build took the image of each candidate nearest to the center $\mathbf c$ of the box of the group, of half-widths $\mathbf h$; a member is within $h_a$ of $c_a$, so an image of the candidate within the reach $R$ of a member is within $R + h_a$ of $c_a$, and it is the nearest image only if $R + h_a \le L_a/2$. In a narrower cell the lists missed pairs: on the argon-krypton mixture of `barostat.test` (23.2 Å, $R$ = 10 Å, groups about 9 Å wide) the potential energy at the start was $-497.7291$ kcal/mol against $-497.7298$ of the matrix and the virial $-1117.6307$ against $-1117.7782$, 0.31 kcal/mol apart with a plain cutoff; the same mixture replicated 2 × 2 × 2 agreed to every digit. A second kernel, after the list kernel, takes the groups with $2(h_a + R) \ge L_a$ along some axis, the only ones whose candidates can have an image on the other side of the boundary within the reach of the box; it searches their candidates again, queues each with the sides on which its image is within $R$ of the box (bits 28 to 30 of the entry of the queue, the distance $L_a - \lvert r_a\rvert - h_a$ along a side), and appends an entry for each such image, with its own mask and shift, to the list of the group from the block where the list kernel left it (its number in the free column 6 of the box). With edges longer than $2R$ a pair has at most one image within the reach, so the masks of the entries of a candidate are disjoint and each pair is still in one list once. The shift of an entry is now $-4$ to $4$ an axis, as $\mathbf e + 4$ in four bits in bits 16 to 27 of its mask (D95, amended). A first version handled both in the list kernel: it took 128 registers a thread instead of 64 and the build of Cellulose 2259 µs instead of 1735 (RTX 3090), 1.4% of the rate; apart, the list kernel keeps its 64 registers (1760 µs) and the second kernel is warps that return in the cells of the Amber suite. The test of the template adds a cell of 4.0 with a reach of 1.9: 896,816 pairs, none missing or duplicated, none in the wrong image (the build before missed 16,302); `groups-gpu.test` compares the groups and the matrix on the mixture over 100 steps, identical to every printed digit. The rates of the suite do not change (RTX 3090, old and new alternating, two runs each): JAC 767.4 and 765.7 ns/day against 766.8 and 768.3, Cellulose 62.6 and 62.6 against 62.7 and 62.4, STMV 41.3 and 41.3 against 41.5 and 41.4. *Amended by D242: the second kernel takes the 27 images a cell away or none along each axis, not the 8 of the nearest or the other side, which were every image within the reach only while $h_a + R \le L_a$ (#263).*
