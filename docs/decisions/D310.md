# D310

Recorded 2026-10-21.

**The physical lowering of `md_dist.halo_copy` and `md_dist.halo_sum` may expose a `mdrt.halo_copy_start` or `mdrt.halo_sum_start` event and a matching `mdrt.halo_wait` that yields the completed destination buffer version.** #490, a slice of #481. With `halo-events=true` on either `--convert-md-dist-to-loops` or `--convert-md-dist-to-md-exec` (false by default), each transfer emits adjacent start and wait ops. The verifier requires a single matching wait in the same block, distinct direct allocations, no buffer or handle use between start and wait, destination consumption through the completed result, and deallocation only after completion; unproved aliases and control flow are refused. `--lower-mdrt-halo-events` lowers the start to the existing synchronous transport and the wait to its destination, preserving the numerical path. The events provide a dependency and lifetime seam; placement of interior work and asynchronous transport remain in #481. No control-file key or output change. *Validation*: the LJ and EAM integration programs compare energy, forces, and virial against one domain in `double` and `mixed` on the CPU and GPU; invalid lifetime and matching cases are verifier tests.
