# D274

Recorded 2026-10-16.

**Steepest descent ends at the first row of the log whose next step has shrunk below a floor: $10^{-6}$ of the cap of 0.1 Å below double precision ($10^{-7}$ Å) and $10^{-10}$ of it in double ($10^{-11}$ Å), with a line at the end that gives the step, the floor, and the largest force; the exit status is 0, and the checkpoint is written at that row.** Issue #369. *Ruled* (the maintainer, 2026-10-16, on PR #376): end the run at the floor, with or without `force_tolerance`; exit status 0; these floors; `stalled` in Python, checked at the end of each part without a tolerance. A trial that does not lower the energy shrinks the step by 0.2 and one that does grows it by 1.2 (D73); where the trials no longer lower the energy by more than its rounding, or its noise, they are taken or not at random, the logarithm of the step falls by about 0.7 a trial ($\tfrac12\ln 1.2 + \tfrac12\ln 0.2$), and the step reaches 0. With a term of `[[energy.model]]` (D272) on 81 flexible waters the step was 0.000000 Å from step 13,000 of 40,000, a largest force of 0.79 kcal/mol/Å against a tolerance of 0.23, and the other 27,000 evaluations changed nothing: the decrease of a trial along the stiff O–H stretch, about $10^{-3}$ kcal/mol, approached the spread of the model's energy between evaluations, $2\times10^{-4}$. The floor must lie below every step at which a trial still lowers the energy; below it, the length matters little, since each factor of 10 costs about 3 trials of the walk to 0. Below double precision the terms take the positions in f32 (D79), whose spacing at 1 Å is $1.2\times10^{-7}$ Å: a shorter step moves no coordinate above 1 Å as the terms see it. In double, $10^{-11}$ Å is where GROMACS ends its steepest descent ($10^{-12}$ nm; $10^{-6}$ nm in single precision), from a first step equal to the cap [[Abraham2015]](../references.md#abraham2015). Measured (CPU, the dipeptide in water of D65 with restraints, rows every step): with PME the least step of 1000 steps is $6.6\times10^{-3}$ Å in mixed and in double, far above either floor; with a plain cutoff, whose energy is not continuous, the step first falls below the floor at step 26 (mixed) and 40 (double), and the 274 and 260 steps after it lower the energy by $4.3\times10^{-4}$ and 0 kcal/mol of 5931. A first step of `initial_step` below the floor ends the run only once it has shrunk below its first length. The check is the host's, at the rows where it reads the forces anyway (D219), so a stalled run takes at most `energy_interval` evaluations more; a Python simulation checks the row at the end of each part, and its row of `state().minimization` has `stalled`. `minimize-stalled.test` and `-gpu.test`, `python-minimize-stalled.test` and `-gpu.test` ([dynamics.md](../dynamics.md), Section 4; paper Section 6.7). *Amends D73.*
