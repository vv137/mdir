# D216

Recorded 2026-10-07.

**The control structure holds the constants of restraints in kJ/mol/nm², converted from the control file's kcal/mol/Å² once where `[[restraints]]` is read; a typed Python restraint passes its constant through unchanged.** Issue #100, the follow-up of the review of D198. Before, the control structure held kcal/mol/Å², so a typed constant went to that unit and back, through a search of the neighbors of the converted value for one that converts back exactly. The file's constant is converted by the expression that the preparation used ($k \cdot 4.184 / 0.1^2$), so runs from a control file are unchanged; a typed constant is now that of the prepared system exactly, where before one not computed from a control-file value could differ by one rounding. *Evidence.* The file/object parity of `test/Driver/ModelTest.cpp` stays bitwise, and typed constants of 4184, 1000, and 0.1 kJ/mol/nm² are kept exactly. On the CPU, a minimization and an NPT run with three overlapping restraints (`CENTER` and `ALL`) of ff19SB in OPC give energy logs and trajectories identical to main's in mixed and double precision.
