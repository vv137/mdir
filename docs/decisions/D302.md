# D302

Recorded 2026-10-20.

**On a device, `md-exec-lower-stage-bodies` lowers every op of a batched body of the shape of a GEMM in f32 to a call of cuBLAS's `cublasSgemm` through the runtime (`mdrtGemmF32`), on the stream of the kernels and in their order, in cuBLAS's default math mode (no TF32), in both precision modes: one reduced loop, the result's last dimension the columns and the others its rows (several are one), the operands read by the rows and the reduced loop and by the reduced loop and the columns, either of them transposed (A only with a single rows dimension), a payload of multiply and add, buffers of contiguous rows. A product that starts from a fill of zero calls with beta 0 instead of the fill's kernel; the weights that a product reads are copied to the device by the runtime when first read (`mdrtDeviceConstant`), each copy owned by the activation that made it (or by none outside one) and freed with it: when the activation closes, when a program unloads its modules (for those made outside an activation), and, with the handle of cuBLAS, when the device changes; a host address names the weights only while their program is loaded (the review of #472). The option `contractions=gemm\|loops` of the pass, `gemm` by default, and `MDIR_STAGE_CONTRACTIONS` choose the strategy (#444); `mdrt_cuda` links cuBLAS.** #462 (#377, step 4b); the maintainer's ruling on #462: products of the shape of a GEMM go to cuBLAS, not to schedules of MDIR's own; AOTInductor's bodies are measured against them; MDIR's own schedules only for contractions that neither serves. On an RTX 4090, cuBLAS takes 13 to 23 µs for the products of PaiNN's bodies at 4,096 rows, against 286 and 712 µs for D293's loops; nablaDFT's PaiNN makes 106 calls (369 kernels against 532), `edge.b` goes from 1.22 to 0.35 ms per call, `node.b` from 4.2 to 0.31, the other bodies to 0.015 to 0.13 ms, and a step from 3,193 to 2,160 ms at 6,144 atoms (without the tile sums of #464; `loops` gives D299's times). Validated against the JAX composition (81 atoms, full virial) and SchNetPack in float64, double and mixed, and NVE from one state. A product with a batch (a loop that indexes the result and both operands, the result's first dimension) is one call of `cublasSgemmStridedBatched` through the same `mdrtGemmF32`, which takes the batch and the distances of the matrices; the layout of each matrix is read from its buffer, a product whose buffers cuBLAS cannot take stays loops, and a sum that starts from zero at its first reduced iteration calls with beta 0 (the maintainer's ruling on #472: in `edge.b`, $y_{r,c} = \sum_f x_{r,f} v_{r,c,f}$, 4,096 products of $3\times128$ by $128\times1$, 22.8 µs as a kernel; `edge.b` from 0.364 to 0.345 ms per call). The sums over the three components of a vector, $\sum_c a_{r,c,f} b_{r,c,f}$, are not matrix products ($f$ is a second batch dimension that no single stride joins to $r$) and stay loops.
