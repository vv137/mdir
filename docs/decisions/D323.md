# D323

Recorded 2026-10-22.

**Read common finite decimal `.gro` coordinate and velocity fields with direct binary64 conversion; retain the general LLVM reader when it cannot consume a complete finite field.** #525, part of #520. The fixed-column width, accepted numeric grammar, errors, control-file keys, output, and overwrite behavior stay the same. An independent Python decimal conversion agrees bitwise on 1,536 coordinate and velocity fields; 300,000 random coordinate values from the old and new readers are bitwise identical, and malformed fields retain their line-level error. The analytic 2-argon kinetic energy is 1.8108 kcal/mol on the CPU and GPU in mixed and double precision (1.8108 reference, 0.0000 difference at the printed 4 decimals). On one RTX 4090, STMV from the GROMACS topology, 1,000 steps in mixed precision, first energy row on main and this branch: ×1 (1,067,095 atoms, 73,629,636 input bytes) 19.375 → 14.223 s, ×11 (11,738,045 atoms, 809,925,187 bytes) 103.277 → 45.680 s. The compiled part remains about 10 s in both; these are one sequential run each. The complete parsed positions and velocities of ×1 and ×11 have matching SHA-256 hashes against main, including all 11,738,045 particles of ×11. In separate single-process CPU reads, topology plus coordinates took 8.64 → 3.34 s (×1) and 86.39 → 30.17 s (×11). The 1,000-step rates in the same GPU timing job were 4.23 → 4.15 ms/step and 57.49 → 57.62 ms/step, respectively, on main 1817dc5d against this branch. The final suite is recorded in the PR.
