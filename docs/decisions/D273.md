# D273

Recorded 2026-10-16.

**A second backend of the plugin of D270, `backend = "AOTI"`, loads an AOTInductor package of the `nequip` package's `ase` target (`.nequip.pt2`) with `torch::inductor::AOTIModelPackageLoader` and runs it on MDIR's stream with the plugin's own pair list (edges, cell shifts, and cell in float64, as the package takes them whatever `model_dtype` is); the forces and the stress are the package's own (the `nequip` package's `ForceStressOutput`, inside the compiled graph), the virial $\mathsf W = -V\sigma$; chunks of centers pass the pairs of the chunk and sum `atomic_energy` over its centers, for a package that declares itself local (`mdir::local_centers`, written by `scripts/mlip/nequip-to-aoti.py` for Allegro); a package without kernels for the device (`AOTI_COMPUTE_CAPABILITY`, or the list `mdir::archs` of a package of several architectures) is refused.** Issue #373, step 2 of ML1 (#137, D270). No key is added. *Ruled* (the maintainer on PR #374, 2026-10-16): one `path`, refused where the package has no kernels for the device (one package for several with `--arch`); a package without `mdir::length_unit` and `mdir::energy_unit` is taken as Å and eV, and the log says so. *Scope*: an AOTInductor package has no standard calling convention; `AOTI` takes the `nequip` package's (`--target ase`) for NequIP or Allegro and refuses any other; another framework's export would take a thin adapter of its own over the shared pair list, buffers, stream, units, and declared effects, and a metatomic with `torch.export` should in time take such packages under `METATOMIC`. The package's host code is compiled for x86-64-v3 by the script, and a package for a processor that the host lacks is refused (AVX-512 on AVX2 stopped with an illegal instruction). `MDIR_MLIP_TIMING=1` times the pair search apart. *Validation* ([metatomic-ml1.md](../metatomic-ml1.md#1111-validation)), RTX 4090 and RTX PRO 6000, `mixed` and `double`: against the `nequip` package's calculator evaluating the Allegro model on four boxes, energy within $1.1 \times 10^{-3}$ kJ/mol, forces $3.7 \times 10^{-3}$ kJ/mol/nm, virial $3.3 \times 10^{-3}$ kJ/mol, the level of METATOMIC; AOTI against METATOMIC within $5.4 \times 10^{-4}$, $4.2 \times 10^{-3}$, $1.5 \times 10^{-3}$; chunks of 1 to 64 centers within the same; NVE over 2 ps drifts by $-0.80$ (`double`) and $-1.35$ (`mixed`) kJ/mol/ns per atom with 0.15 kJ/mol of fluctuation, 15.4 ms per step against 86; 132 kB per pair (85 with the Triton kernel) against 1.22 MB, 3.4 µs per pair on the RTX 4090 (2.2 with Triton, 3.0 and 2.0 on the RTX PRO 6000) against 20.7: 1,840 to 3,680 atom-steps per second, 0.9 to 1.7 ns/day at 0.5 fs for 96 atoms; whole systems up to 1,152 (1,920 with Triton) atoms on 24 GB and 4,608 (7,680) on 96 GB, and in chunks 12,288 atoms in 9.4 GiB and 24,576 in 38 GiB; the pair search, $O(N^2)$, is 1 to 2% of an evaluation up to 3,072 atoms and 9 to 17% at 12,288 to 24,576.
