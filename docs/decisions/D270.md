# D270

Recorded 2026-10-16.

**Learned potentials enter MDIR through an optional plugin, `libmdir_metatomic` (`-DMDIR_ENABLE_METATOMIC`), a C++20 library with RTTI and exceptions that links libtorch and metatomic-torch and is reached from the core only through a versioned C ABI; ML1 evaluates the Allegro model `mir-group/Allegro-OAM-L:0.1` (CC-BY-4.0), made a metatomic artifact by `scripts/mlip/nequip-to-metatomic.py`, against the `nequip` package's ASE calculator (`NequIPCalculator`), evaluating the Allegro model, and the device memory per pair is its primary measure.** Issue #137, M3 (D166, D167, D169). *Ruled* (the maintainer, 2026-10-16): Allegro first through a wrapper of the upstream checkpoint, since no Allegro model has an official metatomic export; the reference is the `nequip` package's calculator; memory is a primary result; the accelerated path is designed now; the plugin is C++20 behind a C ABI. *Measured* (outside the repository, torch 2.14.1, metatomic-torch 0.1.19, nequip 0.19.1, RTX 4090 and RTX PRO 6000): with float64 inputs the artifact agrees with the `nequip` package's calculator to $2.6 \times 10^{-6}$ eV/Å in the forces and $4.8 \times 10^{-6}$ eV in the virial, the spread of that calculator's own evaluations ($2.5 \times 10^{-6}$ eV/Å), and a plain-C host calling the plugin through `dlopen` on its own stream and buffers agrees to the same level; the eager model takes 1.22 MB of device memory per pair (one transient temporary of the tensor contraction, not the 97 kB kept for the backward), so 24 GB holds 10,546 pairs; chunks of 16 or 64 central atoms (`selected_atoms`, exact for a strictly local model) hold the peak at 2.9 and 11 GiB up to 456,424 pairs; the `nequip` package's AOTInductor export takes 132 kB per pair, and with its Triton kernel 85 kB and 8.6 times less time. *Ruled on PR #364* (the maintainer, 2026-10-16): `[[energy.model]]` with `backend`, `path`, and `centers_per_call`; the plugin's own pair list with images first; inputs in the model's dtype; TorchScript with chunks from the control file and from Python first, then AOTInductor, the pair list from MDIR's structures, and ML4's stages; the deterministic mode refuses a model term; GPU only first. *Built* ([metatomic-ml1.md](../metatomic-ml1.md#9-what-the-first-pr-built-d270)): `[[energy.model]]` (`backend = "METATOMIC"`) and `mdir.System.models` (`mdir.ModelTerm`); the plugin builds a full, strict pair list with every image on the device each step, differentiates in f64 positions and a strain, adds its forces into MDIR's own buffer of forces in place, through a host call that declares the write as md_exec's loops do (`mdrt.host_call ... outs(%f)` yields the forces with the model's, then its energy and virial, which the program adds to the potential and the virial; the call reads the positions and the cell), so that no pass can schedule or fuse a reader of the forces before it (*ruled*, the maintainer, 2026-10-16: the dependence is stated, not worked around); chunks with `selected_atoms` for a model that declares `mdir::local_centers`; refused: the CPU, the deterministic mode, a barostat, `[minimize]`, `[free_energy]`, `observe`, tunables, constraints, virtual sites, more than one model. *Validation* (RTX 4090, the `nequip` package's calculator as the reference, four boxes of water including a 6.2 Å cell): with float64 inputs, in `double` and `mixed`, the forces within $3.2 \times 10^{-3}$ kJ/mol/nm, the virial within $1.7 \times 10^{-3}$ kJ/mol, the energy within $6.4 \times 10^{-4}$ kJ/mol (tolerances $1.9 \times 10^{-2}$, $4.8 \times 10^{-3}$, $4.8 \times 10^{-3}$); float32 inputs within $8.2 \times 10^{-3}$, $7.2 \times 10^{-3}$, and $2.8 \times 10^{-3}$ (tolerances ten times those); chunks of 1, 16, and 64 centers the same; `mdir run` and the Python model agree to the log's four decimals over 20 steps; 2 ps of NVE drift by $-0.87$ kJ/mol/ns per atom with a fluctuation of 0.13 kJ/mol; the whole system takes 1.22 MB per pair (13,602 pairs on 24 GB with `expandable_segments`), 64 centers per call 11.6 GiB and 16 centers 3.1 GiB at 456,886 pairs; 20.3 µs per pair whole and 20.8 µs with 64 centers (323 atom-steps per second at 3,072 atoms).
