# D180

Recorded 2026-10-04.

**An approximate f32 quotient rounds before subsequent arithmetic on the GPU.** Amend D98: lowering `a / b` to `a * rcp.approx.ftz(b)` clears only `contract` on the quotient product. Fusing it into the cutoff subtraction changed the shifted soft-core Lennard-Jones derivative by about $5.5\times10^{-4}$ kcal/mol (issue #48). The reciprocal remains approximate and other products may contract. On ethanol in TIP3P, CPU and GPU in mixed and double match the OpenMM 8.6.1 Reference derivatives (3.524760, 3.448651, and 3.279802 kcal/mol) at the six printed decimal places; the mixed regression tolerance is $10^{-5}$ kcal/mol. Fixed-Coulomb vdW energy differences agree within $10^{-6}$ kcal/mol. A single 30,000-step JAC GPU run measured 815.0 ns/day before and 815.2 after over its latter half; this is not a repeated performance estimate.
