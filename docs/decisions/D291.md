# D291

Recorded 2026-10-19.

**A scatter-accumulate of its own in `md_exec`, `md_exec.scatter_add %values at %indices into %dest` with `overwrite(...)` and `scatter(...)`, for values that no loop computes in registers; the destinations of a loop over pairs that adds at both ends of a pair take `scatter(...)` as those of a loop over tuples do; and the table of a block, `hash`, is one emitter that every site calls.** Step 3 of issue #416 (#415, #444; [device-primitives.md](../device-primitives.md), Section 2; white paper, Section 8.5). *Ruled* (the maintainer on PR #445, 2026-10-19): the textual form as proposed; a combiner other than addition later as `combine(...)`, with #422; the op now, with its tests, before its first site, `halo_sum` of #443. *The op*: $a'_i = a_i + \sum_{k : \mathrm{idx}_k = i} v_k$; values and indices on one set, the indices `i32`, the destination of the values' components and type; the storage form updates the destination where it is, `overwrite(true)` from zeros; `atomic` and `hash` taken, `tile` (no bounded footprint) and `sorted` (#417) refused; on the CPU a loop with plain additions on one thread or atomic ones in a parallel loop, on a device a thread a value with device atomics or the table; `md-exec-assign-precision` does not take it yet. *Why both forms*: on `main` every site of step 3 (EAM's density, the $j$ forces of the groups, the edge sums of #441) is the output of a loop, whose values a separate op would have to store and read again; only `halo_sum` and a stored edge sum have values outside a loop. *The emitter*: `kernels::BlockHashTable` and the helpers of block memory (`ScatterEmitter.h`); the tuples' table of D283 moved onto it with no change of generated code (the module after `convert-md-exec-to-gpu` and the lowered program with its PTX byte-identical to `main`'s on JAC NVE and NPT, Factor IX NVE, Cellulose NVE); PME's tile keeps its template. *The loops over pairs*: `pair_for` takes `hash` only with the conflict `atomic`, over groups (the unique traversal) and over a matrix (D290's `(directed, atomic)`); `md-exec-choose-scatter{pair-hash}`, `MDIR_PAIR_SUMS`. Measured on an RTX 4090, the table took 1.18 (JAC), 1.24 (Factor IX), and 1.30 (Cellulose) times the step over groups, 1.23 and 1.32 with every pruning in the loop, and 1.49 (mixed) and 1.05 (double) times an evaluation of EAM on 262,144 particles, since the entries of a block are distinct particles; and 2.06 (mixed) and 1.68 (double) times the two-ended loops of EAM on one domain of 110,592 particles over a matrix, whose rows share entries, since the device's atomics to different addresses are cheap and the table's lookup is paid on every value: **off by default** at both, kept as a switch. No new site measured faster; the table pays where atomics contend (the tuples). *Validated*: EAM over groups on a device with each strategy against `eam_reference.py` (energy, four force components, the virial's trace and xy, to $10^{-10}$ in double and $10^{-5}$ in mixed); on 262,144 particles the table against the atomics to $10^{-10}$ (mixed, the spread of two runs with atomics) and $3\times10^{-16}$ (double) in energy, virial, and $\sum F^2$; `scatter_add` exactly against a sum written out, on one thread, under OpenMP, and on a device with each strategy; memcheck, initcheck, racecheck of the groups' table and memcheck, racecheck of the matrix's and the op's; the two-ended loops of `dist-eam-exec.mlir` with the table against its references, double and mixed; the Amber suite against `main` in one job on an RTX 4090, step-time ratios 1.000 (JAC NVE, Factor IX NVE, Cellulose NVE) and 0.999 (STMV NPT 4 fs); the default generates the same code as `main` (the modules of the Amber systems, `lj-forces.mlir`, `obc.mlir`, and the distributed `dist-eam-exec.mlir` byte-identical).
