# D254

Recorded 2026-10-14.

**An update of the tunables writes its values, in place, into a model that the simulation keeps for its updates; whether a tuple set is disjoint is found in one pass over its members; and a build computes each integral of the tail of a pair term once.** Issue #324, amends D213, under which an update copied the model of the compile and built the program from the copy, and follows D253, which had left an update at 8.3, 30, and 203 ms on JAC, Factor IX, and Cellulose of the Amber suite, most of it not its two starts. *Measured first* (`main` b12ee87 with marks around each part, `Simulation._host_times`; `scripts/benchmarks/activation/start.py --only update --marks --tunable KIND`; one RTX 4090, mixed precision, PME, flexible; `update()` and `gradient()`, ms, the least of six). With the length of a soft pair term tunable the call took 8.39, 30.9, and 205 ms, of which the rebuild of the program 4.19, 18.1, and 143 ms and the two starts 4.14, 12.6, and 61.2 ms. Of the rebuild: the copies of the control and the system 0.07, 0.55, and 2.27 ms; the copy of the topology in `applyTunables` 0.20, 1.44, and 8.35 ms; the fields of the particles 0.08, 0.36, and 1.53 ms; the tuple sets 0.72, 2.17, and 21.6 ms; the constants that the host adds 0.04, 0.13, and 0.53 ms; whether each tuple set is disjoint, a sort of a copy of its members, 2.69, 12.9, and 108 ms; the text 0.34, 0.41, and 0.43 ms; its comparison with the compiled one, with the free of the copies, 0.03, 0.12, and 0.49 ms. So the comparison and the text were nothing, and what an update built anew beside its values was a question about the members of the tuples (64%, 71%, and 75% of the rebuild) and two copies of a model of which it changes one number. Of the two starts: the arguments of the entry 0.92, 3.81, and 23.1 ms (#326), the copy of the state from the device 0.75, 2.53, and 11.8 ms, the program (the uploads, the order, the kept structures, the evaluation) 2.34, 5.97, and 25.9 ms; the copies to and from the device, by the counters of the device runtime, 1.35, 3.95, and 20.2 ms of the call. On the CPU target the rebuild took 6.84, 31.8, and 220 ms on the login node, whose allocator maps the copies anew (34,500 page faults for the copy of the topology of Cellulose). By kind of tunable the rebuild took, for a constant of a pair term, 4.19, 18.1, and 143 ms; for the same with the tail of the term in the correction for the dispersion (D209), 120, 186, and 199 ms, of which 114, 164, and 40 ms the quadratures of the tail, computed for every pair of classes of particles at every evaluation of the tail (for the correction, and for each point of the Richardson derivatives of D230), though the expression a exp(-r/l) reads neither the types nor the charges; for per-type σ and ε with the correction 4.75, 19.8, and 156 ms; for the charges 4.46, 18.8, and 148 ms; for a parameter of a tuple term over every bond 7.98, 37.0, and 236 ms. *The remedy.* (1) `Program::TupleSet::hasNoRepeat`: one pass over the members with a mark for each particle, for every build. (2) A simulation copies the control and the system of its compile once, at its first update, with a topology that nothing else holds, and `model::applyTunablesInPlace` writes the values into it. The sites that an update writes are fixed by the declarations and not by the values (every site of every tunable, the pairs of types that the combining rule or a pair tunable gives, the weights of the seeds, the tails), and each is written from the values given and the values of the types that the declarations hold, never from what the model held: so the kept model after an update is the model that `applyTunables` makes of the model of the compile, whatever the updates before it, a refused one included, and nothing is undone after a refusal. With `MDRT_CHECK_KEPT_TUPLES` every update also builds its program from a copy of the model of the compile, as before, and compares the two member for member and to the bit (`driver::describeDifference`); a difference fails the update, names what differs, and ends the use of the kept model. The variable of D253 is extended and no second one added. (3) The integral of a tail and its value at the cutoff are functions of the values of the names that the expression reads and of the cutoff; a build keeps each under those values, compared as bits, and takes it for every pair of classes and every point of a derivative that asks for the same, to the bit the number computed each time before. The memo is the build's and ends with it: nothing of a tail is kept from one update to the next, so the values that a fit passes through leave nothing behind (#300). *Measured after* (one RTX 4090, mixed precision, `main` and this decision in turn six times in one job, a process for each run; `update()` and `gradient()`, ms, the mean of the least of eight updates over the five rounds after the first, standard deviations of at most 2%): a constant of a pair term 8.43 to 5.38 on JAC, 30.8 to 15.9 on Factor IX, 204 to 86.9 on Cellulose; with its tail in the correction 121 to 8.11, 189 to 22.1, 259 to 112; per-type σ and ε 9.31 to 6.19, 33.8 to 19.0, 223 to 105; the charges 9.00 to 5.88, 32.6 to 18.0, 214 to 96.9; a parameter of a tuple term 12.9 to 9.35, 53.1 to 32.8, 308 to 171; all four 127 to 13.4, 216 to 43.3, 376 to 212. The rebuild inside an update of a constant of a pair term is now 1.19, 3.20, and 25.0 ms, most of it the arrays of the program built from the topology; for a parameter of a tuple term 4.51, 17.0, and 100 ms, most of it the numbering of the tuples of each member for the derivative (a map over the tuples), which depends on the members alone. A start alone (`gradient()`) is unchanged: 2.17 to 2.10, 6.17 to 6.14, 31.1 to 30.9 ms. The rate of a plain run is unchanged: 20,000 steps in one part, the builds in turn six times in one `--idle` job, the five rounds after the first, 1.9082 (standard deviation 0.0004) against 1.9075 (0.0005) ms a step on Cellulose, 0.3879 (0.0002) against 0.3878 (0.0001) on Factor IX, 0.1176 (0.0003) against 0.1176 (0.0002) on JAC. What keeping the arrays that no value enters would still gain, measured in the same job with the prototype: 86.9 to 64.1 ms on Cellulose for a constant of a pair term, 171 to 74.5 for a parameter of a tuple term, 212 to 118 for all four; 5.38 to 4.54 and 15.9 to 12.8 on JAC and Factor IX. *Not changed.* Every array, every constant, and the whole text of the program are built at every update by the code of the compile, and the text is compared with the compiled one as before; the module that the builder emits and the lowered module are identical to the byte to those of `main` for JAC, Factor IX, and Cellulose of the Amber suite, NVE and NPT (`mdir emit --stage=module` and `--stage=lowered`, 166 kB to 8.1 MB), so no entry of the compile cache moves; no key, no format, no result. *Not taken here:* keeping the arrays of the program that no value of a tunable enters, found by a build from a model whose tunable sites hold numbers that are not numbers, and shared between programs (a prototype; a mechanism of its own with its own invariants, left to a second change by the design review of PR #325); the arguments of a start (#326); a memo of the tails across updates. *Memory.* One model for a simulation from its first update on, by the pages that its copy faults in 13 MB on JAC, 33 MB on Factor IX, and 150 MB on Cellulose, which every update allocated and freed before; none for a simulation that is never updated. *Validated.* A sequence on the dipeptide in water with the charges, per-type σ and ε, both constants of a pair term whose tail is in the correction, and the constants of a tuple term tunable (an update of each alone, of all, and back, which gives the first derivative again to the bit; updates between steps; a commit of a borrow; `gradient()`; an update refused by the build after its values were written, and the one after it; a checkpoint and a run continued from it; a frame evaluator beside its sampler), in the deterministic mode, in double and mixed precision, with the comparison of the variable at every update: 203 states, forces, energies, and derivatives in each precision equal to the bit to those of a simulation whose updates copy the model of the compile (`Simulation._keep_model`, private), and, as 406 records, to those of `main`, on the CPU and on a GPU (RTX 4090), where the 103 tests of the Python simulation on a GPU pass at the head; a number of the kept model written over by the test, which the comparison finds and names (`python-update-cost*.test`).
