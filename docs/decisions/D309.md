# D309

Recorded 2026-10-21.

**A full-suite validation on Slurm may use two disjoint lit shards on independent build trees of one commit when two GPU allocations are available.** #489 (#485; [workflow](../workflow.md), contributing guide in the white paper). Each shard runs through `suite-run --batch` with `--num-shards=2` and its own `--run-shard`; both exit successfully and their test selections together cover the suite. Record combined passed, unsupported, and failed counts. The option changes scheduling only: no test, numerical oracle, control key, output format, or overwrite behavior changes. On two RTX 4090 allocations of 16 cores each, 713 tests completed with 685 passed, 28 unsupported, and zero failed in 17:52 from first start to last end; recent one-allocation suites took 34:06–35:27. This trades a second GPU and 16-core allocation for shorter elapsed time, without a claim of less total resource use. *Amended by D[shard-order].*
