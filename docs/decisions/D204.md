# D204

Recorded 2026-10-06.

**In the deterministic mode a step that writes energies moves the particles as a step that does not, with constraints and PME, on the CPU and on devices, and a Python simulation of a model gives the state of `mdir run` within a part.** #102 and #105 had three causes. (1) `md-exec-assign-precision` gave every field stored together with a buffer of the boundary the type of that buffer: the forces that a loop of steps carries reached the f32 buffer of a host call where the loop's result did (every step writing energies, or the program of segments) and were f64 otherwise, so the trajectory depended on the schedule of energies and on the front end (in mixed precision with PME, where the total force is a sum of a loop over particles). In the deterministic mode a buffer now reaches the fields through a loop over particles that copies it (`deterministic` of the pass), and the buffers of forces are f64 in mixed precision, so that a checkpoint holds the forces that the steps carry. (2) The kernel that joins the loops of the constraints with the loops over particles around them (D110) took each member's position relative to the member at place 0, narrowed, so a displacement between two other members (the H–H of SETTLE) was the difference of two narrowed numbers; a step of energy, whose virial reads the changes of the constraints, ran the loops alone and rounded otherwise. In the deterministic mode the joined kernel takes the stored positions. (3) D201 stopped contraction into fused multiply-adds on devices; now every sum or difference in a device kernel with a product as an operand becomes a `math.fma` (`explicit-fma` of `convert-md-exec-to-gpu`), whatever else uses the product, so the rounding follows the formula, not the count of uses. *Evidence.* The dipeptide in water, 20 steps with a row of energies at every step against one at the end: equal bit for bit in positions, velocities, and forces with cutoff electrostatics and PME, with and without SHAKE and SETTLE, in mixed and double precision, on the CPU and a GPU (`energy-interval.test`, `energy-interval-gpu.test`); before, the CPU differed with PME in mixed precision (velocities 1.1e-4 nm/ps without constraints) and the GPU with constraints (1.6e-6) and with PME (4.7e-5). A Python simulation of 20 steps in one part with PME equals `mdir run` bit for bit on the CPU in mixed and double precision (`python-deterministic-run.test`, now `python-deterministic-run-*.test`; 1.1e-6 nm/ps before). JAC, NVE, mixed, deterministic, one RTX 3090: 0.326 ms per step against 0.325 on main; not joining the kernels of the constraints instead cost 0.347, and no contraction instead of explicit fused multiply-adds 0.347 against 0.341. The default mode is unchanged (0.203 against 0.205); explicit fused multiply-adds there measured 0.206 against LLVM's contraction, 0.204. *Open.* The program of segments and that of `mdir run` still part at the rounding of the sums with cutoff electrostatics from step 20 and on a device from the first step, in double precision as well (#121). *Resolved by D218.* *Amended 2026-10-09 (#240):* a fourth cause, with a term over the centers of groups (D139). `md-exec-accumulate-destinations` let the loops of the terms accumulate their forces onto one another only while the loops stood in the order of the sum. A loop that gives the energy of its term with its forces stands where the energy is added (`md-exec-fuse-loops`), and the forces of a term over centers need the derivative of its energy at its sums, so in a step of energy their loop stood after the loop of the 1-4 pairs, which the sum adds after it: the chain stopped, the 1-4 pairs kept a field of their own, and the loop over particles added it in f64 to the f32 sum of the terms before it, where a step without energies accumulated it in f32. The forces on the atoms with 1-4 pairs were rounded otherwise, and the run depended on `energy_interval` in mixed precision (not in double, where both are f64 sums in one order; not under NPT with `work = "EXACT"` or `"FIRST_ORDER"`, where every step computes the virial and has the loops of a step of energy). A loop that stands before the loop of the term before it in the sum now moves down to follow it, with the pure ops between the two that read its sums (the totals of the energies and of the virials), where the other loop reads none of them; the terms then accumulate in the order of the sum in every kind of step. Not a switch of the deterministic mode: a step of energy stores one field less in every mode (the dipeptide with the term in the default mode, mixed, one RTX 3090, 40,000 steps: 0.332 ms per step against 0.332 with a row of the log at every step, and 0.160 against 0.162 with one every 10 steps). *Evidence.* The dipeptide in water with PME, SHAKE, and SETTLE and a harmonic term between the centers of two waters, 40 steps with the rows of the log every 10 steps against the end only: equal bit for bit in positions, velocities, and forces at constant energy, at constant temperature, and under NPT with each `work` of the barostat, in mixed precision on the CPU and a GPU (the comparison of the rows of the log at the end only with those of every 10 steps in `output-independence*.test`, which now has the term, in every ensemble); before, with that term alone, at constant energy the CPU differed by 1.0e-7 nm, 2.1e-5 nm/ps, and 6.4e-2 kJ/mol/nm and the GPU by 8.0e-8, 5.1e-5, and 8.2e-2, and likewise at constant temperature and with the two kinds of work of Trotter type. The first step that differed was the first step of energy, in the forces of the atoms of the dipeptide, not of the waters of the term. The lowered programs of the nine systems of the Amber suite are the same byte for byte as before, in the default mode: without such a term the loops stand in the order of the sum already. *Amended 2026-10-09 (#243):* a fifth cause, on the CPU: the sine of a torsion. #243 found that in double precision on the CPU, under NPT with the scaling of Trotter type, a run depended on `energy_interval` at the last bits of a few velocities, with or without a term over centers. The energy of a torsion, $k\,[1 + \cos(n\phi - \delta)]$, has a cosine and its force a sine of one argument. LLVM's code generator computes a sine and a cosine of one argument in one basic block by one call of `sincos` of the C library, and a sine without such a cosine by `sin`, and the two do not round alike: in glibc 2.35, of $2\times10^7$ arguments drawn uniformly in $[-8, 8]$, 13,574 sines and 13,717 cosines of `sincos` differ from those of `sin` and `cos` in their last bit. The kernels of the dihedrals of the two kinds of step are the same arithmetic in MLIR, but the step that writes energies called `sincos` and the plain step `sin`, so the force of a torsion differed in its last bit where its argument was such a one. Nothing in that is of the barostat or of the constraints: in double precision any step of energy against any plain step has it, rarely (the runs at constant energy and at constant temperature did not meet it in 400 steps). In mixed precision the torsions are computed in f32, and `sincosf` of that glibc gave the bits of `sinf` and `cosf` for each of $10^8$ such arguments, so those runs were equal; the pass does not rest on that. In the deterministic mode the CPU pipeline now runs `separate-sin-cos` before `convert-math-to-llvm`: every `math.sin` and `math.cos` of f32 or f64 becomes a call of `sin`, `cos`, `sinf`, or `cosf`, declared without attributes, which the code generator leaves as calls (a declaration with `memory(none)` would make them the nodes that it merges again). The rounding of a sine then follows from its argument alone, as that of a sum with a product does on a device (`explicit-fma`). A device has no such call. The default mode keeps `sincos`: its pipeline does not have the pass (the pipelines of the nine systems of the Amber suite, for the CPU and a GPU, are those of before). *Evidence.* The first value that differed, found by writing the destination of every loop of the two step functions and comparing them in the order of the program, from the same state: the force of the loop over the dihedrals on the N of the last residue of the dipeptide at step 30, by $5.7\times10^{-14}$ kJ/mol/nm, one bit; the 1-4 pairs, the kick, and the velocity constraint of its bond to H carried it to the velocities of the two atoms (2.2e-16 nm/ps after 40 steps, the positions equal).
