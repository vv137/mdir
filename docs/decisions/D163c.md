# D163c

Recorded 2026-10-03.

**Anisotropic cell rescaling: `coupling = "ANISOTROPIC"` of `[barostat]`, each axis scaled by the strain of its own edge from its own pressure and noise, $\Delta\varepsilon_a = -\tfrac{f_a}{3}(P_0 - P_{aa}) + \sqrt{2k_BTcf_a/(3V)}\,R_a$, the step of z of eq. (9b) of [[Bernetti2020]](../references.md#bernetti2020) on each axis, with `compressibility` one number or three (x, y, z; 0 keeps an edge to the bit, all 0 is an error).** The zero flux of the equation of Fokker and Planck in the three strains holds for the density $V e^{-(P_0V+F)/k_BT}$, with no term in $k_BT/V$ (Section 6.4); the three steps sum to the isotropic step in $\ln V$. $R_a$ is normal number $a$ of stream 1 of the step (`mdrtBarostatStrainAxis`), so the first two are those of semi-isotropic coupling. The pressures, the work of a scaling (of Trotter type or exact), the scaling of rigid groups, of restraints, and of a triclinic cell are those of D119 and D127, which were already axis by axis; `FIRST_ORDER` is refused as for semi-isotropic coupling, and the log ends with the mean pressure of each axis. Shear (a flexible cell) needs the off-diagonal virial and the pressure tensor and is left to another decision. Validated against OpenMM 8.6.1's `MonteCarloAnisotropicBarostat` and the isotropic couplings of both programs (V-rescale in MDIR, Langevin at 1/ps in OpenMM, 4 fs, 1 bar or 200 atm): an argon fcc crystal of 864 atoms at 40 K and 1 atm (20,000 steps of equilibration, 80 more ps) keeps its cubic shape, edges 32.076, 32.112, 32.120 ± 0.015 Å against 32.100 isotropically and OpenMM's 32.112, 32.091, 32.119; volume 33,084 ± 5 Å³ (isotropic 33,077 ± 5; OpenMM 33,097 ± 3 and 33,089 ± 3, all a common 0.04% from the thermostat), spread 101.6 Å³ against OpenMM's 96.9; pressures of the axes 12.8 ± 3.8, 10.0 ± 3.9, −0.8 ± 4.0 bar against 1.0. The LJ mixture of `barostat.test` replicated to 2048 particles at 100 K and 200 atm has the volume 96,283 ± 71 Å³ against 96,352 ± 44 isotropically (OpenMM 96,394 ± 24) and the spread 407.5 Å³ against OpenMM's anisotropic 400.9; but a liquid has no stiffness of shape, its edges wander in both programs (MDIR 47.8, 42.1, 47.8 Å; OpenMM 51.4, 36.7, 51.4), the pressures of the axes follow the walk rather than converge, and OpenMM's anisotropic volume, 96,936 ± 42, is 0.6% above its own isotropic one, which MDIR's is not; at 256 particles OpenMM's run stopped when an edge fell below twice the cutoff. Anisotropic coupling is for solids and cells under unequal stresses. On a GPU in mixed precision as on the CPU (`barostat-aniso.test`, `barostat-aniso-gpu.test`).
