# D238

Recorded 2026-10-09.

**A writable borrow lends the tilts of a triclinic cell: `Borrow.tilt` is a buffer of the host with $b_x, c_x, c_y$ beside `Borrow.cell`, which stays the diagonal $a_x, b_y, c_z$ for every program, the two arrays of `mdir.Cell`; a commit checks the cell (finite, the diagonal at least twice the cutoff, reduced, the pairlist distance at most half of the least of the diagonal), refuses tilts that are all zero as a structural change, and begins the activation with the committed tilts, which are start values of the entry (D227) taken from the state of the host; the grid of PME and the neighbor capacity stay the program's, and no program is built.** Issue #206; amends D229, whose ruling refused a triclinic cell. Decided by the maintainer on PR #253 ([comment](https://github.com/vv137/mdir/pull/253#issuecomment-6075276065)): the shape as proposed; `Borrow.tilt` of a program compiled for an orthorhombic cell is three zeros with the read-only flag of DLPack (NumPy: an array that cannot be written), which neither `written` nor a commit takes as a write, and tilts that a consumer wrote in spite of the flag are refused at the commit as structural, where the proposal raised `UnsupportedError` on the attribute; without a periodic cell `cell` and `tilt` raise; the grid kept; no rebuild of the text; NPT in a triclinic cell from Python its own change (#254); the check of the pairlist distance follows #258. The audit of the builder found the tilts in three places: the arguments `%tilt_bx`, `%tilt_cx`, `%tilt_cy` of the entry, which `startActivation` now takes from the host's state and not from the values of the build; whether the cell is triclinic, which is the text (refused if it would change, in either direction); and the grid of PME when it is not given, structural since D227 and kept, as under a barostat and at a commit of the edges. The reciprocal vectors, the bins and lattice shifts of the neighbor structures, and the minimum image are computed by the program from its cell; the capacity estimate and everything that follows the volume $a_xb_yc_z$ do not read the tilts. The builder's test of the diagonal, not of the widths between the faces, is the right one: every nonzero vector of a lower-triangular lattice is at least $\min(a_x, b_y, c_z)$ long, and the reduced form, which a commit therefore checks and does not restore, makes the pass along $\mathbf c$, $\mathbf b$, $\mathbf a$ find the image. The cell of the host is set in one place, so the frames of an open reporter take a committed cell (a commit of the edges left them with the edges of before) and a simulation that continues from a checkpoint runs with its tilts (#257: it ran with the tilts of its build, and a Python stage after NPT of `mdir run` evaluated $+18855$ kJ/mol for $-12438$). A Python simulation still refuses a barostat in a triclinic cell. *Evidence* (`python-dlpack-tilts*.test`, `python-dlpack-tilts-frames*.test`; 403 waters in a rhombic dodecahedron, deterministic, CPU and an RTX 3090, double and mixed, PME and cutoff): commits of tilts changed by 0.01 nm, of the lattice in another reduced basis (2.6 nm in $c_x$), of tilts changed by 2.5 nm with a strain of 8%, and of the cell scaled as $H\,\mathrm{diag}(\boldsymbol\mu)$, each with 12 steps, and with a thermostat 40 steps, equal to the bit a simulation compiled from the committed state with the grid and the capacity of the first, whose text is the same; the other basis leaves the potential within $8.2\times10^{-12}$ kJ/mol with a cutoff in double precision (0.13 kJ/mol of $-9670$ with PME, the accuracy of its mesh); in a cell 1.54 nm wide between two faces, less than twice the cutoff, with a diagonal that passes, an observed pair term equals a NumPy sum over the images to $4.7\times10^{-16}$; seven refusals change nothing; 20 frames of a run of `mdir run` at constant pressure ($c_x$ from 1.3139 to 1.3813 nm) put into one simulation give the potential, the virial, the volume, and the forces of a simulation compiled from each frame, to the bit.
