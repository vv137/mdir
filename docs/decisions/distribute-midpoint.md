# D[distribute-midpoint]

Recorded at its merge.

The CPU midpoint traversal assigns a finite-range pair to the half-open
domain containing its canonical periodic midpoint. The owner can import
both endpoints. A bonded or correction tuple uses the center of the
axis-aligned bounds of its members after unwrapping about the member with
the lowest particle index. The original topology order determines the
arguments of its force law. The tuple schedule checks every member image
before evaluation.

Rigid waters and hydrogen groups use the arithmetic center of their
unwrapped coordinates for ownership. Migration assigns every member to
that domain. Overlapping group rows report the shared atom and both centers.
