# D292

Recorded 2026-10-19.

**Closure under transposition is a design goal of MDIR: every part is written as a composition of a small set of primitives (map; gather and scatter-accumulate; halo copy $G$ and halo sum $G^\mathsf T$; reduction and broadcast; scan and reverse scan; permutation and its inverse; compaction and expansion; a linear transform with a declared adjoint, the FFT first, whose adjoint is the unnormalized backward transform (the maintainer's ruling on #452, 2026-10-19); the structural sorts and builds, which have no transpose), each with its transpose in the set and its stated properties (associative, commutative, exact, stable, ordered), and [primitives-map.md](../primitives-map.md) maps every part to its primitives, the properties it relies on, its strategies with their measurements, and the state of its code.** Issue #450 (after #415 and #444); [principles.md](../principles.md), Section 9. *Findings recorded with it*: on `main` the shared pieces of the device lowering are `emitReductions`, for every global sum and maximum, and the preconditions of the privatized scatter (`Scatter.h`, D285), and the emitter of its table (`ScatterEmitter.h`, D291); every other primitive is written by hand at each site; the derivative's transposes are rules written in `md-differentiate` for the ops of `md` and declared through `AdjointOpInterface` only for the transfers of `md_dist` (D276); MDIR's `md.gather_*` ops are the pull form of a scatter-accumulate, and over the symmetric relations of pairs the transpose of a gather is again a gather, which the exchange contracts let a lowering turn into the push form (D89, D290); the FFT of PME and the derivative of a reciprocal sum (one op, its forces written in its templates) are missing in the IR until #452 writes the sum as spread, FFT, influence map, inverse FFT, and gather; the opaque model term (D270), the choice of skins (D281), and reweighting on the host do not fit the set, for the reasons the document gives; virtual sites spread their forces by a transpose of their placement written by hand, and the derivative into tables and tuple fields goes through seeds (D230) because no lowering had a scatter into bins. *The gaps* are issues, ordered by the number of parts they change, on #450. Documentation only: no code, key, or output changes.
