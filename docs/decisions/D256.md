# D256

Recorded 2026-10-14.

**An update of the tunables keeps the arrays of the program that no value of a tunable enters, found by a build from the kept model with every tunable site set to not-a-number and compared to the bit with the compiled program's, and shares them with copy on write; it makes the others by the functions that make them at a compile; the first update of each set of tunables that changed is compared with a build from a copy of the model of the compile, and a difference is never a silent fallback.** Issue #328, remedy 3 of PR #325's draft, after D254 (the maintainer's rulings on #325: a PR of its own; the coarse granularity, every array that some declared tunable enters made at every update). *Kept.* The arrays of the fields of the particles, the tables, the members and fields of the tuple sets, and the sites of the derivatives that a build from a model without the values of the tunables makes the same to the bit (`model::poisonTunables`, `driver::buildArrays`, `driver::keepArraysOf`); they are the compiled program's and take no memory of their own (`driver::SharedArray`). *Made.* The arrays that differ (the charges, the products of the 1-4 pairs, the tables of the Lennard-Jones and of the weights of their seeds, the row of the tunable constants, a parameter of a tuple term) by `fillCharges`, `fillTypeTable`, `fillTupleParameter`, `fillPairProducts`, which the compile uses too; a build that would leave an array without its numbers fails. *Checked.* What the not-a-number argument leaves is a value that enters an array only through a condition; the first update of each set of tunables that changed, and every update under `MDRT_CHECK_KEPT_TUPLES`, builds its program also from a copy of the model of the compile and compares member for member to the bit; a difference fails the update under the variable and is said once on the standard error without it, naming what differs, after which every update builds from a copy (review points of #325). What escapes the check: a later update of the same set that moves a value across such a condition; the stage of the arrays reads the values only as copies and products. *Measured* (one RTX 4090, mixed precision, `main` b12ee87, the head of #325 (D254), and this decision in turn six times in one `--idle` job, a process for each run; `update()` and `gradient()`, ms, the mean of the least of eight updates over the five rounds after the first; D254 → this decision): a constant of a pair term 5.35 → 4.60 on JAC, 15.8 → 13.1 on Factor IX, 87.3 → 65.3 on Cellulose (`main` 8.44, 30.9, 204); with its tail in the correction 8.05 → 7.28, 22.1 → 19.4, 112 → 89.7; per-type σ and ε 6.19 → 5.44, 19.0 → 16.2, 105 → 82.8; the charges 5.87 → 5.09, 18.0 → 15.6, 97.8 → 74.4; a parameter of a tuple term 9.35 → 5.30, 32.8 → 16.3, 171 → 75.1; all four 13.4 → 9.27, 43.9 → 27.0, 212 → 118. The rebuild inside an update of a pair constant 1.19 → 0.41, 3.21 → 0.67, 25.0 → 2.47 ms; of a tuple parameter 4.52 → 0.52, 17.1 → 0.99, 100 → 3.68 ms (the numbering of the tuples for the derivative is kept). What remains of an update is its two starts.
