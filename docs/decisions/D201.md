# D201

Recorded 2026-10-06.

**In the deterministic mode a step that writes energies moves the particles as a step that does not, where the compiler can guarantee it: device kernels contract no product into a fused multiply-add, and the virial of the constraints reads the velocity changes that the step applies.** #97 found that a run of `mdir run` on a GPU in mixed precision depended on `energy_interval`, and that a Python simulation agreed with `mdir run` only when their schedules of energy steps agreed. LLVM contracts a multiply-add only where the product has one use, and the energies and the virial of an energy step give the products of the force arithmetic further uses, so the forces of that step rounded otherwise (one f32 ulp on one particle of the dipeptide per step). The virial of SETTLE and SHAKE solved the velocity constraints a second time in a loop that `md-exec-fuse-loops` merged with the step's own solve; it now reads the changes, which over disjoint groups are a particle's group's. The default (nondeterministic) mode keeps contraction. *Evidence.* The dipeptide in water with cutoff electrostatics and no constraints, 20 steps on a GPU in mixed precision: the state with a row of energies at every step and with one at the end is equal bit for bit (it differed before, `test/Driver/energy-interval-gpu.test`); one step from the same state, plain or with energies, gives equal positions, velocities, and forces without constraints in mixed and double, and with constraints equal positions and forces. JAC (NVE, mixed, deterministic, one RTX 3090): 0.323 ms per step against 0.315 over the second half of 10,000 steps (+2.5%), 0.33 over the run in both. *Open (#102).* With constraints the velocities of an energy step still differ (the uses of the constraint changes by their virial change how loops fuse), and with PME the positions differ at the rounding of f64 from the second step. *Amended 2026-10-06 by D204:* the device kernels make a sum with a product as an operand a fused multiply-add by the formula alone (`explicit-fma`) rather than contract nothing; and with constraints and PME the steps of energy now move the particles as plain steps, by the two causes of #102 fixed there.
