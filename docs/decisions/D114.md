# D114

Recorded 2026-10-01.

**A structure of groups may keep a dual list: the loops over pairs take an inner list pruned from the outer one with a shorter reach, whenever its own test of validity asks.** A skin of 1 Å rebuilt Cellulose every 4 steps (405 µs a step for the builds, 1041 for the loop over pairs at 9 Å); a longer skin traded the one for the other (10 Å: 235 and 1318; 11 Å: 157 and 1643). With `pruned_distance` the refresh keeps both: the outer list built with $R$ = `pairlist_distance` as before, and an inner one with $R_\text{in}$ that the loops take. Validity is that of [neighbor-tiles.md](../neighbor-tiles.md), Section 6: the test of D80 against the build, and the same against the configuration and the cell of the last pruning with $R_\text{in}$, each scaled by its own cell; a second loop of `md-exec-expose-validity` computes the second (`stale` of `md_exec.refresh_neighbors`, `md_exec.reference_positions pruned`). The outer list is rebuilt and the inner one pruned from it where the first fails; the inner one alone is pruned again where the second fails. A pruning always starts from the outer list, `inner = outer & (r^2 <= R_in^2)`, so that a pair dropped once comes back; it sets the reference of the inner list only. The pruning: the positions moved into the frames of their groups in f32, the reach widened as the build widens it, so that rounding keeps a pair rather than drops one; a warp for each group takes the outer entries of the group in order and writes those with a bit left to the front of the blocks of the group, in order, across block boundaries (a ballot and a count of the bits below each lane), with the number each block holds; the loop skips the write of a block left empty. It keeps the order of the outer list and adds none of its own; the sums depend on the order of the threads as before. A first version pruned each outer block into itself: the loop over the inner list took 1320 µs, half-filled blocks, and a pruning 933 µs with positions in f64; now 1060 and 382 (Cellulose, 11 / 9 Å). Measured (ns/day, one list of 9 Å against the dual list): JAC 734 against 779 (11 / 8.6 Å), JAC at 4 fs 1352 against 1467 (11 / 9 Å), FactorIX 273 against 286 (11 / 8.6), Cellulose 59.7 against 62.4 (12 / 8.6), STMV 37.8 against 39.9 (11 / 9). `groups-gpu.test` runs the dual list against the matrix to the digits of the log in double precision (100 steps, 37 prunings), and against one list under the barostat of `barostat.test` (1000 steps of compression). Only a device and groups take it; the CPU refuses it.
