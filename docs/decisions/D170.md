# D170

Recorded 2026-10-03.

**Barostat outputs report the area of the xy face spanned by the first two cell vectors beside the volume.** `AREA_XY` in the log and `area_xy` in `[output].energy` are in Å², at `energy_interval`, for every barostat coupling. In the reduced triangular cell this is $A = L_x L_y$, including a nonzero tilt of the second vector. There is no new control key; backups, continuation, and part files follow the existing output rules. This reports cell area, not area per lipid; velocity frames and the full pressure tensor of F4 remain separate work.
