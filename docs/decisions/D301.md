# D301

Recorded 2026-10-20.

**The algebra of every sum is a fact of the IR, a licence to the lowering about the order of evaluation, and the chooser and the lowerings read it instead of the mode.** #422 step 1 (#415, #444; [device-primitives.md](../device-primitives.md), Section 5; [primitives-map.md](../primitives-map.md), gap 1; white paper, Section 8). `md_exec.algebra` (`#md_exec.algebra<...>`, a discardable attribute) gives one value to each value that an op reduces: the `reduce` of `md_exec.particle_for`; the destinations and then the `reduce` of `pair_for` and `tuple_for`; the destination of `scatter_add`; the spreading, the energy, and the virial of `reciprocal`; the sum of `md_dist.all_reduce` and `md_dist.halo_sum` (`algebra::getReducedTypes`, `include/mdir/Dialect/MDExec/Algebra.h`). *Ruled* (the maintainer on PR #470, 2026-10-19), the values and their definitions: **`any_order_exact`**, the lowering may evaluate in any order and grouping, and every order gives the same bits (integers, `or`, fixed point); **`any_order`**, the lowering may evaluate in any order and grouping, and for floating-point values the result's rounding then depends on that order (the floating-point sums of the default mode); **`fixed_order`**, the lowering must use the order the IR states, or an exact form of the sum, so that the result is the same on every run of the same build, for `md_dist` for a given decomposition (#409). `fixed_order` may be met through an `any_order_exact` form (the fixed-point spreading of PME in the deterministic mode). They are licences, not algebraic claims: floating-point addition is not associative. *Guards* (ruled): the licence is about order only, and `any_order` does not permit another precision of the accumulator or another growth of its rounding error (a tree replaced by a serial chain of atomics), which is a separate decision with its own measurement; only `md-exec-assign-algebra` states an algebra (from the mode and the type, the sums across domains `fixed_order` in every mode) and every other pass carries it; with `deterministic` the module carries `md_exec.deterministic`, whose verifier refuses `any_order` on a floating-point value; step 1 changes no number. *Read*: `checkReorder`, `checkTile`, `checkHash` (`Scatter.h`) take the algebras in place of the mode, for `md-exec-choose-scatter` (which lost its option `deterministic`) and the lowering alike; `convert-md-exec-to-gpu` evaluates the tuples once only where every value of the loop allows any order, copies their members by the same fact, refuses a two-ended loop over pairs, a loop over groups, and `scatter_add` where they are `fixed_order`, and spreads PME in fixed point where the spreading is `fixed_order`; `convert-md-exec-to-loops` adds a `fixed_order` `scatter_add` on one thread; the global sums stay block trees whose order depends on the count alone (D150), which meet every algebra. The lowerings and `convert-md-dist-to-loops` refuse a sum without an algebra, and `--md-exec-check-algebra` refuses one anywhere (as `--md-check-transposes`, D295). The mode still decides what is not a sum (#102's displacements, `explicit-fma`, `contract`). *Emitters*: the warp butterfly of `shuffle xor` and the block reduction of D150 are one emitter each, `ReductionEmitter.h` (CUB's `WarpReduceShfl` and `BLOCK_REDUCE_WARP_REDUCTIONS` [[CUB]](../references.md#cub)), called by the global sums, the lanes of a row, the warp totals of the loops over groups, and the counts of the active units; the half-warp sum of `emitGroupPairKernel` keeps its own 64-bit shuffle. *Outside the pipelines* the algebra decides otherwise than the mode did in three cases, none reached by a program of the driver: a loop over shared tuples whose only reduced values are flags evaluates them once in the deterministic mode too; a deterministic module with no `fixed_order` loop over tuples copies their members; a `fixed_order` `scatter_add` is refused on a device and runs on one thread on the host. *Why a discardable attribute*: an argument would change the generated builders at about 140 places, and a lost attribute is refused by the lowerings, never defaulted. **Identity**: `mdir emit --stage=lowered` with `MDIR_GPU_BINARY=ptx` gives the bytes of `main` (0a336ff9) on 32 inputs, both modes: the nine Amber suite systems with the pruning fused and in the refresh, JAC, JAC NPT, Factor IX, and Cellulose in the deterministic mode, JAC on the CPU in both modes, JAC with the matrix, the tables, and the tiles, the prolate kernel, and the listed particles of PME, 165 to 501 kernels a program on a device. **Measured** (RTX 4090, `main` and the branch alternated in one job, three rounds, the Amber suite): the ratio of the step to `main`'s 0.999 to 1.005, within the spread of the runs.
