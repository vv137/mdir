# D116

Recorded 2026-10-02.

**The count of the work of a scaling of Trotter type takes the virial of the groups from the forces of the evaluations, $\mathcal W = \mathrm{tr}\,\mathsf W_\text{eval} - \sum_g\sum_{j\in g}(\mathbf x_j - \mathbf X_g)\cdot\mathbf F_j$, not the virial of the step with twice the internal kinetic energy.** The count is $(\mu^{-2} - 1)K_{1/2} - \ln\mu\,\tfrac12(\mathcal W_b + \mathcal W_a)$ (D92), with $\mathcal W$ the trace of the virial of the groups that scale with their centers, $\sum_g \mathbf X_g\cdot\mathbf F_g = -dU/d\ln\mu$. It took $\mathcal W = \mathrm{tr}\,\mathsf W + 2K_\text{int}$, the virial of the step with that of the constraints, which equals it when the forces of the constraints are those of one configuration. In the step that scales they are not: the constraints of the positions take the forces of the start of the step, before the scaling, and those of the velocities the forces after it, and $K_\text{int}$ is that of the end. The error of $\mathcal W_a$ is of first order in $\ln\mu$, so the trapezoid erred by a second-order term of one sign: on 1039 rigid OPC waters (`time_constant = 2`, a period of 25 steps, GPU, mixed) the conserved energy drifted by $-234$ kcal/mol/ns, independent of the period, where the exact count drifted by $+10$ and GROMACS by $-0.4$; with flexible water the counts agreed. The virial of the groups needs no forces of the constraints, which are internal and drop out: `step_virial` and `step_trotter` return $\mathrm{tr}\,\mathsf W_\text{eval} - \sum (\mathbf x_j - \mathbf X)\cdot\mathbf F_j$ at their new positions, from the forces after the sites are spread (`emitMolecularTrace`; a linear site leaves the atomic virial as it is), and the start of a run and a restart without the stored state compute it as well. It is exact for a group of SHAKE whose angles bend, which the identity with $K_\text{int}$ was not. The pressure that gives $\mu$ is unchanged, and so is the trajectory. **Measured** (1 ns each, slope ± the error of ten blocks): the water box at periods of 5, 25, and 100 steps, $+16.2 \pm 11$, $+12.7 \pm 13$, $+12.0 \pm 12$ kcal/mol/ns, the exact count $+3.7 \pm 3.5$; ubiquitin in 5700 OPC waters with SHAKE on the bonds of hydrogen over 0.6 ns, $+11.4 \pm 33$, from $-228$. With `time_constant = 0.1`, where the strains are far larger, the conserved energy of the water box changed over 20 ps by 0.018 to 0.035 of its value, from 0.30 to 0.32 (the exact count 0.006 to 0.007). The rate is unchanged (ubiquitin at constant pressure 551.7 ns/day; the suite within 2%). `barostat-rigid-gpu.test` (deterministic, the dipeptide in OPC, `time_constant = 0.5`, 50 ps): $9.5\times10^{-4}$ of the value, from $1.3\times10^{-2}$. The first-order count, which scales after the step, keeps $\mathrm{tr}\,\mathsf W + 2K_\text{int}$.
