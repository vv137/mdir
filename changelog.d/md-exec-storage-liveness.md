### Added

- **The liveness of the storage of `md_exec` within the loop over steps** (D[md-exec-storage-liveness], #880). An analysis, `StorageLiveness`, maps each device buffer that a host function allocates before its loop over steps to the points of the host's code where it is live. It is a backward dataflow with kills, through views, the loop's carried buffers, branches, the waits of asynchronous ops, and the joins of side streams. `mdir-opt --test-md-exec-print-storage-liveness` prints it, with the interference of the buffers. A kill is a fill or a copy (`kills=proven`), or also a launch that writes the whole buffer and reads none of it (`kills=stores`, assumed). Nothing enters a pipeline, and the lowered programs are unchanged. On four programs, every buffer that the loop touches is live through the step, except the reference positions of the lists, so reuse by lifetime saves only the buffers that the loop never touches: 2.64% of STMV's peak in use, 8.0% of JAC's. The decision records the census, by buffer, and adds no transformation.
