### Changed

- **The thresholds and launch parameters of the device lowering are tables of tuning policies, one for each strategy, keyed by the architecture and a device class** (D[tuning-policies], #433, step 1). The pruning in the loop over pairs, the scatter-accumulates, and the launches of the loops read their entries for the architecture that the kernels are compiled for. Each entry cites its measurement, and an architecture without an entry takes the default, which the explain remarks of `md-exec-choose-scatter` state. No value changes: every `sm_12x` target, the RTX 5090 included, still prunes in the loop from 20,480 groups, and every other target from 4096. The lowered programs are those of before on `sm_89` and `sm_120`, and the environment overrides are unchanged; the pipeline gains the option `arch=`, so the compile cache misses once for each GPU program after the upgrade. See [docs/device-primitives.md](docs/device-primitives.md#8-tuning-policies-dtuning-policies).
