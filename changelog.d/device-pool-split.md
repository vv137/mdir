### Changed

- **The runtime's pool of device memory serves a request from a larger freed block** (D[device-pool-split], #890). It is a best-fit allocator with splitting and deferred coalescing [Wilson et al. 1995] over the allocations that it takes from the driver. A request takes first the idle piece that last held a block of its size, so the step's recurring requests keep their pieces; otherwise the smallest idle piece that holds it, cut at its size rounded up to 256 bytes. Idle pieces merge with their idle neighbors in their own allocation when a request finds no piece that holds it, at a trim, at the close of an activation, and past the budget. STMV's peak with the pool falls by 5.2% (78.6 MB) and JAC's by 6.9%, at `main`'s rate at STMV. Only a wholly idle allocation goes back to the driver: through the budget `MDIR_DEVICE_POOL_BYTES`, a trim (`mdir.trim_device_memory()`), or a growth of the runtime's neighbor structures that replaces a buffer that is an allocation of its own (D312). An allocation or a free of a block of the pool on the second stream of `mdrtSideBegin`, or naming a stream other than the runtime's first, stops the run with a message. The summary of `MDIR_DEVICE_MEMORY` counts the requests split from a larger piece, and its breakdown at level 2 gives the padding of the pieces in use.
