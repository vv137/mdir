### Changed

- **The runtime's pool of device memory serves a request from a larger freed block** (D[device-pool-split], #890). It is a best-fit allocator with splitting and immediate coalescing [Wilson et al. 1995] over the allocations that it takes from the driver. A request takes the smallest idle piece that holds it, cut at its size rounded up to 256 bytes, and a freed piece merges with its idle neighbors in its own allocation. Only a wholly idle allocation goes back to the driver, through the budget `MDIR_DEVICE_POOL_BYTES` or a trim (`mdir.trim_device_memory()`); a buffer of the runtime's neighbor structures that a growth may replace is an allocation of its own, so the growth still gives it back (D312). The summary of `MDIR_DEVICE_MEMORY` counts the requests split from a larger piece, and its breakdown at level 2 gives the padding of the pieces in use.
