### Added

- **Virtual sites in the products of force matching** (D384, #771 part 2): `FrameEnergies.vjp` gives the product in the tunables (D381) for programs with virtual sites, linear ones (`[ virtual_sites3 ]`, function 1, of GROMACS, as in its OPC and TIP4P) and the extra points of Amber (OPC and TIP4P-Ew from a prmtop), by the tangent of their placement; and the derivative in the positions (D383) for linear sites, spreading the forces to the atoms. For the extra points of Amber the derivative in the positions is refused by name. The two definitions spread the force on a site differently, so force matching should use the one of the source of the reference forces ([docs/python-frames.md](docs/python-frames.md)).
