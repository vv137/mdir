### Fixed

- **`md_alg.sort_by_key` and `md_alg.bin_members` follow views in their remaining checks of distinct buffers** (#874). Three checks of `sort_by_key`'s verifier compared the values alone, so a view (`memref.cast`, `subview`, `memory_space_cast`, or `reinterpret_cast`) got past each: two own buffers that view one buffer, a `by` that views the order, and a use in the key region of a view of an own buffer made outside the region. The region check, shared with `bin_members`, also let that op's region use a view of `items`, `start`, or `by`, or read a view of a side output, made outside it. All of them now compare the buffers that the operands view, through the helper of #871; the messages are unchanged. No program builds such an op; the lowered programs, with the PTX of every kernel, are unchanged.
