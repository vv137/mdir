### Added

- **`[execution] term_streams` runs the force terms of a step on up to three device streams** (D449, #935). `SINGLE`, the default, keeps one stream. `RECIPROCAL` runs the reciprocal sums of PME on a second stream (D87, D429), which before needed a replaced pass pipeline. `TERMS` also runs the loops over tuples (bonded terms, 1–4 pairs, exclusion corrections) on a third stream, with a force destination of their own that the kick adds to that of the pairs; this changes the rounding of the sum of the forces, and in the deterministic mode `TERMS` resolves to `RECIPROCAL`. The side streams are forked and joined by events, so a captured step graph takes them in as parallel branches. The log and the manifest record the resolved value and why; `mdir pin` writes it, and Python takes `Execution.term_streams`.
