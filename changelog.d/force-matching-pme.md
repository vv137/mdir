### Added

- **The tangent of a reciprocal sum in the positions, and its derivative in a number that the charges depend on** (D379, #726): `tangent` and `tangent_derivative(n)` of `md.evaluate` take potentials with particle mesh Ewald. The derivative is exact from two reciprocal sums with their forces for each number. The derivative in the charge of each particle is refused ([docs/ops.md](docs/ops.md), Section 5.3.2).
