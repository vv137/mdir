### Added

- **The pairs of rigid kinds in segments, in the `md` dialect** (#859, D405). `md.kind_pairs` divides the pairs of the rigid kinds of D403 that a consumer takes and of the rest into the segments $s(f, g) = f(f+1)/2 + g$; `md.sum_segment_window` and `md.gather_segment_window` name their segment and prove each site from the weights of its kind (`#md.rigid_kind`). A generator in the driver writes the windows of every segment from the kinds, its site pairs those with a nonzero charge product or Lennard-Jones on both sites, for at most two kinds; for one kind they are the windows of #795. No run reads these ops yet, and no control key changes ([docs/ops.md](docs/ops.md), Section 4.12).
