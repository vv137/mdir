### Changed

- **A loop over pairs over groups is a target-neutral `md_alg` op, `md_alg.traverse_pairs`** (D[md-alg-traverse], #886, step 2 of #877). The new pass `convert-md-exec-to-md-alg` makes it from each loop over pairs over a structure of groups, before `convert-md-exec-to-gpu`. The op declares its visit, the combination of its destinations, and the algebra of each value. Its verifier refuses a write in its region, a use of a destination there, and a declaration that the contract of its list (D413) does not meet. Its serial definition is the oracle. Its SIMT lowering is the schedule of the groups, which `convert-md-exec-to-gpu` calls where the op stands (#888 frees it). The lowered program of every groups case of the corpus and the Amber suite is byte-identical to the base, in both precisions. The options `fuse-pruning` and `fuse-pruning-groups` move to the new pass. `MDIR_TRAVERSE_ORACLE` runs the serial definition on the host in place of the schedule, for the contract test (docs/environment.md).
