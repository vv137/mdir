### Changed

- **The text that `mdir` prints is ASCII only** (D396, #812). The summary of `mdir check`, the log of `mdir run`, and the warnings and errors they report spell the angstrom `Ang` (`cutoff: 9 Ang`, `PME beta: 0.35 Ang^-1`), the density `g/cm^3`, the error of a mean `+/-`, microseconds `us`, and Greek letters by name; the table is in [docs/conventions.md](docs/conventions.md#units-and-symbols-in-printed-text). A script that matches the old text of `mdir check` or of a log must match the new. The reasons for the automatic choices of the neighbor structure and of the skins change in the manifest, the JSON summaries, the cache of the skins, and the settings that Python resolves as well; an older entry of the cache is still read. The line of units of the files of columns and the templates of control files are unchanged. `scripts/check-before-push.sh` refuses a non-ASCII character in a string literal of `tools/mdir/*.cpp`.
