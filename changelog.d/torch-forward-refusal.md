### Fixed

- **Forward-mode AD through `mdir.torch` is refused by name, not a silent zero** (D[torch-forward-refusal], #777): a tangent of `torch.func.jvp`, `jacfwd`, or `torch.autograd.forward_ad` on `theta`, the positions, the cells, the tilts, or a cotangent of a backward raises `UnsupportedError` ("forward mode ... is not implemented; reverse mode (torch.autograd.grad / backward) is"), where it gave a tangent of 0 or `None`; `FrameEvaluator.evaluate` refuses positions and cells that carry such a tangent as it refuses those that require a gradient. The transforms of `torch.func` are not supported through `mdir.torch` (PyTorch's own `RuntimeError`); `torch.autograd` is. Reverse mode is unchanged, to the bit ([docs/python-frames.md](docs/python-frames.md)).
