### Changed

- **The groups of neighbors are built from columns of 96 particles a cube of their width, instead of 64, as an entry of the tuning policies** (D[groups-columns], #912). On an RTX 4090 the step takes 2.1 to 3.3% less time on the four systems of the Amber suite and 0.8% less on a water box of 310,000 sites. The lists hold 2 to 3% fewer entries, whose atomic reductions bound the loop over pairs, and the build is faster. The forces change by the order of their sums only. The devices of compute capability 12 (RTX 5090, RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell) keep 64 until measured. The option `groups-column-particles` of `convert-md-exec-to-gpu` sets another count. See [docs/neighbor-groups.md](docs/neighbor-groups.md#2-the-compact-order).
