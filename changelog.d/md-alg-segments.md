### Added

- **The design of segmented lists of units and of `md_alg.traverse_pairs` over segments**, the first slice of re-landing the rigid-water template of #795 on the `md_alg` layer (D[md-alg-segments], #892). The design states the type `!mdrt.segmented_pairs`, with the rigid kinds of D403 and one D413 contract for each segment of D405, and the op's segmented form, with a table of site pairs, sites formed by the kinds' weights, and the transpose of rule A. It also states the verifier rules with their negative tests, the serial definition and the SIMT schedule, the map from #795's branch, the slices S1 to S5 with their gates (S4 cut into S4a and S4b; S5 makes the groups the one-segment instance of one schedule, with identical PTX as its gate), and the open questions. The groups keep their form and their bytes. Execution is unchanged.
- **Lists of units in the segments of rigid kinds, in the IR, with their serial definition** (D[md-alg-segments], slice S1, #892). The type `!mdrt.segmented_pairs` holds the taken kinds and a contract of D413 for each segment. `convert-md-to-md-exec` makes `md_exec.segment_for` from the segment windows of D405, the passes of md_exec fuse, merge, type, and store it, and `convert-md-exec-to-md-alg` makes the segmented form of `md_alg.traverse_pairs`, whose verifier checks the rules V1 to V13. Its serial definition forms the sites by the kinds' weights, tests the cutoff of each site pair, and gives the forces to the members by W^T; on the system of four kinds of D405 it is within the bound of D405's f64 oracle in every component. No run reads it yet, and `convert-md-exec-to-gpu` refuses it until slices S2 and S3. The lowered program of every case of the corpus is unchanged.
