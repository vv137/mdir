### Changed

- On the GPU the coupling step runs on the device (`[execution] coupling_step = "DEVICE"`, the default; `"HOST"` keeps the path of before). The factor of V-rescale, the heat of the bath and the pressures of C-rescale are computed from the sums of the step by one thread, in the host's order of operations; the host draws the random numbers, which depend only on the seed and the step. A V-rescale step that writes no output copies nothing to the host; a C-rescale step copies its pressures (up to 64 bytes) instead of the sums (two copies of 8 kB, the virial that TOWERS observes and the reciprocal virial of PME), because the next step's launches take the new cell by value. The trajectory is unchanged: bitwise in the deterministic mode. The host's results buffer is page-locked. `MDRT_HOST_READS=1` counts the copies to the host and the waits (D[device-coupling], #1026).
