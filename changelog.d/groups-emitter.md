### Changed

- **The device build of the groups of neighbors is written by a C++ emitter** (#856, D404). `lib/Conversion/MDExecKernels/GroupsBuild.cpp` takes the cell (orthorhombic or triclinic) and the scan of the counting sorts as compile-time parameters, in place of `scripts/generate-groups-gpu-template.py` and its four generated templates (about 59,000 lines), which are removed. The generated code is the same: the PTX of the Amber suite and of a triclinic case, in mixed and double precision and with both scans, is identical to that of before. For tests, `mdir-opt --add-groups-build` adds the build to a module.
