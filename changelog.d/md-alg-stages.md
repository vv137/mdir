### Changed

- **Internal**: three stages of the neighbor builds are now `md_alg` ops: a counting sort whose key a region computes, with declared side outputs and an order that is a contract of its own (`md_alg.sort_by_key`); the append of entries to lists held in blocks from a pool, whose counter keeps the full demand past a capacity (`md_alg.append`); and the plan of a grid (`md_alg.grid_plan`). The groups build, the spatial order, the device build of ragged rows, and the matrix's count of cells use them. The kernels are unchanged, except that the images kernel of the groups build masks the place of an entry before its conditional store rather than inside it: same registers and the same number of SASS instructions (D407, #864).
