### Added

- **`examples/kcl-osmotic`: a K–Cl NBFIX fitted to the osmotic pressure of KCl(aq) by reweighting** (#140). The four stages of a run (minimization, constant volume, constant pressure with restraints, production) for KCl in TIP3P between walls on the ions, and a fit of the K–Cl σ' of the Joung–Cheatham ions to the osmotic pressure $-RT\ln a_w/\bar V_w$ of Hamer and Wu at about 2 m, with 1 m held out. The correction is linear in $C_{12}$ and $C_6$, so two columns of `observe` per row reweight every run exactly, and the effective sample size limits each step. In the study one update moves σ' by +0.006 ± 0.003 Å, and both concentrations then agree with experiment within 1 SE (about 1.1 bar). `test/Driver/kcl-osmotic-reweight.test` checks the reweighting against the energies of other states and against single points.
