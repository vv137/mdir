### Added

- **Forward mode in the tunables of the gradient in the positions: `FrameEnergies.jvp(tangent)`** (D389, #726). For a tangent of the tunables, a mapping of their names to arrays (a name left out is zero), it returns a `FrameTangent` with the tangent of the energies, $\sum_m\partial U_n/\partial\theta_m\,\dot\theta_m$, and of their gradients in the positions, $\sum_m\partial^2U_n/\partial\mathbf x\,\partial\theta_m\,\dot\theta_m$, `(K, N, 3)`, from one pass of a third program of frames; with `gradients=()` the energy alone, without that pass. It is the transpose of `vjp(positions=)` and covers pairs, tuples, terms of the positions, virtual sites, and the charges with PME (per particle or tied), by two sums of the mesh for all entries.
- **The requests `direction` and `direction_forces` of `md.evaluate`**, with the attribute `directions`: `md-differentiate` builds the derivative of a potential along directions of its arguments, $\Psi = \sum_s\dot b_s\,\partial U/\partial b_s$, from the sums of $U$, with its forces; `md-check-derivatives` checks both by central differences along the directions.
